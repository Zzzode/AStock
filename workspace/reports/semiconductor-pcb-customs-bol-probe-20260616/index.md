# Customs / Bill-of-Lading Probe: Semiconductor PCB

**Collection date:** 2026-06-16

**Purpose:** Test whether public customs / bill-of-lading pages can close the named platform/customer revenue-split gap for AI PCB / CCL suppliers.

## Archived Pages

| # | Source | Target | Local file | Public evidence recovered | Boundary |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ImportGenius | Wus Printed Circuit Co., Ltd. | `importgenius-wus.html` | Public profile shows WUS shipment profile and sample shipment context; visible partners include Navico / Continental-related automotive and electronics counterparties. | Does not show NVIDIA / Google / AWS / Microsoft AI-platform shipments or revenue split. |
| 2 | ImportGenius | Victory Giant Technology (Huizhou) | `importgenius-victory-giant.html` | Public profile shows 25 total shipments and top partner Amphenol Manufacturing Bonded Wareh; sample product text includes printed circuit board. | Does not show NVIDIA / Google / AWS / Microsoft AI-platform shipments or revenue split. |

| 4 | Panjiva | Nvidia Corporation buyer profile | `panjiva-nvidia.html` | Public profile shows Nvidia buyer page, country/region origin counts and a paywalled supplier context. | Does not disclose attributable PCB / CCL suppliers or transaction values in the public page. |

## Conclusion

Public customs / bill-of-lading pages do not close the named customer/platform revenue split. They may show trade relationships for specific entities, but the accessible public pages either:

- are paywalled,
- show unrelated or non-AI counterparties,
- are blocked by anti-bot pages, or
- do not disclose product-platform revenue, ASP, quantity, shipment value or customer-level sales.

Use this only as source-exhaustion evidence. Do not use public BOL snippets to infer NVIDIA / Google TPU / AWS Trainium / Microsoft Azure revenue for covered A-share issuers.
