## Consensus Quick View

- **Core investment thesis:** AI server architecture upgrades are driving PCB from cyclical commodity board demand toward high-layer, high-speed, high-density, substrate-like interconnect demand. The most attractive segments are high-end AI PCB, high-speed CCL, IC substrate / ABF / BT, and upstream equipment and consumables.
- **Main demand drivers:** NVIDIA GB300 / Vera Rubin / Rubin Ultra, ASIC servers from North American CSPs, 800G / 1.6T / 3.2T networking and optical modules, CoWoP-like substrate integration, orthogonal backplanes, higher rack density and power density.
- **Value-change mechanism:** both volume and ASP rise. AI servers require more layers, lower-loss materials, tighter line width/spacing, better signal integrity, higher drilling/electroplating precision, and stronger thermal/power handling.
- **Supply-side feature:** high-end capacity remains tight because customer certification, equipment bottlenecks, CCL material qualification, process yield, and long capex cycles slow supply response.

## Key Investment Opportunities

1. **AI PCB manufacturing leaders**
   - High-layer, high-speed, HDI and backplane demand has the clearest revenue link to AI server architecture.
   - Main names: 沪电股份, 胜宏科技, 深南电路, 鹏鼎控股, 景旺电子, 生益电子.

2. **High-speed CCL and laminate price / mix upgrade**
   - M7/M8/M9/M10 migration makes CCL a performance bottleneck rather than a commodity input.
   - Main names: 生益科技, 华正新材, 南亚新材, 建滔积层板.

3. **IC substrate / ABF / BT / glass substrate**
   - Advanced packaging and CoWoP blur the boundary between PCB and packaging substrate.
   - Main names: 深南电路, 兴森科技, 华正新材, 珠海越亚; watch glass substrate ecosystem.

4. **PCB equipment and consumables**
   - Downstream capex and technical inflation benefit drilling, LDI, electroplating, lamination, drilling needles and precision printing.
   - Main names: 大族数控, 芯碁微装, 东威科技, 凯格精机, 鼎泰高科, 中钨高新.

5. **Domestic compute supply-chain substitution**
   - Domestic AI chips and servers create a parallel demand path for CCL, PCB and substrate qualification.
   - Main names: 深南电路, 华正新材, 生益科技, 兴森科技, selected domestic server/compute-chain suppliers.

## Divergence / Risk Flags

- **Demand extrapolation risk:** many reports assume sustained AI server capex and Rubin/ASIC ramps. Any cloud capex pause would hit sentiment and orders.
- **Technology route risk:** copper cable, optical interconnect, CoWoP, glass substrate, mSAP and midplane architectures may shift value among PCB, substrate, optical and packaging suppliers.
- **Supply response risk:** aggressive capex by leaders could create future oversupply if demand ramp is slower than expected.
- **Customer concentration:** AI PCB names may depend heavily on NVIDIA chain or a few North American CSPs.
- **Material price squeeze:** CCL and upstream glass/copper/resin tightness benefits material leaders but can pressure PCB manufacturers if price pass-through lags.
- **Valuation risk:** many core names already price in high growth and scarcity. Report tone is broadly bullish; contrarian/downside materials are underrepresented.
