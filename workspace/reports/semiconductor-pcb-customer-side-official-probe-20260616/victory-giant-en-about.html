Introduction

Victory Giant Technology (Huizhou) Co., Ltd. (“Victory Giant Technology”) was founded in July 2006 at the Xingcheng Science and Technology Park in Xinqiao Village, Danshui Subdistrict, Huiyang District. The plant covers an area of 543,000 square meters. Currently the company has five major R&D and production bases in Guangdong, Hunan, Malaysia, Thailand, and Vietnam (under construction), with more than 10,000 employees (approximately 16,000 globally). In June 2015, the company was listed on the SZSE ChiNext board, stock code: 300476. In April 2026, the company was listed on the HKEX main board. Victory Giant Technology researches, develops, produces, and sells high-precision multilayer, HDI PCB, FPC, and rigid-flex PCBs. Their products find widespread applications in various fields, including artificial intelligence, big data centers, industrial internet, automotive electronics (new energy), next-generation communication technology, new energy, aerospace, medical instruments and other fields. As a Honorary Vice President of the Chinese Printed Circuit Association (CPCA), Victory Giant Technology plays a crucial role in setting industry standards. Globally, Victory Giant Technology ranks 6th among PCB companies (according to Prismark), and domestically, it holds the 3th position among Chinese-printed circuit industry companies,  establishing close cooperative relationships with many global top AI clients, and has quickly grown into a global leader in AI computing power PCBs.

Victory Giant Technology operates a Provincial Engineering Technology Research and Development Center. They hold 380 valid patents in the field of circuit boards and have been honored with the "China Patent Award" for four consecutive years. Furthermore, the company has obtained certifications including UL, ISO9001, ISO14001, ISO45001, IATF1694, CQC, QC080000, China ROHS, intellectual property management system, information security management system, and energy management system. It has also received accolades such as "National Torch Program Key High-Tech Enterprise," "National Green Factory," "National IP  Excellence Enterprise", "Guangdong Innovative Enterprise," "Guangdong Province Manufacturing Industry Single Champion " and "Outstanding National Brand Enterprise”.


By implementing the 'Three Major Strategies' of smart factories, green manufacturing, and high-tech/high-quality services, and promoting the 'Four Innovations' in concepts, technology, talent, and capital, our company has successfully established the first next-generation industrial internet smart factory in China within our industry. Leveraging strategic advantages, technological superiority, quality excellence, production capacity scale, premium clientele, and cultural cohesion, we have gained a competitive edge in AI computing power, AI robotics, autonomous driving and virtual reality. In 2025, revenue is expected to reach 19.292 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 79.77%, with net profit attributable to the parent company of 4.312 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 273.52%. The target is to achieve an output value exceeding 100 billion by 2030.
