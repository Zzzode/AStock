## Evidence value

- Adds official parent-company evidence that Server/Optical and IC Substrate revenue contribution reached 19.7% in 2026Q1.
- Adds 2026 outlook for Server/Optical revenue more than doubling and IC Substrate revenue +70%.
- Adds technology language for GPU/ASIC iHDI/HLC products, 1.6T optical boards and 2027 high-end optical PCB capacity reservation.
- Adds 2025 annual-report evidence: revenue NT$182.522bn, parent-attributable net profit NT$6.791bn, EPS NT$6.91, AI cloud/network/terminal application-related revenue nearly 70%, Customer X sales NT$136.068bn / 74.55%, and Avary Holding contribution NT$11.698bn mainly from revenue growth.
- Adds 2025 operating cash-flow context: operating cash inflow NT$27.617bn, down 9.11% YoY due to receivables/payables not due and higher inventory.
- Adds Chinese-original 2025 annual report and full-year audited financial statements. The Chinese annual report is the governing text and confirms the same revenue, profit, EPS and AI cloud/network/terminal application mix language.

## Boundary

Customer X remains anonymized. The official annual report strengthens parent-company customer concentration, application mix and working-capital evidence, but still does not disclose Apple / NVIDIA / Google / platform revenue split, order value, ASP or shipment data.
