## Report Type Summary

### 1. Single-stock / asset-focused equity research

**Representative sample:** J.P. Morgan Hulu / 21st Century Fox note.

**Typical structure:**
- Front-page rating, ticker, price, target price, analyst block.
- One-page thesis and key bullet points.
- Company/asset-specific operating model and valuation.
- Peer comparison and price performance.
- Detailed disclosure appendix.

**Use for AStock reports:**
- Build a sharper first page: current price, target framework, evidence quality, thesis, downside risks.
- Keep rating/target prices clearly separated from our own non-advisory research priority.

### 2. Market guide / chartbook

**Representative sample:** J.P. Morgan Guide to the Markets.

**Typical structure:**
- Heavy exhibit density.
- Each page answers one market question with one or more charts.
- Little prose; charts carry the story.
- Macro, asset allocation, valuations, earnings, rates, FX, sector, and regional charts.

**Use for AStock reports:**
- Convert dense tables into exhibits: heatmaps, timelines, scatter plots, valuation bridges.
- Give each chart a single message and a source note.

### 3. Global outlook in charts

**Representative sample:** BlackRock BII Global Outlook in Charts.

**Typical structure:**
- Mega-force framing, then chart-led evidence.
- Strong use of visual hierarchy and short captions.
- Focus on scenario uncertainty and structural forces.

**Use for AStock reports:**
- For themes such as AI PCB, start with the structural force and show the transmission chain visually.
- Add scenario bands rather than single-point forecasts.

### 4. Annual outlook / long-term investment perspective

**Representative sample:** Capital Group 2026 Outlook.

**Typical structure:**
- Portfolio manager narrative.
- Thematic long-term view.
- Balanced discussion of opportunities and risks.
- More readable prose, less model-heavy.

**Use for AStock reports:**
- Improve narrative quality and avoid sounding like a broker-summary list.
- Use a clear house view: what we believe, why it matters, what would change our mind.

### 5. Economic and market outlook whitepaper

**Representative sample:** Vanguard 2026 Economic and Market Outlook.

**Typical structure:**
- Macro thesis.
- Forecast ranges.
- Scenario discipline.
- Risk-adjusted asset-class implications.

**Use for AStock reports:**
- Add explicit base/bull/bear assumptions.
- Avoid fake precision; show what is forecast, what is observed, and what is uncertain.

### 6. Capital markets outlook / cross-asset strategy

**Representative sample:** AllianceBernstein 2Q 2026 Capital Markets Outlook.

**Typical structure:**
- Quarterly theme.
- Cross-asset implications.
- Timeline of previous themes.
- Macro/AI/rate/risk interactions.

**Use for AStock reports:**
- Add catalyst timelines and cross-market linkages.
- Separate structural thesis from quarterly market positioning.

## Gaps / Not Collected

- Original Goldman Sachs Global Investment Research PDFs are often hosted on `gspublishing.com` and may be slow, protected, or not served as public downloadable PDF. Public official pages were saved where accessible.
- Morgan Stanley and UBS often publish rich public outlook pages rather than direct PDF files.
- Reddit/RDDT JPM original report on Scribd appears downloadable only through Scribd’s front-end/account flow. No public raw PDF URL was found.
- Bloomberg/FactSet/Refinitiv/JPM research portal content was not accessed or bypassed.

## Recommended Template Takeaways for AStock

1. **First page must be an investment dashboard**, not just a cover.
2. **Every major conclusion should map to an exhibit**: heatmap, bridge, scenario chart, timeline, or network map.
3. **Source quality belongs in the appendix**, while the main body should present our own view.
4. **Use chartbooks for data-heavy sections**, not long tables.
5. **Use explicit scenario ranges**, especially when source quality is mixed.
6. **Keep compliance boundaries visible**: report is research support, not personalized advice.
