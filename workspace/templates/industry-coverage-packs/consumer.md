# Consumer Industry Coverage Pack

Use for brands, retail, food and beverage, apparel, home appliance, services, and internet consumer reports.

## Mandatory Chain Blocks

1. Consumer demand anchors, category growth, channel mix, and price bands.
2. Brand positioning, product portfolio, and SKU economics.
3. Manufacturing/procurement, supply cost, and gross margin drivers.
4. Distribution, online/offline channels, franchise or dealer system.
5. Inventory, sell-through, promotion, and working-capital signals.
6. Competitive landscape, market share, brand moat, and substitution risk.
7. Pricing power, ASP, volume, margin, and operating leverage.
8. Macro income, demographics, regulation, and platform-policy constraints.

## Required Evidence Modules

- Category and channel data.
- Unit/ASP/margin bridge.
- Inventory and sell-through indicators.
- Competitive share and brand evidence.
- Coverage gaps for private brands, channel checks, and price data.

## Core Valuation Eligibility

Core names need category growth evidence, brand/channel moat, unit/ASP/margin economics, and inventory or sell-through validation.
