# AIDC Industry Coverage Pack

Use for AI data-center, AI computing infrastructure, cloud capex, AIDC supply-chain, and AI server infrastructure reports.

## Mandatory Chain Blocks

1. Compute platform and accelerator demand anchors.
2. Server, OEM, ODM, and rack integration.
3. Data-center power, electrical equipment, UPS, transformers, busway, and backup power.
4. Thermal management, liquid cooling, CDU, cold plate, coolant, pump, valve, and facility cooling.
5. Optical, networking, switch, NIC, interconnect, copper cable, and connector.
6. Storage, memory, HBM/DRAM/NAND, SSD, and storage controller.
7. PCB, CCL, high-speed substrate, connector, cable, and precision components.
8. IDC, cloud, telecom operator, colocation, construction, and upstream equipment/materials/components.

## Required Evidence Modules

- Demand anchors: hyperscaler, AI platform, operator, cloud, or GPU platform capex and deployment evidence.
- Product/process exposure by company.
- Customer or platform qualification.
- Capacity, utilization, delivery, certification, or order visibility.
- ASP or price proxy and value amount.
- Margin pool and price-pass-through mechanism.
- Global and China competitive landscape with CR3/CR5 where available.
- Coverage gap matrix for unavailable private, overseas, or demand-anchor evidence.

## Core Valuation Eligibility

A ticker may enter the core valuation pool only when it has company-level product exposure, customer/platform or order/certification evidence, and enough value-chain economics to support revenue and margin conversion.

## Common Gaps

- Treating NVIDIA/cloud capex as proof of A-share supplier revenue.
- Omitting overseas leaders or private ODMs.
- Mixing IDC operators, power equipment, cooling, optical, storage, and PCB names into one valuation template.
- Ignoring low-purity concept stocks.
