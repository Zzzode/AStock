# AIDC Industry Coverage Pack

Use for AI data-center, AI computing infrastructure, cloud capex, AIDC supply-chain, and AI server infrastructure reports.

## Mandatory Chain Blocks

1. Compute platform and accelerator demand anchors.
2. Server, OEM, ODM, and rack integration.
3. Data-center power, electrical equipment, UPS, transformers, busway, and backup power.
4. Thermal management, liquid cooling, CDU, cold plate, coolant, pump, valve, and facility cooling.
5. Optical, networking, switch, NIC, interconnect, copper cable, and connector.
6. Storage, memory, HBM/DRAM/NAND, SSD, and storage controller.
7. PCB, CCL, high-speed substrate, connector, cable, and precision components.
8. IDC, cloud, telecom operator, colocation, construction, and upstream equipment/materials/components.

## Required Evidence Modules

- Demand anchors: hyperscaler, AI platform, operator, cloud, or GPU platform capex and deployment evidence.
- Product/process exposure by company.
- Customer or platform qualification.
- Capacity, utilization, delivery, certification, or order visibility.
- ASP or price proxy and value amount.
- Margin pool and price-pass-through mechanism.
- Global and China competitive landscape with CR3/CR5 where available.
- Coverage gap matrix for unavailable private, overseas, or demand-anchor evidence.

## AIDC Profit-Pool Fields

AIDC reports must quantify or explicitly mark `not disclosed` for these economics. Block-level prose is insufficient for core valuation credit.

- Per cabinet: cabinet count, power density, rental or service price proxy, utilization, power cost, depreciation, EBITDA margin.
- Per MW/GW: power capacity, build cost, delivery schedule, utilization ramp, capex intensity, IRR/cash-flow proxy.
- AI server: server/rack ASP proxy, GPU/ASIC/BMC/NIC/switch/storage BOM role, ODM margin, shipment/order proxy.
- Optical/networking: 800G/1.6T port or module ASP proxy, optical chip/DSP/silicon photonics/CPO-LPO role, gross-margin pool, customer qualification.
- PCB/CCL/interconnect: layer/spec mix, ASP uplift, yield/capacity, customer qualification, capex and margin sensitivity.
- Power/UPS/transformer/busway: MW equipment value proxy, order/backlog, project margin, delivery cycle, working-capital pressure.
- Liquid cooling: kW/rack, CDU/cold plate/coolant ASP proxy, certification, design-in customer, margin and after-sales economics.
- IDC/cloud/operator: signed capacity, utilization ramp, power quota, capex/debt load, cash-flow conversion, renewal or contract duration.

## Required Exhibits

Every full AIDC deep dive must include or explicitly downgrade if unavailable:

- Full-chain architecture diagram using Mermaid.
- Profit-pool chart by chain block and core ticker.
- Core-ticker evidence heatmap covering customer/order/ASP/capacity/utilization/margin.
- Valuation-growth matrix comparing current implied expectations and evidence quality.
- Sensitivity exhibit for order conversion, ASP, utilization or margin.
- Customer/order evidence map that separates demand anchors from upstream revenue proof.

## Evidence Downgrade Rules

- NVIDIA, cloud, operator or AI model capex is a demand anchor only; it cannot support upstream A-share EPS credit without customer/order/product evidence.
- A ticker missing customer/platform qualification or order visibility cannot be `core_valuation`; classify it as `satellite_watch` or `watchlist only / insufficient customer evidence`.
- A ticker missing ASP/value amount or margin-pool evidence cannot receive earnings valuation credit; classify it as optionality or watchlist-only.
- A ticker missing capacity/utilization evidence cannot convert capacity into revenue, EPS or target-price upside.
- Repeated `not disclosed` fields across customer/order/ASP/utilization for a core ticker are A-Level by default and S-Level when an investable target depends on them.

## Core Valuation Eligibility

A ticker may enter the core valuation pool only when it has company-level product exposure, customer/platform or order/certification evidence, and enough value-chain economics to support revenue and margin conversion.

## Common Gaps

- Treating NVIDIA/cloud capex as proof of A-share supplier revenue.
- Omitting overseas leaders or private ODMs.
- Mixing IDC operators, power equipment, cooling, optical, storage, and PCB names into one valuation template.
- Ignoring low-purity concept stocks.
