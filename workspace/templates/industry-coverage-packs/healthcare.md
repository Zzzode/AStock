# Healthcare Industry Coverage Pack

Use for innovative drugs, medical devices, CXO, diagnostics, hospitals, pharmacies, and healthcare services reports.

## Mandatory Chain Blocks

1. Patient population, epidemiology, reimbursement, and clinical demand anchors.
2. Pipeline, product portfolio, indications, and lifecycle.
3. Clinical evidence, registration, approval, and guideline adoption.
4. Manufacturing, quality system, supply security, and channel access.
5. Pricing, reimbursement, volume, penetration, and gross margin economics.
6. Competitive landscape by indication or product category.
7. Sales channel, hospital access, procurement, and policy risk.
8. Regulatory, IP, safety, ethics, and compliance constraints.

## Required Evidence Modules

- Clinical or regulatory evidence.
- Market-size decomposition by patients, penetration, dosage, price, and reimbursement.
- Competitive landscape and substitution risk.
- Value-chain economics from price/volume/margin to earnings.
- Coverage gaps for unavailable trial, approval, or pricing data.

## Core Valuation Eligibility

Core names need approved or evidence-backed assets, visible commercialization economics, and a valuation method matching lifecycle and profit status.
