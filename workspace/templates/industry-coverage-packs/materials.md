# Materials Industry Coverage Pack

Use for specialty materials, electronic materials, chemical materials, metals, gases, films, coatings, and upstream resource reports.

## Mandatory Chain Blocks

1. Raw materials, resource security, and feedstock cost.
2. Processing route, technology grade, purity/specification, and yield.
3. Core product families and application mapping.
4. Customer qualification, certification, import substitution, and order visibility.
5. Capacity, utilization, expansion projects, and bottlenecks.
6. ASP/price proxy, margin pool, cost pass-through, and cycle sensitivity.
7. Global and China competitive landscape.
8. Environmental, safety, policy, export-control, and logistics constraints.

## Required Evidence Modules

- Product-grade evidence.
- Capacity and qualification evidence.
- Price/margin economics.
- Customer or downstream process mapping.
- Coverage gap matrix for private/overseas leaders and unavailable price data.

## Core Valuation Eligibility

Core names need product-grade evidence, capacity/qualification support, price or ASP proxy, and a margin bridge. Pure resource optionality without product economics remains satellite.
