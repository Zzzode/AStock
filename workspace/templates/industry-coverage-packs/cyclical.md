# Cyclical Industry Coverage Pack

Use for commodities, industrial cyclicals, transportation, construction chain, energy, machinery, and macro-sensitive sectors.

## Mandatory Chain Blocks

1. Demand anchors: construction, manufacturing, exports, infrastructure, energy, or downstream capex.
2. Supply capacity, operating rates, inventory, and marginal cost curve.
3. Product grade, logistics, regional spread, and substitution.
4. Price, spread, cost, and margin pool.
5. Contract, spot, futures, and inventory evidence where applicable.
6. Global and China competitive landscape and capacity concentration.
7. Balance sheet, capex cycle, working capital, and cash conversion.
8. Policy, environmental, export, tariff, and macro risk.

## Required Evidence Modules

- Price/spread and volume bridge.
- Supply-demand balance and inventory.
- Cost curve and margin sensitivity.
- Cycle position and downside stress.
- Coverage gaps for private capacity, overseas supply, or opaque inventories.

## Core Valuation Eligibility

Core names need cycle-normalized earnings, asset/cost position evidence, balance-sheet resilience, and valuation methods suited to cyclicality such as PB/ROE, EV/EBITDA, SOTP, or cycle-adjusted PE.
