# Semiconductor Industry Coverage Pack

Use for semiconductor equipment, materials, fabs, design, packaging, EDA/IP, and semiconductor localization reports.

## Mandatory Chain Blocks

1. Downstream demand anchors: foundry, IDM, OSAT, memory, automotive, industrial, AI, and consumer electronics.
2. Design, EDA, IP, and chip architecture.
3. Wafer manufacturing, foundry, process node, and fab capacity.
4. Semiconductor equipment: lithography, etch, deposition, cleaning, metrology, ion implantation, CMP, furnace, and testing.
5. Materials: wafer, photoresist, precursor, specialty gas, wet chemicals, slurry, target, mask, and packaging substrate.
6. Advanced packaging, testing, probe card, carrier, thermal, and interconnect.
7. Distribution, customer qualification, and localization substitution path.
8. Export control, supply security, and overseas leader comparison.

## Required Evidence Modules

- Process node or application mapping.
- Customer/fab qualification and certification status.
- Capacity, yield, utilization, and shipment/order evidence.
- ASP or price proxy and margin pool.
- Global and China competitive landscape with localization boundary.
- Value-chain economics and evidence gaps.

## Core Valuation Eligibility

Core names need product/process exposure, validated customer or fab qualification, economics that can bridge to revenue/margin, and a business-model matched valuation method.
