# Template Brief

**Archetype:** institutional strategic-allocation evidence memo, adapted to a China-mainland personal-market boundary.

**Reader decision:** distinguish (1) a publicly documented institutional method, (2) its economic engine, (3) a constrained personal research analogue, and (4) a strategy that must remain non-equivalent or blocked.

**Narrative order:** decision boundary → institutional taxonomy → cross-market translation matrix → three concrete research protocols → crypto jurisdiction boundary → personal admission workflow → red team → source appendix.

**Presentation rules:** source IDs beside material facts; tables are prose-supported; formulas are transparent house methodology; no target return, allocation, product selection, account action or order. The reader must see `q=0` before any rule description.
