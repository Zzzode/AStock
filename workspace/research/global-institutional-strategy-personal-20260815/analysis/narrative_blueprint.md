# Narrative Blueprint

The reader should leave with a sharper answer than “copy a quant fund.” The report first separates an institution’s public story from its economic engine. It then asks whether a personal investor can reproduce the required instruments, data, funding, legal rights and execution—separately for China securities, domestic futures and crypto. Only then does it show transparent CTA, factor and event research rules.

The conclusion is neither pessimism nor a product recommendation: transfer the research discipline; do not pretend to transfer the institutional edge. A qualified futures investor may eventually evaluate a domestic-futures paper-replay protocol, but contract-level proof and risk approval remain separate. Crypto remains economic research only in this China-mainland framework. The final pages turn these distinctions into an admission workflow and red-team challenge list.
