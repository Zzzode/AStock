# House View

Global leaders do not possess one magic signal. Their durable advantages combine a valid economic engine with breadth of instruments, primary data, specialist teams, financing/borrow, risk aggregation, execution quality, legal rights or control. A personal framework should therefore copy **research discipline**, not a manager’s label.

The strongest portable candidates are deliberately modest: a delayed-data domestic-futures trend **paper research** protocol; point-in-time, long-only A-share factor **research**; and timestamped public-information event **research**. These are not alpha claims. They are falsifiable operating designs that can later be rejected after a formal replay.

The framework blocks equivalence claims for HFT/stat-arb, market making, volatility dispersion, global carry/RV, private equity and private credit. It also blocks a China-mainland crypto route. The present default is 100% protected liquidity only because individual account, contract, data, risk and legal evidence is missing—not because cash has an expected-return advantage.
