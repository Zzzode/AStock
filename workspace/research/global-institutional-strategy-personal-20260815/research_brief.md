# Research Brief — Global institutional strategies translated for China personal markets

## Decision question

Which specific, publicly described strategy families used by leading global investment institutions are economically meaningful, operationally feasible, or structurally non-replicable for a China-mainland personal investor across A shares, domestic futures and crypto markets?

## Frozen scope

- **Data cutoff:** 2026-08-15.
- **Institutional reference set:** Bridgewater, Man AHL, AQR, Citadel, Two Sigma, Blackstone, Apollo and the broad HFR hedge-fund taxonomy. Their official materials describe mandates, methods or infrastructure; they do not prove transferable alpha, expected returns or investor suitability.
- **Market translation:** China-mainland listed securities and lawful domestic futures research; crypto is evaluated as an economic research category and a regulatory/access boundary, not an implementation route for a China-mainland resident.
- **User context:** A futures qualification is reported conversationally, but no broker, exchange, contract, data-entitlement, risk-capital or tax evidence has been supplied. The report therefore keeps every market exposure at `q=0`.
- **Deliverable:** Chinese strategic-investment evidence memo with a reusable operating playbook, no named security, product, target price, allocation, order or live-execution authority.

## Required answers

1. What is the documented institutional strategy taxonomy, and what economic engine does each family use?
2. Which families can be translated into China A-share or domestic-futures research without falsely claiming equivalence?
3. Which families can be researched in crypto markets, and why does that not create a China-mainland implementation route?
4. What exact data, timing, account, risk and monitoring sequence would a personal investor need before paper research or any separate manual decision?
5. Which attractive-sounding strategies must stay blocked because the institutional advantage is structurally unavailable?

## Boundaries that cannot be relaxed

1. No claim of replicating a named manager's proprietary signals, historical return, execution quality, financing terms or access.
2. No named stock, ETF, futures contract, cryptoasset, exchange, wallet, product or account is an approved investment or execution instruction.
3. A domestic futures strategy remains research-only until dated broker/exchange eligibility, actual-contract lifecycle data, margin, position-limit, delivery and roll evidence are present.
4. China-mainland crypto activity is not presented as a lawful implementation route, workaround or execution plan.
5. Statistical arbitrage, HFT market making, global carry/RV and volatility dispersion are not relabelled as simple indicator, grid or retail-option strategies.

## Report classification and downgrade path

This case is a `strategic_allocation` research-framework evidence memo. It may be published as education and research only if evidence, PDF, review and verifier gates pass. If any primary-source, data-timing, legal-access or render gate fails, downgrade to an unpublished research note; never upgrade it into a personalised recommendation or live portfolio approval.
