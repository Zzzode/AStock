## S14 — China virtual-currency risk notice

- URL: https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7614318/content.shtml?f_link_type=f_linkinlinenote
- Authority: joint PBOC/CSRC and related agencies notice, published by CSRC on 2026-02-06.
- Fact used: it states that virtual currency does not have legal-tender status, strictly prohibits virtual-currency-related business activities in China, and says overseas entities/individuals may not illegally provide related services to domestic entities.
- Limitation: it is not an individual legal opinion. This report therefore treats mainland crypto implementation as not offered rather than attempting to infer a workaround.
