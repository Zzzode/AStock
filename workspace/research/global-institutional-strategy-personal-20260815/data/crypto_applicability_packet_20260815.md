# Crypto Applicability Packet

**Current status: q=0; implementation not approved.** The CSRC-hosted 2026 joint notice is used only to set the China-mainland research boundary (S14). This is not a legal opinion, and the packet does not offer a venue, wallet, product, technical method or cross-border workaround.

Any future non-mainland, jurisdiction-specific economic research would still require all ten dossier items in the JSON packet: independent legal/access evidence, precise vehicle identity, custody and counterparty rights, pricing, liquidity, funding/leverage restrictions, protocol risks, tax/accounting, approved risk budget and independent red-team review. Until then, crypto may be discussed only as a market-structure research subject.
