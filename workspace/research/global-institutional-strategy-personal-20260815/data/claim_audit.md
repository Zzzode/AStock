# Claim Audit

The JSON packet is the machine-readable authority. Every reader-facing factual claim is either source-linked or explicitly labelled as an analytical translation. “Verified” means only that the limited wording is supported; it never means that an institutional return stream, a product, or personal suitability has been verified.

| Claim IDs | Classification | Evidence use | Publication treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C01–C06 | Public institutional descriptions | Taxonomy and archetypes | Cite source ID next to the statement; do not infer returns or exact models. |
| C07–C08 | Primary academic references | CTA/factor vocabulary | Treat as research references, not domestic performance evidence. |
| C09–C12 | Exchange/regulatory facts | Operating and jurisdiction boundary | Use dated, scoped wording; no legal or suitability conclusion. |
| C13–C16 | Analytical translations | Personal research design and exclusions | Mark as house methodology; do not present as an institutional replica. |
| C17 | Framework scope control | Current q=0 state | Not an allocation recommendation or return call. |

Critical claims C12, C16 and C17 are independently checked in R0, R1 and R4. See `sources/official-source-notes-20260815/source_capture_notes.md` for source-level notes.
