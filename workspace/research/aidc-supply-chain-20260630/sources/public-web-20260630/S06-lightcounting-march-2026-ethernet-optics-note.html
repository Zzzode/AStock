$100 Billion Market for AI Cluster Optics by 2030?

March 2026

LightCounting releases March 2026 edition of Ethernet Optics Report

This is not a typo. There is a reasonable chance for annual sales of optical interconnects used in AI clusters to reach $100 billion by 2030. However, many “stars have to align just right” for this to happen.

Sales of Ethernet optical transceivers doubled in 2024 and increased by another 70% in 2025, exceeding our expectations. Suppliers of InP lasers added capacity to meet the growing demand last year, and shortages for these and many other devices used in transceiver manufacturing are easing now. There will be enough production capacity to double the sales of optical transceivers in 2026, but it will be more than what is needed by the customers. Shortages for XPU and switch ASICs will limit expansion of AI clusters in 2026, and this should limit the growth in sales of optical transceivers to “only” 60% in 2026, unless customers create deep inventory reserves for the future.

There is a lot more uncertainty about the market growth in 2027-2031. It is possible that the “Fantastic Growth” will continue in 2027 and beyond, as illustrated in the figure below. Historical data on sales of optical transceivers in 2004-2025 shows a cyclical pattern as growth periods of 2-3 years are interrupted by a flat or negative year. Most of the cycles are due to supply chain rebalancing, and we may see a similar situation in 2027-2028. Our forecast projects for a “Soft Landing,” but a supply chain equilibrium is often restored by a market correction—the “Bumpy Ride” scenario.

There are several tailwinds for the optical connectivity in AI clusters. Larger clusters will require more optical transceivers per GPU – up to 6 transceivers per GPU in scale-out networks. Optical connectivity in scale-up networks, which require almost 10x more bandwidth compared to scale-out networks, opens another market segment for suppliers. However, the “Fantastic Growth” scenario looks like a fantasy compared to the historical data.

One can argue that the AI boom has changed everything and the market history can be safely ignored. However, the top four cloud companies (Amazon, Google, Meta and Microsoft) responsible for more than 50% of AI spending on optical transceivers today, accounted for more than 40% of Ethernet transceiver sales in 2018-2023. History may not repeat itself, but it may rhyme.

Many “stars have to align just right” for the “Fantastic Growth” scenario, but even one of the factors can push the market into the “Bumpy Ride” scenario.

This report analyzes the impact of growing data traffic and the changing architecture of data centers on the market forecast for Ethernet optical transceivers with a focus on the high-speed modules used in AI clusters. It leverages extensive historical data on shipments of Ethernet modules combined with extensive market research to forecast sales of these products in 2026-2031. The forecast includes more than 100 product categories, including 100GbE, 200GbE, 400G, 800G, 1.6T and 3.2T retimed transceivers, LPO/LRO and CPO/NPO sorted by reach and form factors.
