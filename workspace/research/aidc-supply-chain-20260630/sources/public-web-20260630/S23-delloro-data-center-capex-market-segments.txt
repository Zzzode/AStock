		 Dell’Oro Group’s Data Center IT Capex reports track global infrastructure spending by hyperscale cloud service providers and enterprises, a market on pace to exceed $1 trillion in 2026. 
 The reports provide detailed analysis of data center capital expenditures, with specific emphasis on IT equipment such as servers and storage systems, as well as metrics including installed server base, number of regions and availability zones, and global infrastructure footprint. 
 The report focuses on how hyperscale cloud investments are shaping the broader data center infrastructure market. As data center capex represents the largest share of Cloud service provider spending, even small fluctuations in spending by these companies can create significant ripple effects across the global supply chain. To provide a comprehensive view of the market, Dell’Oro Group tracks capex across key customer segments, including: 
 
 Top 4 US Cloud: Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft 
 Top 4 China Cloud: Alibaba, Baidu, ByteDance, Tencent 
 Top 4 Tier 2 Cloud: Apple, CoreWeave, IBM, Oracle 
 Rest-of-Cloud: includes neoclouds and AI model developers 
 Colocation 
 Telco 
 Enterprise 
 
 Data center capital spending is a critical indicator of demand across the entire IT infrastructure ecosystem. Investments in servers—particularly accelerated systems for AI workloads—drive spending across facilities, networking, and storage, influencing vendor revenues and supply chain dynamics. At the same time, the expansion of cloud regions and availability zones reflects long-term growth strategies, while shifts in spending allocation between AI and general-purpose infrastructure are reshaping technology roadmaps and competitive positioning. 
 With this report, you will be able to answer critical business questions including: 
 
 What were the primary drivers of cloud service provider capex this quarter, and how is it being allocated across servers, networking, and facilities? 
 How is capital spending being distributed between accelerated AI-optimized server platforms and general-purpose compute and storage infrastructure? 
 What are the strategic implications of cloud spending trends for global supply chain vendors and regional market outlooks? 
 What is the current global footprint of cloud service providers, and how are their expansion plans impacting legacy Enterprise growth? 
 How are emerging technologies like edge computing shaping the long-term infrastructure requirements for the Telco segment? 
 
 To answer these and other important questions, Dell’Oro Group delivers quarterly reports and five-year forecast reports on the Data Center Capex. 
										 Data Center IT Capex Quarterly Reports

 The Dell’Oro Group Data Center Capex report includes quantitative and qualitative information, and outlook on the market and the top-ten Cloud service providers and the major customer segments.  Data center capex

 Number of data centers, regions, and availability zones globally and by region
