Raw scope notes from S03:

- The merger is a common-control combination; 600150 retrospectively adjusted prior financial statements.
- The statutory accounting combination date is disclosed as **2025-09-30**, determined by transfer of control.
- The absorbed entity contributed revenue of CNY47.1773bn and net profit of CNY2.7093bn from 2025 year-start to the accounting combination date. Its comparative-period revenue and net profit were CNY35.2698bn and CNY0.9421bn.
- China Shipbuilding Industry's pre-combination profit is classified within the listed company's non-recurring items for statutory adjusted-profit presentation. This is why the adjusted-parent-profit series cannot be reconstructed by simply adding the two legacy companies' adjusted profits.
