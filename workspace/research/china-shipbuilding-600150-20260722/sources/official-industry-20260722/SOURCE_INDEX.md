## Perimeter rule

“中国船舶集团 / CSSC group” and listed 中国船舶（600150.SH）are different scopes. Group rankings, group orders, and sister-yard achievements are context only unless an exchange filing identifies the listed subsidiary, contract, and consolidation boundary. In particular, 沪东中华 is outside the disclosed 600150 post-merger principal-yard perimeter and its LNG orders are not counted as 600150 orders.
