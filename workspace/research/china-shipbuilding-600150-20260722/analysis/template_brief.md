# Template Brief

## Recommended Archetype

Use the J.P. Morgan single-stock equity-research note as the primary archetype, combined with Vanguard-style scenario discipline and AllianceBernstein-style cycle and catalyst exhibits. This is a company note, not a shipbuilding chartbook: every industry page must advance the earnings or valuation argument for 600150.SH.

- `workspace/templates/global-broker-research/pdfs/jpm-equity-research-hulu-sample.pdf`: first-page rating, current price, target/fair-value range, thesis and risk dashboard.

## Required First Page

- Legally effective company/restructuring basis, price date, current price, fair-value range, implied return and action.
- Three-part house view: backlog quality and mix, delivery/margin conversion, and what the current price already discounts.
- 2026E revenue/net profit/EPS bridge with evidence quality.
- Bear/base/bull range, key validation thresholds and strongest opposing argument.
- Explicit warning where order, naval-business or Street evidence is unavailable or not comparable.

## Required Chapters

1. Investment-committee summary and action discipline.
2. Company and restructuring perimeter: actual versus pro-forma basis.
3. Shipbuilding cycle: demand, capacity, contracting, prices and delivery slots.
4. Product/yard mix, competitive position and chain economics.
5. Backlog-to-revenue-to-margin bridge and earnings scenarios.
6. Financial quality, cash flow, customer advances, capex and balance sheet.
7. Valuation: cycle-adjusted PE/EV-EBITDA or SOTP/PB checks, market-implied expectations and Street comparison.
8. Secondary-market behavior, catalysts and monitoring calendar.
9. Risk, contrarian case, falsification and compliance boundary.
10. Evidence, source-quality and model appendices.

## Required Exhibits

- Restructuring perimeter and share-count bridge.
- Global orderbook/new-order/delivery and newbuild-price cycle map.
- Yard and product-mix map by merchant/naval/repair segment.
- Backlog quality scorecard separating official listed-company evidence from group-level or inferred context.
- Delivery and margin-release waterfall.
- Working-capital and customer-advance bridge.
- Bear/base/bull valuation band and market-implied earnings requirements.
- Peer valuation and cycle-position matrix.
- Catalyst timeline, risk heatmap and evidence pyramid.

## What Not To Do

- Do not begin with a generic shipping-industry history.
- Do not use group-level CSSC order announcements as listed-company revenue evidence without perimeter reconciliation.
- Do not equate rising newbuild prices with realized margin before delivery timing and cost lock-in are addressed.
- Do not use one strong quarter or pre-restructuring EPS denominator as the final valuation basis.
- Do not stack long tables in the main body; move dense source audits to appendices.
