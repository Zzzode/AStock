\begin{houseviewbox}[Investment view]
We rate China CSSC Holdings (600150.SH) \textbf{Neutral-positive / Hold while validating}. At CNY33.02, the stock is already close to our independently derived base value of CNY33.08. Our multi-anchor target is CNY33.07, implying approximately 0.15\% upside, with a base fair-value range of CNY31.64--35.96. The next re-rating requires simultaneous evidence of earnings delivery, core-business margin improvement and operating cash conversion; backlog size alone is insufficient.
\end{houseviewbox}

The legally effective listed entity is the post-merger company with 7.526 billion shares. Historical comparisons use the 2025 common-control restatement, while 2025 reported EPS retains the disclosed weighted-average denominator. The company reported CNY151.98 billion of 2025 revenue and CNY7.85 billion of parent net profit. Its unaudited 2026 first-half preview indicates CNY9.2--11.0 billion of parent net profit and CNY9.0--10.8 billion of adjusted parent net profit. We do not annualize either the first quarter or the first-half preview.

Our base case forecasts 2026--2028 revenue of CNY177.5, CNY200.0 and CNY215.0 billion; parent net profit of CNY17.28, CNY21.65 and CNY24.68 billion; and EPS of CNY2.296, CNY2.877 and CNY3.280. The model bridges effective delivery volume, a clearly labelled revenue-intensity proxy, price/mix, recognition, segment margins, expenses, tax and minority interests. It does not convert the CNY467.45 billion civil/offshore backlog directly into revenue. The approximately CNY50 billion COSCO project is already included in aggregate disclosed orders and therefore receives no incremental revenue or EPS credit.

Our 2027E primary method applies 11.5 times PE to EPS of CNY2.8767, yielding CNY33.08. Bear/base/bull scenario values are CNY11.21, CNY33.08 and CNY48.34. The final target applies 85\% weight to fundamental value, 15\% to the observable market anchor and zero weight to broker target prices because original broker PDFs do not disclose usable targets. Unverified military economics, unallocated CSSC Group orders, a possible Hudong-Zhonghua injection and weak third-party target-price leads all receive zero valuation credit.

The strongest bull counterargument is that waiting for formal half-year cash-flow confirmation may miss a high-certainty earnings-upgrade phase. We would become more constructive if formal first-half parent/adjusted profit exceeds CNY10.1/9.9 billion, core shipbuilding/repair/offshore gross margin is at least 11.72\%, operating cash flow is positive and delivery remains on plan. We would downgrade if profit falls below CNY9.2/9.0 billion, core margin drops below 10.5\%, major delivery or customer-financing problems emerge, or full-year operating cash flow remains below 0.8 times parent net profit without a visible recovery path.
