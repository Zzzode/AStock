# Template Brief — Full-Market Refresh

- Archetype: institutional full-market strategy refresh.
- Chapter sequence: investment decision -> market state -> valuation discipline -> focus names -> risks -> evidence boundary -> audit appendices.
- Required exhibits: formal-model action table, three-tier action framework, conditional-event list, sector-stage map, valuation-method matrix, formal-model comparison, recovery-model map, risk monitor and evidence coverage.
- Avoid: presenting a 142-row formula ledger before the decision, treating internal ranges as external consensus, or presenting the partial 30/112 continuous-flow table as a complete universe.
