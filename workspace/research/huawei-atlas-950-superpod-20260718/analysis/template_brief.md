# Template Benchmark Brief

## Recommended Archetype

Use an institutional industry-chain deep dive with a first-page investment-committee dashboard. The narrative should combine BlackRock-style structural-theme framing, J.P. Morgan-style stock dashboards and valuation bridges, Vanguard-style observed/forecast/scenario separation, and AllianceBernstein-style catalyst and risk mapping.

## Reference Samples

- `workspace/templates/global-broker-research/deep_template_analysis.md`
- J.P. Morgan equity-research note for the first-page decision dashboard.
- BlackRock BII outlook for a concise house-view spine.
- Vanguard outlook for scenario discipline and avoidance of fake precision.
- AllianceBernstein outlook for catalyst timelines, risk heatmaps, and scenario ranges.

## Selected Industry Coverage Pack

`workspace/templates/industry-coverage-packs/aidc.md`. All eight mandatory blocks must appear in the full-chain universe or in the coverage-gap matrix: compute demand anchors; server/OEM/ODM; power; thermal/liquid cooling; optical/networking/interconnect; storage/memory; PCB/CCL/connectors/cables; and IDC/cloud/operator/upstream infrastructure.

## Required First Page

The first page must show the event and data cutoff, the report's direct conclusion, final core/satellite ranking, current prices, fair-value ranges or watchlist boundaries, implied upside/downside where defensible, evidence quality, the next-quarter validation trigger, and the strongest invalidation condition.

## Required Chapter Sequence

1. Investment-committee summary and action map.
2. What Atlas 950 is: 950DT, SuperPoD, UnifiedBus, and 1024-versus-8192 configuration reconciliation.
3. Architecture transition and bill-of-material implications.
4. Full-chain universe, taxonomy, and coverage gaps.
5. Company-level customer-chain evidence and value-chain economics.
6. Competitive landscape and localization boundary.
7. Growth-earnings bridges for eligible names.
8. Current-price valuation, market-implied expectations, and broker/Street comparison.
9. Variant perception, risks, catalysts, and monitoring framework.
10. Source, claim, methodology, and compliance appendices.

## Required Exhibits

- Full-chain architecture and value-flow diagram.
- Atlas 900/CloudMatrix 384 versus Atlas 950 comparison.
- 1024-card physical deployment versus 8192-card design-scale reconciliation.
- BOM/profit-pool map by chain block.
- Core-candidate evidence heatmap for customer/order/ASP/capacity/utilization/margin.
- Core-versus-satellite scorecard.
- Earnings bridge and current-price-implied growth sensitivity.
- Multi-anchor valuation matrix.
- Risk heatmap and catalyst timeline.

## What Not To Do

- Do not present a concept-stock list as a supply-chain map.
- Do not treat Huawei's capex or product launch as proof of an A-share supplier order.
- Do not treat the 8192-card roadmap as a currently delivered physical system.
- Do not use one PE template across server, optical, PCB, power, cooling, and IDC companies.
- Do not place workflow files or repository paths in the reader-facing PDF.
- Do not lead main chapters with dense audit tables or broker-summary prose.
