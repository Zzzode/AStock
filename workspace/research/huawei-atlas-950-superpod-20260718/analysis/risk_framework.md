# Risk Framework

## Overall Assessment

Overall risk is High. Product-existence risk is lower after the 1,024-card physical display, but Atlas-specific supplier attribution remains L4 thesis risk and valuation crowding remains L3-L4 market risk. The current model assigns CNY 0 Atlas-specific revenue, profit and EPS, so roadmap failure removes catalyst/multiple optionality rather than modeled Atlas earnings.

## Risk Taxonomy

- Geopolitical/compliance: Entity List/FDPR, HBM/equipment controls, site power and cross-border procurement.
- Competitive: NVIDIA/AMD workload economics, alternative domestic architectures, Huawei internalization or multi-sourcing.
- Execution: 2026Q4 roadmap conversion, 1,024-to-8,192 scaling, memory-definition gap, HBM/package yield, UnifiedBus utilization, supplier/BOM allocation, power/thermal/optical topology and customer acceptance.
- Financial: working capital, margin/yield, concentration and optimistic external forecasts.
- Market: valuation crowding, event reversal and rumor attribution.
- ESG/HRF: restricted-entity, data/security, surveillance and dual-use mandate risks.

## Control Policy

1. Keep Atlas-specific EPS at zero until product + qualification/order + economics are public.
2. Separate system, engineering, supplier and economics proof; one level cannot substitute for another.
3. Use accepted deployments and recognized company revenue, not launch dates or announced capacity.
4. Review live restricted-party and export-control status before any mandate-level decision.
5. Downgrade a claim immediately after official denial, non-confirmation or contradictory architecture evidence.
6. Require cash conversion and segment margin together with order/revenue growth.

## Monitoring Windows

- 2026Q3-Q4: 950DT availability, named customer, accepted system count and production workload.
- 2026-12-31: repeatable commercial deployment and payment/revenue evidence.
- 2027H1: physically identified stable 8,192-card customer system and workload scaling.
- Next technical disclosure: reconciliation of 144 GB local HiZQ 2.0 and 256 TB global address space.
- Next two reporting periods: supplier qualification/order, allocation, ASP, margin, receivables, inventory and operating cash flow.
- October 2026: iFlytek model launch, performance/cost disclosure and monetization.

## Publication Red Lines

- Call the July system an 8,192-card delivered system.
- Label the 256 TB global address space as HBM.
- Infer an exclusive optical/cooling/connector/HBM supplier from capability, rumor or an investor question.
- Use predecessor installations as Atlas 950 order evidence.
- Use policy or operator capex as a company order.
- Add Atlas revenue to EPS without product, order and economics evidence.
- Compare cross-vendor peak FLOPS without precision, sparsity, system and power caveats.
- Ignore Entity List/FDPR, HBM controls or mandate restrictions.
- Recommend position size without the user's portfolio/risk mandate.
- Treat a rising share price as falsification of an evidence gap.
