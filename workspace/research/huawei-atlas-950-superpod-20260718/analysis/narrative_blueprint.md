# Narrative Blueprint

## First-Page Decision

The report opens with a non-consensus but evidence-consistent decision: the physical 1,024-card display validates architecture direction, not the 8,192-card delivery state or any A-share supplier order. It separates relationship evidence from valuation attractiveness and refuses a blanket “Huawei chain” recommendation.

## Chapter Causal Sequence

1. IC summary states the decision and ranked monitoring actions.
2. Evidence governance corrects the four highest-risk factual conflations.
3. Technology explains why a larger scale-up domain changes system bottlenecks.
4. Full-chain research traces value amount to order, revenue, margin, cash flow and valuation credit.
5. Company analysis separates direct relationships, broad AIDC earnings and low-purity value satellites.
6. Street analysis identifies forecast usefulness and consensus blind spots.
7. Valuation translates evidence into twelve reproducible models with zero Atlas-specific credit.
8. Risk analysis defines invalidation and observable triggers.
9. Full-chain appendix proves that unavailable and negative nodes were not omitted.
10. Recommendation closes with evidence-upgrade actions, not event-driven trading language.

## House View / Variant Perception

- Consensus: a physical Atlas 950 display foreshadows broad, immediate upstream orders.
- House view: physical validation reduces architecture risk but leaves customer, BOM, supplier allocation and company economics unresolved.
- Assumption gap: the market collapses four sequential gates into one event.
- Strongest opposing case: suppliers cannot disclose confidential orders, so public evidence will lag price discovery.
- House response: preserve a capability watchlist and update frequently, but do not add unsupported EPS.
- What proves the house view wrong: public platform qualification/order/revenue evidence arrives quickly and margins validate before the market multiple compresses.

## Reader-Facing Language Rules

- Use “first public physical Atlas 950 display,” not “Huawei’s first supernode.”
- Use “1,024-card physical system” and “8,192-card maximum-design roadmap” separately.
- Use “256 TB global unified address space,” not “256 TB HBM.”
- Use “Huawei/Ascend relationship” only at the disclosed scope.
- Use “Atlas-specific revenue credit is zero,” not “industrial opportunity is zero.”
