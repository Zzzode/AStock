# Source Exhaustion / Open Data Requirements

Formal point-in-time total-return evidence, two frozen benchmarks, a dated
cross-asset T2 set, candidate-level distribution/tax/cost reconciliation,
risk-budget exception, suitability record and current market data remain open.
Direct-equity, ETF, REIT, sovereign and cash candidates must each complete
structure-specific item diligence. These are not hidden risks: they force q=0
and protected-liquidity fallback. Cash-distribution information remains a
total-return accounting and product-diligence input, not a standalone strategy
target.

The 2026-08-14 public four-ETF screen is a negative diagnostic, not a partial
backtest: 207 of 633 complete rolling five-year *price* windows were below 5%.
It lacks total-return events, costs, taxes, market-status and execution data.
`data/ou_tr5_v1_replay_requirements_20260814.{json,md}` records the required
pre-test controls. The draft dynamic rulebook/mapping and the field-level data
admission matrix are not frozen or source-tested. Evidence-limited R1
diligence now covers all T1 groups, but every nominee still needs R1 closure
and R2 valuation, liquidity, access and portfolio-overlap work. See
`source_exhaustion_log.json`.

The formal replay also needs a unitized, scale-neutral engine. The current
discrete-lot portfolio simulator cannot answer the present research objective.
ETF/REIT events, bond coupons/redemptions and non-standard corporate actions
must be added or explicitly block the affected historical period. The same
holds for the separately marked receipt state and the protected-liquidity and
look-through field contract. Finally, 260008 and the three current REIT
nominees lack five years of listed history; they can remain prospective T1
research names but cannot be treated as five-year realised return observations
at this cutoff.

The official CSI index discovery cross-check also rejects a shortcut through a
published static reference: none of six predeclared broad-equity, dividend or
equity--bond rules cleared every observed five-year discovery window. The
closest 30/70 reference still had 32 misses out of 999 and a 4.43% minimum,
before product-level costs or execution. See
`data/official_index_discovery_20260814.{json,md}`.
