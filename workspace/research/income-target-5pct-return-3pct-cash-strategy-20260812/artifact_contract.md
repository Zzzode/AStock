# Artifact Contract

This is an open-universe strategic-allocation **research framework**, not an
approved portfolio. It preserves the full opportunity set at research intake,
then restricts actual portfolio consideration through a finite decision set,
item-level institutional research and portfolio-level approval. Publication
requires transparent treatment of the sole conditional 5% total-return
objective, TWRR/MWRR accounting, distribution handling without double counting,
return-evidence and risk-budget blockers, current `q=0` state and a scoped
sign-off. It does not permit an annual-return guarantee, a separate cash target,
a hidden implementation approval or an asset-class quota disguised as a
recommendation.

| Artifact family | Required decision content | Blocking condition |
|---|---|---|
| Intake and house view | Sole conditional 5% TWRR objective; T0/T1/T2/T3/T4; first-round queue versus open research universe; current action | A first-round ETF/REIT list is presented as a permanent ban on direct equities or other eligible instruments. |
| Return model | TWRR, MWRR/XIRR, external-flow rule, distributions counted once, tax/fee/settlement and return-of-capital treatment, open-universe / finite-approval state | Separate cash target, sale proceeds as investment return, invalid flow treatment, or preallocated class weights without a T2/T4 approval. |
| Item research | Structure-specific direct-equity, ETF/fund, REIT and sovereign/cash dossiers; current valuation/reference and liquidity evidence | A ticker, screen, category label or historical distribution substitutes for an item dossier. |
| Risk and execution | Retired-vector stress, current `q=0`, cross-asset overlap, dynamic capacity, protected-liquidity fallback | A rejected fixed vector is treated as risk-approved or tradable. |
| Reader report | Open research universe, today’s non-action, upgrade gates, objective-outcome path and appendix glossary | A product queue is presented as live holdings, order advice or a complete investment menu. |
| Sign-off | `RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK_PASS`, implementation not approved, four blocked implementation gates and no live authorization | Generic PASS, missing blocker, or stale rendered artifact. |

Machine-readable owners, blockers and review cycles are in
`artifact_contract.json`.
