## Mandate correction

The prior version incorrectly retained an independent cash-receipt objective
after the user had removed it. This revision supersedes that formulation. The
sole strategy research objective is a rolling five-year, cost-aware
time-weighted total return of at least 5% annualized after a specified
portfolio has been frozen and tested. It is conditional and ex post; it is not
a guarantee, calendar-year floor, asset-value protection claim, probability
estimate or approval to invest.

Cash distributions remain relevant only as facts needed to reconstruct total
return and assess product economics. Settled interest, dividends, ETF
distributions and REIT distributions are recorded once, with tax, fee,
settlement and return-of-capital-like components identified separately. They
do not create a second target, cash-yield threshold, allocation quota or
annual success test. Cash disposition remains investor-directed: retain,
withdraw or take no action. Any later proposal to deploy cash into a market
instrument is a new, dated portfolio review subject to the full T1--T4 and
effective-$q$ gates.

## Scope correction

The earlier 10% / 15% / 40% / 25% / 10% reference vector and the exclusion of
direct stocks were a first-round product-research design, not a valid permanent
investment mandate. They are retired as allocation instructions. A predefined
product menu would prevent a direct equity, ETF, REIT, sovereign instrument or
cash product from competing on its actual marginal total return, valuation,
risk and liquidity contribution.

The standing policy is therefore:

> **Open research universe; finite decision universe; limited approved
> investable universe; dynamic approved portfolio.**

The open universe covers lawful and account-accessible China public-market
instruments: direct A-share equities, listed equity/fixed-income/commodity
funds and ETFs, public REITs, sovereign and cash instruments. A product is not
accepted merely because it is in one of those formats. Margin financing,
leveraged or inverse products, futures/options, private or materially
illiquid instruments require a separate written authorization path; this is
not a judgment that they can never be researched.

The ten previously named products are the first completed product due-diligence
queue, not an exclusive menu. Direct equities may be nominated at any research
cycle; none is currently named, has a current-price valuation package, or is
approved for the investable universe.

## Item-level standard and portfolio gates

Every non-zero holding must have an auditable, current research dossier at the
level appropriate to its structure:

- Direct equity: issuer/industry evidence, audited financials, driver-to-
  revenue/EPS bridge, governance, current-price bull/base/bear valuation,
  cash/dividend quality, catalysts, invalidation, A-share float/liquidity and
  price-limit/suspension review; its T3 code must reconcile one-for-one to
  dated valuation and broker/Street records.
- ETF/fund: legal structure, index or mandate, holdings look-through,
  concentration/overlap, NAV/IOPV or reference-value method, tracking,
  distributions, derivatives/securities-lending where material, costs and
  execution capacity.
- Public REIT: project/tenant or offtaker operations, cash-distribution bridge,
  debt/refinancing/capex, asset valuation/NAV timing, governance and
  liquidity.
- Sovereign/cash product: issuer/guarantee/term mechanics, withdrawal or
  maturity terms, cash-flow convention, tax, account and concentration.

Passing the item dossier is necessary but insufficient. A candidate must also
pass portfolio-level total-return attribution, correlation and common
risk-driver mapping, marginal stress contribution, capacity, concentration,
tax/settlement and suitability review. No asset-class quota must be filled.
Cash may remain the correct outcome.
