# Source Registry

| ID | Authority | Record | What it supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | CSRC | Public REIT guideline | 90% annual distributable-cash distribution rule | A cash-yield or return guarantee |
| R02--R09 | SSE / fund managers | Public REIT annual report and distribution notices | Historical or announced cash facts | Future cash or total return |
| R10--R12 | SSE / fund managers | Dividend ETF notices and annual reports | Actual/cited ETF distribution mechanics and history | Fixed ETF cash income |
| R13 | SSE / fund manager | 511010 product summary | Treasury-ETF identity and non-guarantee boundary | Protected liquidity or a fixed coupon |
| R14 | SSE / fund manager | 511010 2025 annual report | Historical 2023--2025 NAV growth, multi-year NAV-growth history, and 2025 distribution record | Investor account return, final-portfolio result, forward cash yield, or return floor |

The machine-readable registry at `data/source_registry.json` contains the full URLs, local source paths and limitations.
