# OU-TR5-v1 Formal Replay Requirements

- Status: **not formally testable; portfolio q=0.**

`OU-TR5-v1` is the next proposed rulebook identifier, not an approved
strategy. Before a result is viewed, it must freeze T2 membership, selection,
substitutions, weights, rebalancing, cash residual, costs, taxes, order timing,
no-fill handling, distribution accounting and benchmarks.

## Objective boundary

The research objective is observed only if every predeclared complete rolling
five-year out-of-sample cohort has at least 5% annualised **net TWRR**. This is
not a future guarantee, calendar-year floor, probability estimate, account-level
tax conclusion, capacity claim or authorization to invest.

## Non-negotiable data and accounting controls

- Preserve a dated member list and availability timestamp for every decision.
- Use primary records for product/issuer mechanics and an authoritative or
  licensed point-in-time archive for raw market/event data.
- Use raw prices plus a complete event ledger, or a single verified
  total-return series. Never add distributions to a series that already embeds
  them.
- Record each settled distribution once. A later withdrawal is an external
  flow, not a second return.
- Treat halts, limit locks, capacity shortfall and unavailable quotes as
  no-fill/partial-fill states; never manufacture a fill or substitute.

## Scale-neutral boundary

The research replay may express unitized weights and proportional costs. It
cannot establish lot feasibility, minimum fees, market impact, capacity or
holder-specific tax. Those require a separate current feasibility review; no
default amount is inserted into this research protocol.

## Acceptance sequence

1. Versioned rulebook frozen before testing.
2. Hash-bound point-in-time data and event coverage.
3. Correct mechanics, cost/tax/settlement and total-return reconciliation.
4. Full disclosure of every predeclared five-year window, including misses.
5. Independent risk-budget approval.
6. Current access, suitability, liquidity and execution feasibility.

Failure at any stage leaves the portfolio at `q=0`. The public four-ETF static
price screen is a useful falsification result, but it is not a substitute for
this protocol. The existing discrete-lot portfolio engine cannot directly test
the scale-neutral objective; a unitized replay layer and cross-asset event
adapters remain required.
