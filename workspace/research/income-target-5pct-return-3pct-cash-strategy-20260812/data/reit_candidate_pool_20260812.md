# Public REIT Candidate Pool

The candidate pool is deliberately wider than the minimum project-independence screen for any future REIT proposal. It contains one toll-road, one consumer-infrastructure, two rental-housing, one Shanghai rental-housing, one industrial-park and one photovoltaic record. Each is watch-only until current price, TTM cash distribution, premium/discount to NAV, operating data, debt/capex, liquidity, spread, lot and tax fields are captured. This REIT queue is not a required portfolio sleeve and does not restrict the wider T1 cross-asset research universe.

The pool’s historical and announced cash data are evidence of cash mechanisms, not forecasts of total return. Full records are in `data/reit_candidate_pool_20260812.json`.
