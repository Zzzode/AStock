# Portfolio Total-Return Model

Status: **open-universe research framework; implementation not approved**.
The sole objective is a conditional rolling-five-year, annualized, cost-aware
TWRR of 5%. It is not a guarantee, a point forecast or an annual floor. The
framework is scale-neutral: it uses proportional measurement and does not
infer any account-level fact.

Cash distributions are portfolio cash flows, not a second objective. Strategy
performance is TWRR with a subperiod break at every external flow; investor
experience is separately reported as MWRR/XIRR. Receipt eligibility is separate
from cash disposition: the investor may retain, withdraw or take no action
with a receipt, with ledger recording only. A later new market-allocation
proposal requires its own dated review. A retained receipt remains in marked
value, while a withdrawal is an external flow; neither treatment can create a
second return. An issuer-labelled or economically similar capital-repayment
component must be separately flagged, receive predeclared accounting/tax
treatment and is not labelled investment return.

The former gross cash-yield screen and the 10/15/40/25/10 fixed asset-class
vector are retired. Distribution history may be used for product mechanics,
operating analysis, valuation and risk review, but it does not establish a
cash-receipt target, an allocation quota or a selection threshold. Missing
tax, fee or settlement evidence leaves the relevant cash-flow fact unavailable;
it may not be replaced with a headline yield or sale proceeds.

T1 is open to lawful, account-accessible direct equities, ETFs/funds, public
REITs, sovereign instruments and cash tools. T2 is a finite dated decision
universe; T3 is a named item-level approved investable universe; T4 is a
separately approved dynamic portfolio. Open T1 membership is not approval.
Every direct equity requires a current-price bull/base/bear valuation, audited
financial and earnings bridge, governance, cash-flow, liquidity and
portfolio-overlap dossier before T3 consideration. ETF and REIT dossiers use
structure-appropriate modules at the same evidence standard.

There is no approved model allocation or inferred account holding. T2 contains
no frozen direct-equity candidates; T3 and T4 are empty. Formal return
evidence, risk-budget approval, suitability and current-session implementation
records are all blocking gates. No asset class has a pre-approved target weight.
