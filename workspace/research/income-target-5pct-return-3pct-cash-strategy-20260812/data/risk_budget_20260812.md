## Required T2/T3/T4 risk package

Before any non-zero portfolio proposal, freeze a dated cross-asset T2 set and
recalculate common stress, implementation-cost uncertainty, loss capacity and
investment-horizon/liquidity-need evidence. The package must include every proposed direct
equity, ETF/fund, REIT, sovereign and cash tool; its issuer group, industry,
factor, property/rental, regulated-revenue, customer, financing and rate
exposures; and item-level valuation, liquidity, tax and settlement evidence.
Independent risk approval is required before an item can contribute to T4.

No pre-approved asset-class quota, direct-equity cap or REIT aggregate weight
exists in this framework. A concentration limit must be proposed from the
frozen candidate set and accepted with the T4 risk budget. This is stricter
than assigning a number before the assets and their common drivers are known.

## Mandatory risk actions

- At documented external-flow-adjusted \(DD_t\leq-12\%\), pause new risk
  additions pending re-underwriting; any cash disposition remains
  investor-directed and separately recorded.
- At \(DD_t\leq-18\%\), impose a risk stop: no new market-risk proposal,
  cancel unexecuted model tranches and require an incident/de-risk proposal
  before any later re-approval.
- Rebounds, distributions and unfavorable objective outcomes cannot release the stop. These
  controls govern new research allocations; they do not direct a sale or promise
  that losses stop at a threshold.

## REIT and cross-asset controls

If a public REIT enters T2, it must show at least five independent projects,
three manager/sponsor groups and three operating cash-flow sectors before it
can reach T3. Project, manager, sector, tenant/offtaker, refinancing and
cross-sleeve overlap must be mapped. A verified rolling-60-observation
correlation greater than 0.70 creates a common risk cluster; missing history
does not prove independence. The final numerical limits belong to the approved
T4 risk package, never to a product category label.
