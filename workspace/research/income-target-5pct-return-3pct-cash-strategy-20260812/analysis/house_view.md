# House View — Open Research, Closed Evidence Gates

## Decision

**Research architecture: PASS. Investment implementation: NOT APPROVED.**
The earlier product menu and fixed reference vector were useful only as a
first-round evidence queue. Treating them as a permanent restriction would
needlessly exclude direct equities and other lawful products before comparing
their total-return drivers, valuation, risk and liquidity.

The strategy is corrected to an open research universe with a finite,
time-stamped decision universe, a limited approved investable universe and a
dynamically approved portfolio. This preserves the option to research direct
stocks without allowing “open” to become automatic investment permission.

## What the framework seeks

The framework seeks a rolling five-year, cost-aware TWRR objective of 5%.
Cash distributions remain components of total return and are recorded under
the receipt and external-flow rules; they are not a separate target. No
asset-class label, historical distribution rate or old reference weight can
demonstrate the objective.

Direct equities may contribute earnings growth, dividend growth and valuation
re-rating. ETFs may improve diversification or execution. Public REITs can
provide asset-level cash-flow exposure. Sovereign/cash tools can supply
liquidity and investment-purpose resilience. Which exposure is superior can only
be answered after item-specific research and a cross-asset portfolio test.

## Why no portfolio is currently investable

1. **No frozen decision universe:** direct equities are not yet named and the
   ten first-round products are not a portfolio selection.
2. **No return evidence:** no point-in-time, cost/tax/capacity-aware replay
   exists for a frozen cross-asset portfolio or B0/B1 benchmark set.
3. **No risk budget:** the old vector's known -25.00% planning-stress subtotal
   breached the -18% action line. No replacement candidate-level stress and
   loss-capacity package is approved.
4. **No current implementation or suitability packet:** current price,
   valuation, liquidity, account, tax, settlement, concentration and
   investment-purpose facts are not complete.

## Decision rule

Every actual holding must first pass structure-appropriate R0–R4 diligence and
independent evidence, valuation, risk, liquidity and operating/tax review.
Passing an item places it only in T3. A T4 portfolio then needs a separate
cross-asset comparison of incremental total-return drivers, valuation, overlap,
stress, concentration, capacity and suitability. Cash may remain the correct
selection.

The current action is no model market allocation and no inferred account
holding. That state does not claim to meet the total-return objective or direct
the use of any cash receipt.
