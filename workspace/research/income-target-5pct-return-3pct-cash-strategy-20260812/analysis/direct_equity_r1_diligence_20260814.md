## 000333 — 美的集团

### Business / earnings driver evidence

- The FY2025 annual report describes a consumer-and-business matrix: smart home, industrial technology, building technology, robotics/automation, new energy, healthcare and smart logistics. FY2025 revenue was RMB456.452bn (+12.11%), attributable profit RMB43.945bn (+14.03%), and profit excluding non-recurring items RMB41.267bn (+15.46%) (annual report pp. 8, 11-12).
- The issuer reports overseas revenue of RMB195.9bn (+16%) and ToB revenue of RMB122.8bn (+17.5%) for FY2025. These are issuer-reported business drivers, not independently benchmarked market-share claims (annual report foreword pp. 1-2).
- Q1 2026 revenue was RMB131.099bn (+2.55%), attributable profit RMB12.675bn (+2.03%), and profit excluding non-recurring items RMB10.962bn (-14.02%). Q1 building technology, robotics/automation and industrial technology revenue were RMB10.8bn (+10.1%), RMB8.2bn (+11.8%) and RMB6.8bn (-11.7%), respectively (Q1 report pp. 2-3).

### Quality, cash flow and balance-sheet evidence

- FY2025 operating cash flow was RMB53.346bn, down 11.84% year on year, versus RMB43.945bn attributable profit. This is a cash-conversion item to reconcile by segment, working capital and investment flows; it is not a free-cash-flow result (annual report p. 8).
- FY2025 year-end cash was RMB85.247bn, short-term borrowings RMB43.905bn and long-term borrowings RMB12.659bn; reported asset-liability ratio was 61.17%. These gross balance-sheet captions do not establish net debt, debt capacity or a valuation conclusion without maturity, restricted-cash, lease and acquisition analysis (annual report pp. 49-50, 109).
- The FY2025 audit opinion is reported as standard unqualified in the annual report.

### Sector, catalysts and risks

- Sector framing is multi-driver rather than a single appliance cycle: consumer demand, building investment, industrial/robotics orders, overseas localisation, FX/tariff exposure and supply-chain execution must be separated. The current archive has no primary competitor peer set, so no relative competitive conclusion is permitted.
- R1 monitoring variables are reported overseas/ToB and the three Q1 technology segments; adjusted-versus-reported profit; cash conversion and receivables/inventory; and disclosed acquisition integration. They are monitoring inputs, not catalysts supporting a valuation outcome.
- Key diligence risks: slower consumer/building demand, overseas tariff/FX/localisation execution, technology-segment cyclicality, working-capital reversal, financial-asset gains/non-recurring reconciliation, leverage and integration. The FY2026 Q1 adjusted-profit divergence is a required bridge, not evidence of a trend.

### Open gate

Build a segment/geography margin and cash-conversion bridge; collect official peer/sector data; reconcile non-recurring financial/investment items; review governance, related parties and post-Q1 announcements; then attach a dated market packet before any valuation work.

## 600900 — 长江电力

### Business / earnings driver evidence

- The issuer describes itself as a large hydropower operator with 71.795GW controllable hydro capacity at FY2025 end, including 71.695GW in China. Its six domestic cascade stations generated 307.194TWh in FY2025, +3.82% year on year (annual report pp. 9-10, 18).
- FY2025 revenue was RMB86.242bn (+2.07%), attributable profit RMB34.503bn (+6.17%) and profit excluding non-recurring items RMB33.446bn (+2.89%). Domestic hydropower revenue was RMB75.662bn, +1.59%, with 65.79% gross margin. Reported average grid tariff was RMB280.28/MWh (annual report pp. 12, 16-18).
- Q1 2026 revenue was RMB18.112bn (+6.44%), attributable profit RMB6.761bn (+30.50%) and profit excluding non-recurring items RMB6.237bn (+19.20%). The reported/adjusted growth gap needs an item-level bridge before any run-rate inference (Q1 report pp. 2-3).

### Quality, cash flow, capital and leverage evidence

- FY2025 operating cash flow was RMB60.563bn (+1.53%); fixed-asset investment was RMB9.443bn, primarily RMB9.368bn of construction investment. These are reported cash/capex items, not a distributable-cash or total-return measure (annual report pp. 12, 18-19).
- The reported asset-liability ratio was 58.27%, and financial expense fell 15.81% to RMB9.371bn. Year-end short-term borrowings were RMB15.181bn after repayment of maturing debt; a complete maturity, fixed/floating-rate, covenant and acquisition-funding bridge is still absent (annual report pp. 12, 17, 72).
- The FY2025 audit opinion is reported as standard unqualified in the annual report.

### Sector, catalysts and risks

- The required causal chain is **hydrology → dispatch → generation/sales volume → tariff/market trading → operating cost/financial cost → earnings/cash flow**. Neither historic generation nor cash flow can stand in for this bridge.
- Monitorable R1 variables are basin inflows, dispatch and utilisation, tariff/market-trading mix, pumped-storage construction/approval, debt-cost path and disclosed acquisitions. The archive does not contain a primary peer comparison, so no relative sector-quality conclusion is permitted.
- Key diligence risks: hydrological variability; power-market/tariff and curtailment exposure; outage/safety; construction/capex execution; debt and acquisition funding; and governance/related-party mechanics. The issuer’s strategic/policy discussion is context, not a return forecast.

### Open gate

Reconcile Q1 non-recurring items, obtain hydrology/dispatch/tariff operating data through the latest disclosure, map all debt/capex/acquisition commitments and related-party arrangements, assemble primary peer/policy evidence, and attach a dated market packet before valuation.

## 600036 — 招商银行

### Business / earnings driver evidence

- FY2025 net operating income was RMB337.273bn (+0.05%) and attributable profit RMB150.181bn (+1.21%). Net interest margin was 1.87%, down 11bp; net fee/non-interest income represented 36.08% of operating income. The report describes retail finance, corporate finance, investment/financial markets and wealth/asset management as the main business segments (FY2025 annual results pp. 16-18, 22-23).
- Q1 2026 net operating income was RMB86.952bn (+3.85%) and attributable profit RMB37.852bn (+1.52%). Net interest income increased 4.99% to RMB55.642bn but NIM declined 8bp year on year to 1.83%; the bank attributes margin pressure mainly to loan repricing, insufficient effective credit demand and lower retail-loan mix (Q1 pp. 3, 10).
- Q1 fee and commission income was RMB20.656bn (+4.87%); wealth-management fee income was RMB8.507bn (+25.42%). Retail loans were RMB3,683.040bn (-1.00% from 2025 year end) while corporate loans were RMB3,440.173bn (+6.98%) (Q1 pp. 11-12).

### Quality, capital and funding evidence

- At 2026-03-31, NPL ratio was 0.94%, allowance coverage 387.76% and allowance-to-loan ratio 3.63%; NPL balance increased RMB1.652bn from year end. FY2025 CET1 ratio was 14.16%, down 70bp year on year; FY2025 credit-cost ratio was 0.60% (FY2025 pp. 17-18; Q1 p. 9).
- Q1 customer deposits were RMB9.959tn (+1.25% from year end), 81.67% of total liabilities. This is funding evidence, not a liquidity or capital stress pass (Q1 pp. 12-13).
- Q1 real-estate credit-risk exposures were RMB347.935bn; property-development loans were RMB283.708bn (4.00% of loans) and the disclosed property-development NPL ratio was 4.44%. These facts require granular vintage, collateral, provisioning and scenario work rather than being netted against group-level NPL/coverage ratios (Q1 pp. 14-15).
- The FY2025 H-share annual report says both PRC Accounting Standards and IFRS accounts received standard unqualified audit reports. The captured Q1 report is unaudited and states that the Chinese version prevails in case of discrepancy; the Chinese A-share filing has not been captured, so this disclosure set cannot complete R1 verification.

### Sector, catalysts and risks

- The correct model architecture is **asset yield and funding cost → NIM → loan mix/fees → credit costs/provisions → capital/ROTE**. Manufacturing revenue/FCF shortcuts are inapplicable.
- Monitorable R1 variables are NIM and loan/deposit mix, wealth/fee revenue, credit cost, property/retail asset quality, allowance coverage and CET1. They are not buy/sell catalysts.
- Key diligence risks: further margin compression; weak retail and housing demand; property and local-government/wholesale credit risk; fee-income sensitivity; provisioning/capital pressure; regulation and funding competition. No official peer data were captured, so relative franchise or competitive claims remain open.

### Open gate

Capture and reconcile the Chinese A-share FY2025/Q1 disclosures; build a granular NIM–credit–provision–capital/ROTE bridge including property and retail stress; review governance, related parties and subsequent announcements; assemble official peer evidence; then attach a dated market packet before any PB–ROTE–cost-of-equity valuation work.
