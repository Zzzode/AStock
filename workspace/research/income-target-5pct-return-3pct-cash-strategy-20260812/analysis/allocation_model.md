# Allocation Architecture — Open Universe, No Pre-Allocated Product Menu

## Decision

The former 10% / 15% / 40% / 25% / 10% vector is retired as a prospective
allocation. It was a first-round product-research design, not a validated
portfolio. Its known -25.00% planning-stress subtotal exceeds the -18% action
boundary before an undefined sleeve is assigned a stress; it cannot serve as
either a live allocation or an asset-class quota.

The current allocation state is therefore:

| State | Eligible protected liquidity | Approved investable universe | Approved portfolio | Meaning |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Current | Notional reference only; no account holding inferred | Empty | Empty | No approved model allocation; the framework makes no cash-use instruction. |
| Future review | Determined only after a portfolio risk-budget approval | Named T3 securities/products only | Dynamic T4 selection only | No fixed asset-class allocation, target weight or fill requirement is pre-approved. |

## Open-universe architecture

| Tier | Purpose | Permission |
|---|---|---|
| T0: research boundary | Defines legal/account/tax/settlement/liquidity-accessible formats. | Research only. |
| T1: open research universe | Receives nominations across direct equities, ETFs/funds, public REITs, sovereign and cash products. | Every item q=0. |
| T2: decision universe | Freezes a finite, dated cross-asset comparison set. | Research only. |
| T3: approved investable universe | Holds named items with complete structure-appropriate R0–R4 research and independent checks. | May enter portfolio review. |
| T4: approved portfolio | Selects from T3 based on marginal total-return drivers, valuation, risk, overlap, capacity and liquidity. | Requires separate portfolio approval. |

The ten documented ETF/REIT products are a completed first-round T2 queue.
They do not receive a structural advantage over a future direct equity or
other lawful candidate. Conversely, a direct equity does not receive a
structural advantage merely because it offers a stated dividend yield.

## Required item dossier

Every proposed non-zero exposure must have an evidence package appropriate to
its instrument:

- **Direct equity:** audited financials, company and industry evidence,
  revenue-to-EPS bridge, governance/capital allocation, current-price
  bull/base/bear valuation, dividend/free-cash-flow bridge, catalyst and
  invalidation, A-share float/suspension/price-limit/liquidity/capacity.
- **ETF/fund:** legal mandate/index, full holding look-through, issuer/sector
  overlap, NAV/IOPV or reference-value method, tracking and distribution
  mechanics, material derivatives/securities lending, cost and execution
  capacity.
- **Public REIT:** asset/project, tenant/offtaker, operating KPI, cash
  distribution bridge, debt/refinancing/capex, NAV/asset-value timing,
  governance and liquidity.
- **Sovereign/cash product:** issuer/protection/term/withdrawal mechanics,
  cash-flow convention, tax, account and concentration evidence.

No generic “asset-class approval” substitutes for an item dossier. The
appropriate format can differ; the evidence and decision standard cannot.

## Portfolio approval

T3 approval is necessary but insufficient. The T4 record must compare all
eligible candidates on:

1. incremental total-return drivers and a reproducible valuation/expectations
   bridge;
2. tax-, cost- and settlement-aware total-return attribution, including distribution accounting;
3. common issuer-group, industry, factor, customer/tenant/offtaker, financing,
   region/regulatory and rate exposures;
4. marginal stress loss, concentration, correlation, liquidity and capacity;
5. account, settlement, tax, investment-horizon and liquidity-need suitability; and
6. monitoring triggers, invalidation and review expiry.

There is no requirement to allocate to every asset class, use all approved
names, or take any action after an unfavorable objective outcome. A failed or
inferior candidate does not create a model allocation or a cash-use instruction.

## Cash-flow accounting boundary

The former gross-yield buffer is retired. Cash distributions are reconciled as
components of total return, not used as a cash-coverage test or an allocation
screen. For each settled interest payment, dividend, ETF distribution or REIT
distribution, retain the payment date, source, gross amount, tax, receipt fee,
net amount, settlement date and source evidence. Sale proceeds, unrealized
gains, NAV accretion, external contributions and unsettled receivables are not
cash distributions. An issuer-labelled or economically similar capital-
repayment component must be separately flagged and receive a predeclared
accounting/tax treatment before it can enter investment-performance
attribution.

Distribution history may inform product mechanics, operating review, valuation
and downside analysis. It does not establish a separate target, a selection
threshold, a mandatory REIT allocation or a forward return estimate.

## Fallback

Any failed or missing item or portfolio gate leaves the candidate at `q=0`; it
does not infer an account holding. It may not be redirected automatically to a
higher-yield issuer, correlated ETF, direct stock, lower-credit product or
catch-up allocation.
