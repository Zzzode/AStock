# R0 Primary-Source Diligence: Public REIT Candidates

**Status:** evidence memo only; research-only / q=0. No allocation, recommendation, order, automatic reinvestment, or cash-income target is authorised.

**Cut-off:** 2026-08-14. The sole conditional/ex-post objective is a rolling five-year, cost-aware investor TWRR of at least 5%. The test cannot be observed for any of the three funds at this cut-off because each has less than five years of listed history.

## Boundary: cash disclosure is not market-price return

The annual reports provide audited fund/project accounting, distributable cash and appraisal information. A distribution changes NAV around the ex-date and says nothing by itself about the market price paid or received. Therefore these items must remain separate:

| Evidence item | Permitted use at R0 | Not permitted |
|---|---|---|
| Distributable amount / actual cash distribution | Describe fund cash-distribution mechanics and historical reporting | Treat as yield, target cash income, or investor TWRR |
| Unit NAV / appraisal | Identify the dated accounting/appraisal reference and model assumptions | Treat as current executable value or a market price |
| Reported cash-flow distribution rate | Identify the fund’s period-end market-capitalisation statistic | Substitute for total return, forward return, or a cost-aware result |
| Secondary-market price and transaction data | Not captured in this R0 pack | Infer from cash, NAV, fund IRR illustration, or an unaudited operating notice |

## Evidence by ticker

### 508069 — 华夏南京交通高速公路REIT

- The audited FY2025 report records 500.0m units, NAV of RMB 4.8970/unit, distributable amount RMB 298.65m (RMB 0.5973/unit) and actual distribution RMB 0.7718/unit (report pp. 8-10).
- The manager’s FY2025 cash-flow distribution-rate statistic is 9.19%; its definition is annual distributable amount divided by report-end market capitalisation. This is not an investor return measure (p. 8).
- The income-approach appraisal at 2025-12-31 is RMB 2.705bn. The report says the road project’s revenue met 99.12% and net cash flow met 103.81% of appraisal forecast (pp. 99-101).
- R0 risk inputs: the appraisal changes whole-life toll assumptions for regional development and road-network change; it also reflects a lower debt cost / changed capital structure. The project reported a bank-loan balance of RMB 286.8m at year-end (pp. 99-101; annual report debt disclosure pp. 16-17).

**T2 requirement:** re-underwrite traffic, toll, competing-network, capex reserve and debt-service assumptions; capture current price, float/lock-up and executable liquidity separately. The FY2025 annual report is stale at the August cut-off; the manager’s product list shows a 2026 Q2 report, which must be archived before T2.

### 508068 — 华夏北京保障房REIT

- The audited FY2025 report records 724.218m units, NAV of RMB 2.9927/unit, distributable amount RMB 74.82m (RMB 0.1033/unit) and actual distribution RMB 0.0940/unit (pp. 10-12). The year includes a June 2025 follow-on offering; unit comparisons need an explicit pre/post-offering bridge.
- Its reported FY2025 cash-flow distribution-rate statistic is 2.53%, which the annual report defines using year-end market capitalisation (p. 10); it is not a market-price total return.
- The six rental-housing projects’ income-approach appraisal totals RMB 2.024bn at 2025-12-31. Actual FY2025 aggregate revenue and operating net cash flow were 103.68% and 106.63% of forecast respectively (pp. 119-122).
- R0 risk inputs are disclosure-specific: the appraisal changed assumed rent adjustment from every three years to every five; 2026 assumed occupancy at Shengyue was reduced from 90% to 88%; Langyue collection rate fell to 93.21%, driving lower 2026-27 assumptions (pp. 119-121). These are assumptions/risk flags, not price conclusions.

**T2 requirement:** capture the 2026 Q2 report and current rent, occupancy, collection, policy/approval and post-offering per-unit bridge; separately capture current market access and price/NAV relationship.

### 508096 — 中航京能国际能源REIT (formerly 中航京能光伏REIT)

- The audited FY2025 report, after the 2025 expansion and February 2026 name change, records 600.880m units, NAV RMB 9.1720/unit, fair-value reference NAV RMB 8.1264/unit, distributable amount RMB 323.36m (RMB 0.5381/unit) and actual distribution RMB 0.2372/unit (pp. 11-13). Its 5.33% reported cash-flow distribution-rate statistic is not TWRR.
- At the 2025-12-31 appraisal date, the project values were RMB 382m for Jingtai PV, RMB 1.508bn for Yulin PV and RMB 2.465bn for Lianghe hydropower (pp. 131-132). Jingtai’s value declined 15.86% year on year, with the report citing shorter remaining operating life and reduced forecast revenue; the legacy photovoltaic projects’ revenue and net cash flow met only 78.36% and 78.60% of forecast (pp. 132-134).
- The 2026-07-02 SSE project-operation notice is unaudited and uses an estimated June settlement. It reports Q2 Jingtai settlement electricity down 33.96% YoY and settlement fee down 24.95% YoY. The notice attributes this to weaker solar resource, supply/demand imbalance and export constraints, while noting the values can change with final settlement (pp. 1-5).

**T2 requirement:** obtain the next audited/periodic cash-flow bridge and appraisal, reconcile the Q2 preliminary settlement, separately refresh electricity-price, curtailment/export, hydrology, subsidy-receivable and working-capital data, then capture current price, book depth, float/lock-ups and costs. The annual report’s 2025-12-31 close-price IRR illustration is historical and assumption-bound; it cannot be used as an August-2026 valuation input.

## Exact common blockers before T2 valuation/risk/market-access work

1. **No current price reference:** no official timestamped close at 2026-08-14 is archived for any ticker.
2. **No price-to-NAV bridge:** no current/unit NAV or valuation update paired with the same-date price is in this evidence pack. Premium/discount to NAV, implied cash-flow multiple and price sensitivity are therefore uncomputed.
3. **No executable-liquidity packet:** bid/ask, depth, 20-day average turnover/volume, free float, lock-up release schedule, trading unit, settlement constraints and actual broker/exchange fees are absent. Market access and cost-aware return are not assessable.
4. **No adjusted investor-return series:** there is no daily adjusted market-price series tied to every ex-date and cash-payment date, nor transaction costs. Cash distribution, unit NAV and the managers’ cash-flow distribution rates cannot be substituted.
5. **Insufficient observed history:** listing dates are 2024-11-01 (508069), 2022-08-31 (508068), and 2023-03-29 (508096); no candidate can demonstrate a rolling five-year realised TWRR as of the cut-off.
6. **Financial freshness:** 508069 and 508068 are FY2025-only within this bounded archive even though their managers list 2026 Q2 reports. 508096’s July operating notice is preliminary, unaudited and not a substitute for its next financial/valuation disclosure.
