# Valuation Audit

## Scope and result

This report does not make an investable security-level valuation call. Current price, market capitalization, issuer forecasts, broker targets and fair-value ranges are intentionally absent because the evidence packet does not contain them. The action for each market-traded fund is therefore `watchlist only / q=0`, not a buy or sell instruction.

## Audit checks

- Price/share reconciliation: not applicable to allocation sleeves; no current price conclusion is made.
- Target/upside arithmetic: not applicable; no targets are published.
- Distribution-accounting arithmetic: the former uncalibrated gross-yield screen is retired. Settled distributions must reconcile to holder taxes, receipt fees, settlement dates and the total-return series. Distribution history may inform mechanics, valuation and risk work, but it is not a target, selection threshold or a second return; subsequent cash disposition does not create a second return.
- Cash-flow mechanism: 90% REIT distribution rule, ETF conditional distribution and historical notices are documented but do not establish forward cash yields.
- Fake-precision check: pass. No historical distribution is converted to a live yield, target price or future total return.
- Publication action boundary: pass. The report labels every traded sleeve q=0 until current validation.

Model Reproducibility: PASS (mechanical research framework only)

This PASS means that the disclosed conditional allocation, accounting and blocking gates reproduce. It does **not** certify historical strategy performance, future returns, cash distributions, implementation eligibility or investor suitability.
