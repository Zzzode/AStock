# Template Brief

Selected report archetype: institutional strategic-allocation memo with a total-return objective scorecard. This is not an industry-chain, single-stock target-price or broker-consensus report.

Reader question: can an A-share-accessible, scale-neutral multi-asset framework be responsibly researched against a 5% long-horizon total-return objective, and what is permitted today?

Required answer: distinguish the literal request from the conditional rolling research objective; lead with current action and implementation status; identify the cross-asset candidate pool; state scale neutrality, TWRR/MWRR and receipt-versus-disposition accounting, return-evidence/benchmark gate, eligibility gates, risk escalation and objective-outcome protocol.
