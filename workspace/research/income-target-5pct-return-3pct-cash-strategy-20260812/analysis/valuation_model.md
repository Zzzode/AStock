## Three-Tier Targets

Not applicable. Publishing numeric targets without current price and business-model evidence would create false precision. The strategy uses item-level eligibility and portfolio-risk gates rather than pre-set asset-class weights or security price targets.

## Relative / PEG / PSG Comparison

Not currently applicable because no direct-equity issuer has been frozen in T2. Once an issuer is named, the report must apply a company-appropriate PE/PB/DCF/SOTP or other reproducible current-price method, with audited financial history, revenue-to-EPS bridge, bull/base/bear cases, catalysts, invalidation and cross-asset comparison. This is a temporary evidence state, not an exclusion of operating-company candidates.

## Seasonality Calibration

REIT quarterly distributions may contain seasonal operating effects, project timing and reserve changes. The strategy therefore prohibits converting a single quarter into a full-year distribution yield. Only a reconciled TTM distribution record can inform product-mechanics, valuation and risk review.

## Next-Quarter Threshold

For a REIT candidate, the next review must confirm actual distribution, operating KPI, debt/capex reserve, liquidity and price/NAV fields. For 510880, it must confirm actual TTM distribution and holdings overlap. Without these, q remains 0.

## Method and Assumption Bridge

The applicable method is not a target-price model. Actual distributions establish a receipt history; current price/NAV and operating data determine whether that history is relevant to product mechanics, valuation and risk review. It is not a cash-budget screen and cannot infer a 5% total return.

## Market-Expectation Valuation Bridge

Not published because current prices, trading values, premium/discounts and market-implied cash expectations are missing. A future implementation packet must distinguish market price from NAV and must not treat a higher distribution rate as proof of a better total-return opportunity.

## Broker/Street Comparison

Not currently applicable because no direct-equity issuer is in T2/T3. A future named direct-equity dossier must audit available broker/Street evidence or document its absence, distinguish it from the house view and never use a broker target as an automatic allocation input. Its code must reconcile one-for-one across the T3 registry, the dated valuation record and the broker/Street record; the declared T3 issuer count must equal that reconciled list.
