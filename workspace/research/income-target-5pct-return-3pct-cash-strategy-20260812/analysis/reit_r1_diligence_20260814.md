## 508069 — 华夏南京交通高速公路REIT

### Asset and project economics

- The single asset is the Nanjing Ring Expressway Southeast Section, operated as a traffic-infrastructure project. Revenue is principally vehicle tolls, with a minor rental component. FY2025 toll income including tax was RMB503.142m, split between passenger RMB244.886m and freight RMB258.346m (FY2025 annual report pp. 12-13).
- The FY2025 appraisal comparison records project revenue of RMB488.997m, 99.12% of forecast, and operating net cash flow of RMB347.396m, 103.81% of forecast (pp. 100-101). These are appraiser/accounting-period observations—not investor total return or an estimate of future distribution.
- Asset economics need a formal traffic-volume, vehicle-mix, toll-policy, network competition, maintenance and concession-life bridge. The annual report identifies road-network changes and the cost of debt/capital structure as valuation inputs, but the concession contract, tariff schedule and remaining-term legal evidence are not archived (pp. 99-100).

### Manager, governance, capital and contract evidence

- Disclosed roles: 华夏基金 is fund manager; 招商银行 is custodian; 中信证券 is asset-backed-securities manager; 南京公路发展（集团） is the operating-management coordinator and the Nanjing Ring Expressway Southeast company is the implementation operator (pp. 6-7). Role disclosure is not a governance-quality finding.
- The report says the manager supervises and assesses the operating manager under the operating-management service agreement; it describes related-party transactions as normal-business commercial terms. A full related-party, fee, conflict and approval matrix remains required (pp. 12, 13-14).
- At FY2025 end the project-company bank-loan principal balance was RMB286.8m; the original RMB300m fixed-asset loan runs from 2024-10-25 to 2031-10-22, is linked to LPR less 94bp, and is secured by a pledge of toll rights. The disclosed annual principal schedule must be reconciled to the full project/fund liability stack, interest-rate risk and cash waterfall. The report also identifies shareholder-loan and bank-loan liabilities following the SPV merger (pp. 16-18).
- FY2025 cash outflows include RMB409.456m of shareholder-loan interest and RMB3.369m capex. These entries require transaction-level reconciliation; they cannot be used to infer recurring cash available to investors (p. 17).

### Risks, monitoring and invalidation evidence

- Monitor factual inputs: traffic volume/mix, toll-policy changes, road-network additions, maintenance/capex, loan-service waterfall and appraisal-driver updates.
- Invalidation/review triggers for a future thesis must be set only after the toll concession, tariff/policy, traffic and debt-covenant source documents are archived. Before then, no price, return or suitability conclusion is permitted.
- Main uncovered risks: concession/fee-policy enforceability, traffic diversion, toll discounts, construction/maintenance, debt-service priority, related-party incentives and secondary-market liquidity.

## 508068 — 华夏北京保障房REIT

### Asset and project economics

- The fund holds six public-rental-housing assets through two project companies: 4,264 rentable units and 241,509.97 sqm at FY2025 end. Reported occupancy was 94.45% and aggregate rent collection was 98.53%; the 2024 comparison is not like-for-like because four assets were added through the follow-on offering (FY2025 annual report pp. 15-16).
- Public-rental rents are government-approved. Reported rent levels were RMB35–65/sqm/month across the assets, and individual leases are generally three years. This makes rent-policy/eligibility/tenant-allocation evidence central to economics; it is not comparable to an unrestricted market-rent asset (pp. 15-16).
- The FY2025 appraisal comparison reports aggregate project revenue of RMB103.133m (103.68% of forecast) and operating net cash flow of RMB84.067m (106.63% of forecast) (pp. 121-122). The fund’s FY2025 distributable amount and cash distribution remain accounting facts, not a price-return input.

### Manager, governance, capital and tenant-contract evidence

- Disclosed roles: 华夏基金 is fund manager, China Construction Bank custodian and 中信证券 asset-backed-securities manager; the operating managers are 北京保障房中心, 北京市燕房保障性住房建设投资 and 北京市燕东保障性住房建设投资 (pp. 6-9). Governance quality is not established by these role disclosures.
- FY2025 fees disclosed include RMB1.739m fund-management fee, RMB1.348m ABS-management fee, RMB0.176m custody fee, RMB18.783m operating-management fee and RMB0.298m incentive fee. Related-party transactions are described as commercial terms, but an R1 conflicts/fee/approval and sponsor-support matrix has not been built (pp. 13-14).
- The 2025 purchase-price settlement for added assets was RMB943.540m; the acquired projects had total assets RMB766.800m and total liabilities RMB35.013m at the disclosed transaction date. No complete current project/fund debt maturity, rate, collateral, debt-service or expansion-financing bridge is in this R1 record (p. 14).
- Rent collection and tenant quality need a project-by-project approach: report-end receivables gross balance was RMB2.854m with RMB1.158m loss allowance (40.57% expected-loss rate) because certain tenants were long overdue. Tenant mix also differs materially—for example, Shengyue had 72.42% bulk-leased area—so aggregate occupancy is insufficient (pp. 14-16).

### Risks, monitoring and invalidation evidence

- Monitor factual inputs: government rent-adjustment/approval notices, allocation policy, project-level occupancy, collection/arrears, bulk-tenant concentration, lease expiry/replacement and capex/maintenance reserve.
- A future thesis must specify policy, rent collection, occupancy and funding invalidation thresholds only after full lease/tenant, rent-approval and debt-source files are captured. No such thresholds are asserted here.
- Main uncovered risks: government pricing and allocation policy, limited rent reset flexibility, collection/arrears, bulk-tenant concentration, renewal/turnover, expansion comparability, related-party operation and secondary-market liquidity.

## 508096 — 中航京能国际能源REIT (formerly 中航京能光伏REIT)

### Asset and project economics

- The fund held two photovoltaic and two hydropower assets after the 2025 expansion. The PV projects reached full-grid connection in 2015 and 2017 and have disclosed design lives of 25 years; the two hydropower projects reached full-grid connection in 2011 and have disclosed design lives of 40 years (FY2025 annual report pp. 19-20).
- The FY2025 legacy-PV appraisal comparison reports revenue of RMB301.004m versus RMB384.128m forecast (78.36%) and net cash flow RMB241.723m versus RMB307.554m (78.60%). The report links variance to operating performance below expectation and working-capital additions; the new hydropower assets were only consolidated for the final days of 2025, so they cannot support a full-year comparison (pp. 133-134).
- The official 2026-07-02 project notice is preliminary/unaudited and uses an estimated June bill. It reports Q2 Jingtai PV settlement electricity down 33.96% year on year and settlement fee down 24.95%, while Yulin PV settlement electricity rose 28.69%. Resource conditions, regional supply/demand, dispatch/export constraints and settlement price are the relevant driver set (Q2 notice pp. 1-5).

### Manager, governance, capital and offtaker/contract evidence

- Disclosed roles: 中航基金 is fund manager; 华夏银行 custodian; 中航证券 ABS manager. 内蒙古京能新能源科技 manages the PV assets, while 保山能源发展 and 保山腾冲保能和顺 manage the hydropower assets. The manager reports supervision and performance assessment under operating-management agreements (annual report pp. 7-10, 19-20).
- Related-party records include operating management, 330kV/110kV transmission-line lease arrangements with original-rights-holder affiliates, manager/custodian fees and receivable-factoring finance from the custodian. These disclosures are evidence of related arrangements, not a finding that risks are mitigated. Full contracts, pricing, concentration, termination/renewal, grid/offtaker and approval evidence has not been captured (pp. 17-18).
- At FY2025 end, receivable-factoring principal was RMB335.154m with RMB7.914m accrued interest; FY2025 new factoring borrowing was RMB255.868m, repayment RMB143.977m and factoring interest RMB7.934m. The fund also reserved RMB16.597m for capex and RMB110.351m for year-end operating liabilities in distributable-cash adjustments. - The annual report’s project appraisals and NAV/fair-value-reference-NAV are dated 2025-12-31 only. They cannot be paired with an August 2026 price; no current premium/discount or implied cash-flow valuation is calculated here.

### Risks, monitoring and invalidation evidence

- Monitor factual inputs: irradiation/inflow, generation, curtailment/export capacity, marketised power-price/settlement, subsidy receivables, transmission-line arrangements, hydropower seasonal performance, capex and factoring balance.
- A future thesis must set cash-flow and contract counterparty invalidation conditions only after current quarterly financials, electricity contracts/settlements, receivable ageing and debt terms are archived. No thresholds or valuation conclusion is stated here.
