# Template Benchmark

- **Archetype:** single-stock institutional equity research note.
- **Benchmark:** JPM equity research single-name format, adapted to Chinese A-share disclosure boundaries.
- **First-page dashboard:** current price, fundamental target, market-adjusted target, scenario range, action, H1 earnings bridge and evidence quality.
- **Chapter sequence:** investment committee summary; evidence and financial base; chain and competitive position; growth and earnings bridge; valuation and target price; secondary market and capital behavior; risks, catalysts and action; evidence/model appendices.
- **Required exhibits:** confirmed-fact boundary table, product mix and margin table, chain role map, earnings bridge, scenario valuation, capital-layer table, monitoring thresholds and source audit.
- **What is avoided:** table-only recommendations, unsupported named customers, demand-anchor-to-revenue conversion, generic AI multiples, internal-house rows presented as Street consensus, and seat-name identity claims without evidence.
- **Research scope:** custom single-stock communications/cable/safety-system map. The case uses a product/customer map and explicitly records unavailable, satellite and demand-anchor nodes.
