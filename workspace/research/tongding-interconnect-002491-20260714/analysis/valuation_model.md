## Relative / PEG / PSG Comparison

PEG/PSG is not used because 2026E is a recovery denominator and the company has no disclosed segment growth rate, ASP, utilization or customer allocation. The valid relative check is the blended cyclical PE/PB/PS method and current-price implied PE.

## Seasonality Calibration

The model does not annualize Q1. H1 official guidance is the starting point; H2 is separately modeled at CNY1.10/2.40/3.70 hundred million adjusted profit in bear/base/bull. This avoids treating the Q1 fair-value loss as recurring seasonality.

## Method and Assumption Bridge

Primary method: adjusted PE on consolidated recovery earnings. Secondary checks: parent PB and conservative revenue PS. The main assumption is H2 adjusted profit; the main catalyst is formal H1 confirmation and positive cash conversion; the invalidation is profit or cash-flow failure.

## Market-Expectation Valuation Bridge

The price pays for H1 delivery, H2 optical price/volume continuation, safety-business contribution and an AI/data-center option. Only the first three have company-level evidence; the option is not capitalized.

## Market-Implied Sentiment Anchor

The observable market anchor is CNY18.50 with a 10% weight. The fundamental anchor is CNY9.16 with a 90% weight. The final target is CNY10.09. The sentiment premium is explicit rather than hidden in the model.

## Growth Earnings Dependency

The model gives earnings credit to the official company-level recovery and optionality credit to AI/data-center exposure. It does not apply a high-growth multiple because the base/growth split, ASP, recognized revenue ratio, utilization, customer and order bridge are not disclosed.

## Full-Chain Classification Dependency

The company is the core listed valuation node in the product/customer map; operators, power-grid investment and data-center capex are demand anchors, and safety subsidiaries are satellite earnings exposure. Missing customer/order fields cap the valuation method at a cyclical blend.
