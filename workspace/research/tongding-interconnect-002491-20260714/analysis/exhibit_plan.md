# Exhibit Plan

| Exhibit | Purpose | Reader decision |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | price, targets, H1 bridge and action | whether to chase or wait |
| Evidence boundary | separate disclosure from inference | what can enter the model |
| Product mix | show diversified profit pools | whether pure-optical valuation fits |
| Chain map | show upstream, midstream, downstream and demand anchors | where evidence is missing |
| Earnings bridge | map H1 guidance to H2 assumptions | what must be delivered |
| Scenario valuation | show target-price math | risk/reward at current price |
| Capital layers | distinguish funds, seats, financing and control | institution or hot-money classification |
| Monitoring table | catalysts and invalidations | what to check next |
