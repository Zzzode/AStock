# Risk Framework

## Core risks

1. Optical-fiber price and volume recovery may be cyclical rather than durable.
2. Q1 operating cash flow remained negative while receivables and inventory were large.
3. YunChuang Data fair-value changes can widen the gap between parent and adjusted profit.
4. Customer, order, ASP, utilization, certification and delivery evidence is incomplete.
5. Short-term debt exceeds cash and the controlling shareholder has pledged shares.
6. Long-established cable competitors can pressure price and gross margin.
7. High turnover, financing leverage and event-driven seat activity can accelerate drawdown.

## Invalidation

- H1 adjusted NP below CNY2.30 hundred million.
- Q2 adjusted NP below CNY1.27 hundred million without a documented seasonality explanation.
- Operating cash flow deteriorates while earnings rise.
- Optical-fiber gross margin or inventory worsens without price/order explanation.
- Current price breaks the CNY18.50 sentiment anchor while profit expectations are revised down.

## Risk budget

The report uses `event-after pullback validation`, not an unconditional buy label. New exposure should wait for either a price reset toward the behavioral anchor or official evidence that closes the earnings/cash/segment gaps.
