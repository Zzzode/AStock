# Source Exhaustion and Implementation Gaps

This case has exhausted the available primary routine disclosures needed to construct source-indexed product cards, but it has **not** exhausted the evidence required for a live allocation. The following gaps are material and intentionally remain blockers:

1. Same-session ETF NAV/IOPV and creation/redemption fields.
2. Same-session REIT asset valuation/NAV bridges after asset-base changes.
3. Bid–ask, depth, market-maker, free-float and multi-session capacity evidence.
4. Investor-specific tax, settlement, account, custody, capital-purpose and suitability facts.
5. Original broker reports or auditable target/forecast consensus for every named product.
6. A point-in-time strategy replay with total-return, cash, costs, taxes, capacity and benchmarks.
7. An exact protected-liquidity product and an exact defensive-diversifier product.

The broker collection performed separate search and access probes. Unavailable or weak sources are retained with zero valuation weight; they cannot become a Street consensus by repetition. See [source_exhaustion_log.json](source_exhaustion_log.json).
