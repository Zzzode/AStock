# Street Evidence Analysis — No Usable Product Consensus

## Decision-use conclusion

The Street corpus is insufficient for an external valuation anchor across all ten named products. The correct output is not a “weak consensus”; it is **no formal consensus**. Each product remains `watchlist_only / insufficient Street evidence`, and all broker-derived valuation weights are exactly zero.

This conclusion does not mean that the official primary-product evidence is unusable. It means only that the report cannot present independent sell-side targets, forecast averages or a cross-broker validation as though they had been fully collected and audited.

## Evidence hierarchy applied

1. An archived original broker PDF or broker-official research page can be evidence only if it identifies the product, broker/analyst, date, stance, current target/return input, relevant forecast or cash-flow assumption and valuation method.
2. An original PDF that does not contain those actionable fields can be cited as historical context, but has zero valuation weight. The 财信证券 2025-03-31 `508011` note falls here.
3. Media reposts, preview sites, third-party report pages, encrypted download listings and search snippets are discovery/context material. Their visible text is never copied into formal target, forecast, rating or valuation-method fields.
4. Sector reports do not become a single-product price target or allocation recommendation.

## Context that does not become a consensus

The public REIT sector pages are useful in a constrained way: they flag that sector-level valuation and liquidity conditions can vary by asset type and supply cycle. They do not establish any conclusion about the price or expected distribution of `508069`, `508011`, `508068`, `508077`, `508055`, `508080` or `508096`. Likewise, individual reposts can highlight questions for primary-source verification (rent pressure, tenant concentration, expansion integration), but do not supply investable precision.

## Implication for the strategy report

The strategy cannot claim that its 5% total-return objective or 3% cash objective is validated by broker consensus. The product valuation section must rely on its own transparent, primary-source-based framework and must explicitly flag the missing contemporaneous NAV/IOPV/liquidity inputs and missing independent Street anchors. The implementation gate stays closed: `q=0` for all market-traded sleeves.
