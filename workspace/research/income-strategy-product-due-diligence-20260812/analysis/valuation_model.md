# Product Valuation Discipline — Research-Only Diagnostic

This cross-asset product case is deliberately not an issuer target-price model. Every product is WATCHLIST ONLY / q=0; no row has a current fair-value range, final target, implied upside/downside or broker anchor. The available facts support dated diagnostics and define what must be collected before valuation, not a claim that a stale NAV comparison is current value.

## Final Valuation Table

The complete ten-row table is in the structured current-product valuation packet. ## Three-Tier Targets

Not published. Bear/base/bull price targets would be false precision without same-date NAV/IOPV for ETFs or current asset valuation, debt, capital-expenditure and project operating bridges for REITs. The only scenario discipline is an evidence threshold: a missing field preserves q=0; it does not create a base case.

## Relative / PEG / PSG Comparison

Not applicable as a pricing method for this universe. PE, PEG and PSG require issuer earnings/growth denominators and would be method mismatches for a sovereign ETF and heterogeneous infrastructure vehicles. No peer-multiple conclusion is made. Any future comparison must use matched ETF tracking/premium mechanics or REIT asset/cash-flow/NAV methodology after current data are available.

## Seasonality Calibration

Not available. Q2 reports are unaudited and issuers warn that receipts and expenses are uneven. 508055 and 508080 have partial first-year history; 508068, 508077 and 508096 have expansion-related comparability breaks. Quarterly cash or annualized issuer ratios are historical observations, not calibrated forward denominators.

## Next-Quarter Threshold

For every ETF: same-session NAV/IOPV, creation/redemption status, bid-ask/depth, 20-session capacity, current holdings and tax/account data. For 511010 additionally require duration, modified duration, DV01 and convexity. For every REIT: current independent asset valuation/NAV bridge, project debt/maturity/capex, operational drivers and actual-distribution bridge; 508096 first requires NAV reconciliation.

## Method and Assumption Bridge

ETF diagnostic method is price versus dated NAV only, with no conclusion on current premium/discount. REIT diagnostic method is price versus audited historical NAV only, with a date-gap disclosure. These diagnostics are not fundamental value because assets, financing, leases, traffic, generation and capital structure can change after the NAV date. The secondary check is execution eligibility; it also fails because the SSE snapshot omits spread, depth, IOPV/NAV and capacity.

## Market-Expectation Valuation Bridge

The market price is an observable market point, not an intrinsic-value anchor. A positive or negative price/NAV diagnostic may reflect date mismatch, changing assets, rate expectations, financing, liquidity or undisclosed current information. It cannot be decomposed into cash-flow, multiple or duration expectations using this evidence set. Thus the market-expectation bridge is explicitly insufficient evidence.

## Broker/Street Comparison

The broker packet covers all ten products. One historical original PDF and several reposts/previews do not disclose a usable current rating, target, forecast or method. All broker valuation weights are 0.0 and the proper disposition is no usable Street anchor, not consensus.

## Market-Implied Sentiment Anchor

No formal sentiment anchor is calculated. The 2026-08-12 last price and amount are recorded, but a single snapshot lacks price history, depth, current NAV/IOPV and current asset value. Assigning market or broker weights would suggest precision absent from the packet; both are zero for target construction because no target is constructed.

## Growth Earnings Dependency

Not applicable. No high-growth, order, shipment, ASP, PEG, PSG or segment-growth valuation credit is used.

## Full-Chain Classification Dependency

Not applicable. This is a custom cross-asset product-due-diligence case, not a full industry-chain valuation report. Product classification is ETF, toll road, community retail, rental housing, industrial park or energy infrastructure, and all classifications are used only to identify appropriate missing valuation fields.

The appropriate research action is no valuation action: WATCHLIST ONLY / q=0. Price/NAV diagnostics are labelled with source date and gap; no historical cash distribution or manager annualised ratio becomes a yield forecast, target price or allocation input.
