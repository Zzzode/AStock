# Template Brief

- Archetype: JPM-style single-stock equity research note, adapted for a cyclical and asset-heavy integrated petrochemical company.
- Benchmark sources: `workspace/templates/global-broker-research/deep_template_analysis.md` and `workspace/templates/global-broker-research/JPM-Equity-Research-Report-Hulu.pdf`.
- Research framework: the cyclical-industry module at `workspace/templates/industry-coverage-packs/cyclical.md`.
- First-page dashboard: action label; current price/date; bear/base/bull fair value; final target or range; implied return; evidence quality; 2026E earnings bridge; catalysts; invalidation; leverage/cash-flow warning.
- Chapter sequence: IC summary; business and ownership map; full petrochemical chain and cycle position; Brunei project economics; domestic PTA/polyester operations; competition and cost position; historical financial quality; 2026-2028 scenario model; valuation and Street comparison; secondary market; risks and monitoring; evidence/model appendices.
- Required exhibits: integrated chain map; company asset map; crude-to-PX/PTA/polyester spread bridge; volume-price-margin bridge; Brunei-versus-domestic earnings bridge; debt/capex/cash-flow bridge; peer comparison; bear/base/bull SOTP; market-implied expectations; catalyst and risk calendar.
- Narrative standard: chapters open with the investment question and causal logic, embed exhibits inside prose, and end with an explicit investment implication.
- Avoid: source-digest prose, table-only chapters, generic commodity optimism, one-quarter annualization, unsupported feedstock or customer claims, and valuation based on a single PE multiple.
- Custom cyclical module: separate realized earnings from inventory gains/losses, normalize tax and minority interests, and explicitly test crude/feedstock shocks, refining spreads, PX/PTA/polyester spreads, utilization, freight, FX, capex and interest expense.
