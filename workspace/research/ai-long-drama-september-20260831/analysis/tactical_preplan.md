# Tactical Preplan -- Research Only

## Hypothesis

The hypothesis is not that one stock remains strong. It is that a verified AI-long-drama catalyst develops into a repeatable A-share attention and commercialisation theme during September.

## Five gates

1. A second source-backed content, platform, technology, or commercialisation catalyst appears.
2. Mango Media retains the listed platform-exposure anchor status, or an alternative has a primary company source for project relationship.
3. A `project_direct` main-board candidate advances from first board to a verified second-board structure while new `project_direct` first boards replenish breadth.
4. A `project_direct` main-board capacity candidate retains price acceptance and meaningful traded liquidity without a locked-limit assumption.
5. Market regime, breadth, trading status, and risk controls permit conditional observation; they must not be `risk_off` or execution-blocked.

## Scenario outcomes

- Pulse: no main-board advance or broad weakness on D+1. Reject the main-theme hypothesis at D+1 close.
- Local continuation: one main-board advance but no replenishment or capacity. Watch only; reassess at D+2 close.
- Initial main theme: all five gates pass over D+1 to D+2. Conditional research plan only, subject to a complete data-verifier packet and a risk-budget check.
- Reinforced theme: a second official catalyst arrives by D+3 to D+5 while the platform-exposure anchor, direct-project main-board capacity candidate and new direct-project names retain leadership. Continue the conditional research state.
- Isolated acceleration: one name rises while breadth and main-board capacity disappear. Reject; do not substitute unrelated media names.

## No-ChiNext route

Without ChiNext access, the valid universe is not a collection of shadow beneficiaries. Palmread is the first broad-content main-board capacity candidate to recheck; Bona Film and TVZone Media are satellites. No main-board issuer is presently verified as a project-direct beneficiary. Broad-content strength documents diffusion only; a main-board name can pass the project-main-theme gates only after it is tagged `project_direct` and meets the same advancement, capacity and risk gates.

## Hard constraints

T+1, price limits, suspension, opening gaps, and unavailable order-book evidence prohibit any assumption of immediate exit or fill. This memo does not set a position, stop, target, entry price, or order instruction.
