# Risk Framework

1. Causality risk: the programme may be a one-off event rather than an industry profit-pool shift.
2. Mapping risk: broad AI-content, short-drama, film and publishing labels do not establish direct project exposure.
3. Market-structure risk: the 31 August packet has no complete limit-up ecology, confirmed board-count hierarchy or reproducible auction data.
4. Execution risk: T+1 and price limits make assumed fills and same-day exits invalid.
5. Regulatory and content risk: production, review, copyright and audience reception can alter scheduling and economics.
6. Portfolio-governance risk: the local desk has no signed restricted-list authority and no paper portfolio risk packet; any formal paper entry remains blocked.
