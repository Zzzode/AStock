# Segment Valuation Model

This multi-stock screen uses a compact segment/SOTP validation layer even though the published targets are PE-based.

| Company | Segment | Revenue / net profit role | Multiple treatment | Sensitivity | Validation trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 002497 雅化集团 | Lithium salt and resource self-supply | Growth and cycle segment; drives most of 2026E net profit uplift | Cycle-adjusted PE; no separate SOTP premium until cash conversion improves | Lithium price, shipment, inventory gain, hedge result, self-supply | H2 profit at least CNY 1.73bn and improving operating cash flow |
| 002497 雅化集团 | Civil explosives | Base business and earnings floor | Lower stable-business multiple inside consolidated PE | Overseas mining-service execution and margin | Stable annual profit contribution of roughly CNY 0.55-0.60bn |
| 002812 恩捷股份 | Wet-process separator | Core growth segment; volume, ASP, utilization, and unit profit would drive normalized EPS | Zero formal valuation credit while the company-driver gate is blocked | Unit profit, utilization, yield, and customer price are the dominant sensitivities | Company evidence independently rebuilds the CNY5.16 EPS bridge |
| 002812 恩捷股份 | Overseas / 3C separator mix | Potential margin and customer-mix uplift | No incremental multiple until orders and revenue are auditable | Certification, customer conversion, ramp timing | Company-level order and revenue evidence rather than industry demand |

The SOTP cross-check does not create a higher target. It is a guardrail against assigning consolidated growth credit to stable or unverified segments.
