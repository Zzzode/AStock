## Why Now

Official H1 previews show real profit acceleration while prices have de-rated sharply. This creates wide scenario ranges. Only five months remain, however, and the market is highly volatile. A double requires both earnings delivery and renewed risk appetite.

## Core Thesis: 002497 雅化集团

The operating bridge varies lithium shipments, normalized per-tonne contribution, gross margin, opex/tax, net profit, EPS, and PE. The main model yields CNY25.32, shown to readers as CNY25-26. The CNY42 external target is an undisclosed-horizon bull reference with a capped 10% weight. A double needs H2 profit and cash to validate together; the judgmental bull probability is 20%.

## Zero-Weight Sensitivity: 002812 恩捷股份

The CNY103 external target is mathematically sufficient for a double, but it depends on one broker's 2027E EPS and operating assumptions. The company has not quantified the inputs needed to independently rebuild that denominator. The model therefore publishes no formal target, expected upside, or probability.

## Quality Backup: 600150 中国船舶

Order backlog, Q1 cash conversion, and delivery-price visibility make China Shipbuilding the best quality backup. A double still requires a large rerating, and no current explicit external point target was collected. Its range remains a zero-weight house diagnostic.

## False-Positive Audit

Holding-company investment income, one-off earnings, peak memory-cycle profit, and already-crowded AI valuations cannot be assigned growth multiples mechanically. 300390 also fails the current-price double hurdle even in its diagnostic bull range.

## Monitoring Discipline

Upgrade only when company evidence validates the operating bridge and cash conversion. Downgrade immediately if lithium price reverses, separator price/utilization evidence remains single-source, ship margins fail to convert backlog, or memory earnings and cash diverge further. A lower share price alone is not an upgrade trigger.
