# Variant Perception

## 002497 雅化集团

- Consensus: lithium volume, higher resource self-supply and price recovery can lift 2026 net profit to roughly CNY 3.0bn; a 16x multiple supports CNY 42.
- House view: the operating bridge is credible enough for a formal scenario, but the five-month rerating path is not. The reader range is CNY25-26 and the double is a 20% bull scenario.
- Strongest opposition: current prices may already signal that lithium-price gains, inventory benefits and H1 profit cannot be extrapolated; 2026E operating cash flow is also much weaker than accounting profit.
- Falsification: H2 net profit below CNY 1.73bn, renewed hedge losses, lithium-price reversal, or materially negative operating cash flow invalidates the core tracking thesis.

## 002812 恩捷股份

- Consensus: separator supply tightness, utilization and unit-profit recovery can drive 2027E EPS to CNY 5.16; 20x supports CNY 103.
- House view: the target is auditable but the company-level denominator is not. CNY103 and the CNY30.96/67.08/103.20 range therefore remain zero-weight external sensitivities, with no house target or probability.
- Strongest opposition: new supply or weak demand may cap price recovery, and a 2027 earnings jump may not be capitalized in 2026 if order/customer/cash evidence remains incomplete.
- Eligibility trigger: company-level shipment, realized unit profit, effective utilization, new-line yield, customer price, and cash conversion close the current valuation blocker.

## 600150 中国船舶

- Consensus: long backlog and higher-price deliveries support multi-year profit growth.
- House view: quality is comparatively high, but a double requires the market to pay about 21.1x 2027E EPS, roughly twice the current implied multiple.
- Strongest opposition: shipbuilding is cyclical; investors may refuse to capitalize peak delivery margins at a high multiple.
- Falsification: delivery delays, weaker margin, new-order price deterioration, or integration synergies not appearing in reported results.

## 301308 江波龙

- Consensus: memory-price inflation plus enterprise/AI storage can create exceptional 2026 earnings.
- House view: the original 2026E forecast is already a peak-like denominator and implies almost no 2027 growth. Negative operating cash flow and recent price collapse make the bull multiple too fragile for a formal target.
- Strongest opposition to the house caution: supply discipline could keep memory prices higher for longer, while enterprise mix creates a structural rather than cyclical margin step-up.
- Falsification of caution: positive operating cash flow, inventory quality, stable H2 gross margin and 2027 earnings growth above 15% would justify a model rebuild.

## Cross-Sectional Variant

The market's apparent low PE is not the variant. The variant is whether the denominator survives and whether investors rerate it before year-end. The house view differs from a simple target-price screen by assigning low probability to the timing leg and zero weight to incomplete external anchors.
