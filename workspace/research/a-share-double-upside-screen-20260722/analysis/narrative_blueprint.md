# Narrative Blueprint

## Chapter 1: What is the answer?

Question: Does the current A-share market contain a defensible year-end double? Evidence: current prices, formal targets, probability-weighted outputs. Synthesis: no base-case double; two bull-case candidates. Reader action: focus research on validation rather than chase the target.

## Chapter 2: Why is the hurdle unusually high?

Question: What does a five-month double require? Evidence: compound monthly return, market breadth, turnover, recent drawdowns. Synthesis: timing and risk appetite are as important as fundamentals. Reader action: reject long-horizon targets that are not year-end compatible.

## Chapter 3: How was the market screened?

Question: Why do many apparent doubles fail? Evidence: official preview universe, current prices, cash quality, valuation method, external-anchor gate. Synthesis: low-base and one-off denominators create false positives. Reader action: retain only names with auditable operating and valuation bridges.

## Chapter 4: Why 002497 and 002812?

Question: What must happen operationally? Evidence: lithium shipment/self-supply/cash bridge and separator shipment/utilization/unit-profit bridge. Synthesis: each can double only in a bull scenario. Reader action: monitor specific H2 thresholds and invalidation.

## Chapter 5: What about the backups?

Question: Why not promote 600150, 301308, 002240 or 300390? Evidence: missing point anchors, cycle risk, cash conversion and threshold math. Synthesis: optionality exists but evidence is incomplete or upside insufficient. Reader action: keep zero formal weight until proof improves.

## Chapter 6: What is the valuation saying?

Question: What does the current price imply and what rerating is required? Evidence: share count, market cap, EPS, current PE, required PE, bear/base/bull and blended targets. Synthesis: base targets do not double. Reader action: distinguish fair-value range from tail target.

## Chapter 7: What could go wrong before year-end?

Question: Which risks arrive first? Evidence: lithium price, separator price/utilization, memory price/cash flow, ship delivery/margin, risk appetite. Synthesis: failure can occur through either earnings or multiple. Reader action: use downgrade rules, not narrative loyalty.

## Appendix: Can the evidence be audited?

Question: Which claims are official, broker-derived or inferred? Evidence: source registry, claim audit, source-exhaustion record. Synthesis: original targets receive limited weight and gaps remain visible. Reader action: treat the report as a dated research packet, not a permanent recommendation.
