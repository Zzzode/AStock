## Year-End Timing Risk

A five-month double implies roughly 15% compound monthly appreciation. Even correct long-term theses can fail the 2026-12-31 deadline. Timing risk is therefore a first-class model variable, not a footnote. The collected broker targets have undisclosed horizons and cannot be relabeled as year-end forecasts.

## Balance-Sheet and Cash Risk

002497 and 002240 are exposed to working-capital and commodity-cycle swings. 301308's inventory-intensive business and negative Q1 operating cash flow create a large accounting-to-cash gap. 002812 must fund capacity while utilization recovers. The report does not treat low headline PE as protection when cash conversion is unproven.

## Invalidation Rules

- 002497: remove core tracking if H2 profit falls below the bridge or cash conversion remains materially negative.
- 002812: retain zero weight until company-level shipment, price, utilization, yield, unit-profit, and cash evidence rebuilds the 2027 EPS bridge.
- 600150: keep zero formal weight until an external price anchor and margin delivery are auditable.
- 301308: require positive cash conversion and non-peak 2027 growth before rebuilding the valuation.
- 002240: lithium price, self-supply and cash flow must validate together.
- 300390: do not promote while the bull value remains below the double threshold.

## Portfolio Interpretation Boundary

This report is a research screen, not an order-execution or position-sizing instruction. Tail optionality should not be confused with expected return. The only conditional core candidate has a 20% judgmental double probability. The evidence-blocked 002812 sensitivity has no house probability or formal expected return.
