## Valuation Crowding and Fund Attitude

301308 is the clearest valuation-crowding case: rapid appreciation into the memory upcycle was followed by a roughly 37% two-week drawdown. The price action suggests institutional and momentum capital are revising the peak-cycle multiple, even though the exact institutional fund attitude cannot be identified from public daily bars. 002497, 002812, 002240 and 300390 also show sharp risk reduction. 600150 shows more stable relative performance.

## Seat, Northbound, Financing and Hot-Money Evidence

No complete, company-by-company exchange seat dataset, northbound holding series, financing-balance series, or verified hot-money classification was collected for this report. Those fields receive zero analytical weight. A single daily seat list or media label would not establish institutional accumulation. Financing and northbound data should be added only if a current exchange-grade time series can be reconciled across all names.

## Trading Style Implication

The observed style is a high-beta trend-swing market with violent factor rotation. A five-month double requires both earnings delivery and a favorable style regime. Therefore the report treats 002497 and 002812 as conditional research watches, not as mechanical breakout trades, and keeps 301308 in the high-risk observation tier despite its mathematical tail upside.
