# Template Brief

## Archetype

The report blends the J.P. Morgan equity-research first-page decision dashboard with the AllianceBernstein scenario-range market note. It is a cross-sectional opportunity screen, not a chartbook and not a collection of company summaries.

## First-Page Dashboard

The first page must show the 2026-07-22 data cutoff, the direct answer to whether any high-confidence double exists, current prices, base and bull values, bull upside, action, evidence quality, the year-end validation trigger, and the strongest invalidation condition for each primary name. It must distinguish `base-case upside` from `bull-case double optionality`.

## Required Chapter Sequence

1. Investment committee conclusion and ranked action map.
2. Market regime and the arithmetic of a five-month double.
3. Full-market screening funnel and false-positive audit.
4. Operating-driver and earnings-quality comparison.
5. Company capsules for 002497, 002812, 600150, 301308, 002240, and 300390.
6. Valuation recovery, current-price-implied expectations, and scenario targets.
7. Catalyst calendar through 2026-12-31.
8. Contrarian case, risk matrix, and monitoring discipline.
9. Source-quality and model appendix.

## Required Exhibits

- Screening funnel from broad candidate pool to deep-model pool.
- Scorecard using 30% earnings delivery, 25% valuation headroom, 20% catalyst timing, 15% cash/balance-sheet quality, and 10% evidence quality.
- Base-versus-bull upside matrix.
- Earnings-to-cash conversion comparison.
- Current-price-implied PE / normalized denominator comparison.
- Driver bridge for shipbuilding, memory, separator, and lithium names.
- False-positive matrix for holding companies, one-off earnings, and crowded AI hardware.
- Catalyst timeline from H1 results through year-end.
- Probability-impact risk heatmap.
- Evidence pyramid separating official filings, original broker PDFs, public snapshots, inference, and rumor.

## Custom Evidence Scope

Every deep-model ticker must be tested on earnings acceleration, deducted-profit purity, cash conversion, share-count consistency, valuation headroom, business-cycle durability, price/ASP or order evidence, year-end catalyst visibility, balance-sheet risk, and evidence quality. Missing company-level evidence blocks a core action even when model upside exceeds 100%.

## What to Avoid

- Do not present a bull target as the base target.
- Do not annualize 2026Q1 or 2026H1 without a seasonality and cycle bridge.
- Do not apply 25-45x growth PE to holding-company investment income or peak cyclical earnings.
- Do not use a downstream demand story as proof of company orders, utilization, or margins.
- Do not treat a broker rating without an explicit target as a Street price anchor.
- Do not stack long tables without prose that states the investment implication.
- Do not expose workflow paths or review mechanics in the reader-facing body.

## Page Design

Use compact institutional tables, one main message per exhibit, Chinese prose before and after each exhibit cluster, restrained color, and visible data-source notes. Detailed claim audits and source registries belong in the appendix.
