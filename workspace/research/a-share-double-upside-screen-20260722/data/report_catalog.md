- Forecast dispersion: 2026E attributable net profit CNY16.550bn-CNY20.036bn; 2027E CNY22.728bn-CNY25.712bn.
- Risks disclosed across the reports: global shipbuilding capacity expansion, shipping/shipbuilding demand weakness, trade frictions, raw-material and FX volatility, and delayed decarbonization policy execution.
- Target-price boundary: no explicit target in five original PDFs. Formal target weight is zero.

- Risks: storage demand weakness, slower product commercialization, customer concentration, and downstream demand weakness.
- Freshness and target boundary: no original report after 2026-05-07 and no explicit target price through the 2026-07-22 public refresh. Formal target weight is zero.
