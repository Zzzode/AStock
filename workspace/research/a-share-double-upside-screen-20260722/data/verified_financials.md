## Accounting and comparability controls

- `600150`: use the merger-restated 2025H1 base when measuring the current combined issuer. The preview's +143.56%-191.21% parent-NP growth is the comparable range; +212.29%-273.39% is the legacy standalone comparison. FY2025 and 2026Q1 also reflect the same-control merger accounting described in the filings.
- `300390`: use the adjusted prior-period figures after the same-control acquisition of Tianhua Times. The corrected 2025 annual report, not the superseded original, is the source of truth.
- `301308`: H1 revenue CNY22-25bn and parent NP CNY9.2-11.0bn are preview ranges. The implied margin is not an audited margin and should be stress-tested against wafer prices and working capital.
- `002812`: the H1 preview is a turnaround range; it gives no H1 revenue or separator volume. Do not infer an ASP or utilization rate from the profit range.
- `002240`, `300390`, `002497`: the previews attribute recovery to lithium volume/price and cost improvement, but disclose no H1 physical tonnage or realized ASP. Commodity-price sensitivity must remain explicit.

## Model-use boundary

The H1 ranges can anchor scenario constraints but cannot substitute for audited H1 results or full-year estimates. Any FY2026 model must separately state its H2 volume, ASP, margin, working-capital, and share-count assumptions. A midpoint may be used only as an explicitly labeled scenario input, not as a reported fact.
