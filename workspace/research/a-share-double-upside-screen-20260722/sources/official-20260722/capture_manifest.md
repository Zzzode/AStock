## Accounting and evidence limitations

- FY2025 annual reports contain audited financial statements. The 2026Q1 reports and 2026H1 previews explicitly state that their figures are unaudited.
- `600150` completed a same-control absorption merger with China Shipbuilding Industry in September 2025. The filing presents both legacy and merger-restated comparative bases; the restated basis is required for like-for-like comparison of the current issuer.
- `300390_2025_annual_report_corrected.pdf` supersedes the original 2025 annual-report file. The corrected filing is used throughout this case.
- H1 previews provide ranges and management explanations, not audited point estimates, product-level ASP, or complete cash-flow statements.
- Text extractions can introduce whitespace and table-layout artifacts. Page-level quotation or dispute resolution must return to the PDFs.
