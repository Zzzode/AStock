# Research Brief: A-Share Double-Upside Screen Through 2026 Year-End

- Case ID: `a-share-double-upside-screen-20260722`
- Objective: identify A-share companies whose evidence-backed bull case can reach at least 100% upside from the 2026-07-22 price by 2026 year-end, while separating that tail scenario from the base case.
- Report type: full-market opportunity screen with multi-stock valuation capsules.
- Reader language: Chinese body with a short English abstract.
- Depth: detailed 25-35 page institutional market note.
- Market-data cutoff: 2026-07-22 close or the latest full-realtime quote captured after the close.
- Financial-data cutoff: FY2025, 2026Q1 reported results, and official 2026H1 previews or preliminary results available by 2026-07-22.
- Event horizon: 2026-07-22 through 2026-12-31.
- Benchmark archetype: J.P. Morgan first-page equity dashboard plus AllianceBernstein scenario-range market note; prose-led rather than a pure chartbook.
- Custom evidence scope: earnings acceleration, earnings quality, valuation headroom, price position, year-end catalyst visibility, cash conversion, industry-cycle durability, balance-sheet risk, and evidence quality.
- Initial screen universe: 600150, 301308, 002812, 002240, 300390, 002497, 002460, 000623, 600739, 002432, 000685, 002648, 000703, 600673, 600183, and 002916.
- Initial deep-model pool: 600150, 301308, 002812, 002240, 300390, and 002497. This is provisional until evidence and model reviews pass.
- Conditional watch pool: 000623, 600739, 002432, 000685, 002460, 002648, 000703, and 600673. These names require method repair, stronger catalysts, or better current-price upside.
- High-valuation exclusion checks: 600183 and 002916 are retained as AI-hardware crowding controls, not presumed beneficiaries.
- Demand and cycle anchors: global shipbuilding backlog and delivery prices, memory wafer supply and end-demand, lithium carbonate and battery-separator pricing, downstream battery demand, and A-share risk appetite.
- Ranking method: 30% earnings delivery, 25% valuation headroom, 20% catalyst timing, 15% cash/balance-sheet quality, and 10% evidence quality. A ticker cannot rank as core if any publication blocker remains.
- Definition of double-upside: final bull value divided by the 2026-07-22 current price minus one is at least 100%. The base case is reported separately and is never described as a double unless it independently clears the threshold.
- Core conclusion boundary: the report may conclude that no high-confidence base-case double exists. Tail optionality is not a recommendation.
- Downgrade path: unsupported high-growth or customer/order claims become `watchlist only / insufficient evidence`; missing defensible current-price valuation becomes `evidence memo`; incomplete Street anchors receive zero weight and explicit disclosure.
- Exclusions: automated trading, brokerage execution, undisclosed rumors, and mechanically annualized one-off earnings.

## Intake Pass Conditions

The final report must use current prices, distinguish reported facts from forecasts, provide business-model-matched bear/base/bull valuation for each deep-model ticker, show what must happen before 2026 year-end for a double, and explain why every rejected high-upside model was rejected. Publication requires all review cycles, XeLaTeX compilation, rendered-page inspection, model reproducibility, the case verifier, the repository research gate, and final IC sign-off.
