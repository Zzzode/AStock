# Narrative Blueprint

1. Start with the household decision problem, not a product or a historical return.
2. Establish the liquidity and debt constraints that determine whether investment risk is permissible.
3. Explain the causal role of every sleeve before naming product examples.
4. Separate legal/product mechanics from the house allocation assumptions.
5. Present cash distribution as a cash-flow choice within total return, not an alpha claim.
6. Translate weights into a fixed-CNY200,000 implementation, then show the conditional deployment algorithm.
7. Challenge the thesis with concentrated dividend exposure and a multi-asset loss scenario.
8. Finish with a checklist and triggers that change the action.

Reader-facing text must remain Chinese, prose-led, and clear that the strategy is conditional research rather than a real-time execution instruction.
