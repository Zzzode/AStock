# Template Brief

- Archetype: AllianceBernstein-style capital-markets strategy note, adapted for a CNY retail household rather than an issuer report.
- First-page dashboard: direct conditional conclusion, target allocation variants, hard exclusions, deployment sequence, expected temporary-loss range, and investor facts still needed.
- Reader-facing language: Chinese. Governance and model-audit terms may remain English where a machine check requires them.
- Required chapters: decision dashboard; objectives and assumptions; evidence and product mechanics; allocation model; implementation map; cash-flow and tax mechanics; scenario/risk framework; annual contribution and rebalance process; monitoring; appendix source register.
- Required exhibits: capital-allocation waterfall; fixed-CNY200,000 implementation table; product-role map; cash-flow-versus-total-return bridge; shock table; annual-add algorithm; guardrail dashboard.
- What to avoid: headline-yield ranking, a one-day market call, live-order language, unverified product data, promises of capital preservation, and treating distributions as an incremental return.
- Framework: custom strategic multi-asset allocation for CNY retail capital.
