# Publicly Available Research Sentiment Boundary

No sell-side consensus is used. The investment conclusion rests on product structure, the user-stated mandate horizon, liquidity sleeves inside $V$ and transparent strategic assumptions rather than broker target prices. This avoids treating a broker forecast for a constituent company or a historical ETF distribution as an allocation signal.

Potential crowding is also not claimed because the case has no reproducible current holdings, fund-flow, margin-financing or order-book packet. The only documented concentration warning is the published sector composition of a representative dividend-low-volatility index (S16), which is an input to a required look-through test rather than a market-timing conclusion.
