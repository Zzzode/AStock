# Current Valuation Model Boundary

The structured allocation rows deliberately show `not_applicable` for price, share count, market capitalization, revenue, net profit, EPS, target and upside. Those fields are in the generic schema because it is designed for issuer research; inventing numbers for fund sleeves would be false precision. Each row now separates (1) Tier-1 product-identity evidence, (2) the controlled allocation decision, and (3) execution evidence, which is currently unavailable. The only model outputs are conditional eligibility, role, risk limit and staging rule. The user-defined fixed CNY200,000 is the full investable capital $V$; protected liquidity and trading cash are internal sleeves, and REIT is zero-weight unless three independent projects pass the stated screen. The common execution-policy reference is data/execution_eligibility_policy_20260812.json, status not_run; its deterministic fallback is eligible protected liquidity, never staging cash above CNY10,000.
