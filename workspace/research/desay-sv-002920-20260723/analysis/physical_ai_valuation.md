## Risk-adjusted real-option valuation

Company disclosure establishes that the business is pursuing low-speed autonomous logistics and embodied intelligence; it also describes Chuanxing Zhiyuan applications and the AI Cube compute terminal. Those facts do not disclose a named robot customer, delivered robot units, AI Cube ASP, recognised revenue, gross/net margin, incremental R&D or working-capital burden. Rather than make this omission invisible, the valuation uses three mutually exclusive FY2028 outcomes, discounts their post-proof values at 12% for two years, and weights them by disclosed maturity.

| FY2028 outcome | AStock probability | Conditional FY2028 value / share | Discounted current value | Weighted current value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No measurable commercial conversion | 55% | CNY0.00 | CNY0.00 | CNY0.00 |
| First commercial scale: CNY1.0bn revenue, 8% net margin, 22x P/E | 35% | CNY2.95 | CNY2.35 | CNY0.82 |
| Replicable platform scale: CNY5.0bn revenue, 10% net margin, 25x P/E | 10% | CNY20.94 | CNY16.69 | CNY1.67 |
| **Risk-adjusted Physical AI option** | **100%** | -- | -- | **CNY2.49** |

The 55% no-conversion branch is deliberately the largest branch because the primary record still lacks a named customer, delivered product, units, revenue, margin and cash evidence. The 35% commercial case reflects the product, IR cooperation, nomination and planned 2026 delivery disclosures. The 10% platform case is low because it requires several unproven steps: actual delivery, multiple customers/scenarios, durable margins and cash conversion. These are AStock probabilities, not facts, consensus or company guidance.

The calculation is: FY2028 option value = revenue × net margin ÷ 0.5968bn shares × post-proof P/E; present value = FY2028 option value ÷ (1 + 12%)². The final CNY88.9 house reference equals the CNY86.44 base-business multi-anchor value plus CNY2.49 option value, rounded for display. The option layer has no 2026E EPS credit and does not change the 18.5x base-business P/E.

## Conditions for valuation migration

| Evidence state | Research classification | Valuation treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Direction / product disclosure only | `strategic option` | Retain majority no-conversion branch and low commercial probability |
| Customer or fleet named, designation/contract disclosed | `commercial option` | Recalibrate first-commercial-scale probability; do not infer revenue from a designation |
| SOP/accepted deliveries and recognised revenue | `revenue option` | Replace revenue proxy with units/ASP or a disclosed revenue range |
| Margin, cash conversion and replicated customers | `earnings option` | Recalibrate margins, multiple and platform-scale probability from evidence |

## What would make it a large opportunity

The sensitivity shows that the central issue is **scale × margin × duration**, not whether an AI Cube or robot controller exists. For illustration, CNY2bn of annual incremental revenue at an 8% net margin and 22x post-proof P/E corresponds to about CNY5.90 per share; CNY5bn at 10% and 25x corresponds to CNY20.94 per share. These are conditional arithmetic outcomes, not forecasts. They make the investment question concrete: can management demonstrate a multi-billion-yuan, economically attractive and repeatable Physical AI platform during 2027--2028?
