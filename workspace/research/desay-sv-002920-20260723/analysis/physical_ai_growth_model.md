# Physical AI Growth Model — 2026--2028 Opportunity Calibration

## Status: OPTION MODEL, NOT A FORECAST

The automotive base model remains unchanged: it contains reported cockpit, intelligent-driving and connectivity businesses, and generates the CNY86.4 base-value anchor. This document tests the *future* question: how much incremental revenue and profit would Desay SV's Physical AI paths need before they become material to per-share value. It is a calibration framework, not a forecast, because the available disclosures do not provide customer-level volume, ASP, recognised revenue, project gross margin, incremental R&D or cash-conversion data.

## Three routes and their economic gates

| Route | Existing capability that may transfer | First disclosed proof | Missing economic proof | Earliest valuation credit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High-level AD / central compute | Vehicle-grade compute, perception, control, software integration and vehicle engineering | Automotive products and anonymous central-computing orders | Named programme, SOP, unit value, revenue and margin | Already included only through aggregate automotive earnings; no separate uplift |
| Low-speed autonomous logistics | Autonomous-driving stack plus Chuanxing Zhiyuan low-speed platform | The company describes industrial logistics, urban distribution and cold-chain scenarios | Fleet/customer, operating model, delivered units, ASP, recurring/service revenue, project margin | After delivery and recognised revenue; not at platform announcement |
| Embodied intelligence | AI Cube compute terminal and robot-domain-control engineering | The company describes AI Cube/robot product direction; broker reports describe a 2026 planned delivery | Customer, model, unit volume, ASP, production acceptance, revenue, margin and working capital | After customer-to-income closure; broker statement alone is not sufficient |

The routes share silicon, vehicle-grade manufacturing, algorithms and system integration, but they do **not** share a demonstrated profit pool. Automotive-domain revenue must not be counted again as robot or logistics revenue.

\[

\text{conditional value/share} = \text{incremental EPS} \times \text{P/E}.
\]

All cells below are **sensitivity cases, not Desay SV forecasts**. The important implication is scale. A CNY0.5--1.0bn newly recognised business is strategically meaningful but, at hardware-like margins, does not by itself justify a large re-rating of a CNY49.82bn market-cap company. A materially convex outcome requires several billion yuan of recurring/delivered revenue, credible project profitability and a sustained platform valuation. That is the opportunity to underwrite over 2027--2028, not a 2026 base-EPS assumption.

## Evidence-gated probability decision

The report now uses a **separate, risk-adjusted real-option layer** rather than leaving the future opportunity outside valuation altogether. Its probabilities are explicitly AStock house underwriting, not company guidance or consensus. This is an intentionally conservative bridge: product release, a robot-controller nomination and planned 2026 delivery justify a non-zero commercialisation case; the absence of a named customer, actual delivery, unit, revenue, margin and cash evidence requires a majority no-conversion branch and a low platform-scale branch. The option never changes 2026E base EPS or the CNY86.4 automotive-base target.

| Mutually exclusive FY2028 outcome | Probability | FY2028 conditional value / share | Present value at 12% for two years | Probability-weighted value / share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No measurable commercial conversion | 55% | CNY0.00 | CNY0.00 | CNY0.00 |
| First commercial scale: CNY1.0bn revenue, 8% net margin, 22x P/E | 35% | CNY2.95 | CNY2.35 | CNY0.82 |
| Replicable platform scale: CNY5.0bn revenue, 10% net margin, 25x P/E | 10% | CNY20.94 | CNY16.69 | CNY1.67 |
| **Risk-adjusted Physical AI option value** | **100%** | -- | -- | **CNY2.49** |

The combined house reference is therefore CNY86.44 for the current automotive multi-anchor value plus CNY2.49 for the Physical AI option = CNY88.93, displayed as **CNY88.9**. This is a risk-adjusted current value, not an assertion that FY2028 revenue will be CNY1.0bn or CNY5.0bn. It is designed to make the user's future-opportunity question visible in the valuation system while preserving the evidence boundary.

The following four gates control whether the probabilities and scenario values can be raised or must be reduced:

| Gate | Observable evidence | Model action | Failure / no evidence action |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 — Commercial identity | Named customer or fleet/operator; product/model and contract/nomination status | Increase/decline the first-commercial-scale probability, subject to source governance | Reduce first-commercial-scale probability toward zero |
| G2 — Production conversion | SOP/acceptance, cumulative units or delivered fleet and first recognised revenue | Replace CNY1.0bn proxy with a units × ASP or disclosed-revenue range | Do not infer revenue from a designation or launch plan |
| G3 — Unit economics | Gross margin, project/segment margin, incremental R&D and working-capital evidence | Replace 8%/10% net-margin sensitivities and reassess P/E | Reduce option value or remove it if the economics are not viable |
| G4 — Replication | Multiple customers/scenarios, renewal/service data or a multi-year revenue disclosure | Increase the platform-scale probability and evaluate duration | Treat as one-off project and reduce terminal multiple |

## Investor conclusion

Physical AI now changes the **blended** CNY88.9 reference value, while the CNY86.4 base target remains intact as the automotive earnings anchor. The actionable variant is a call option whose value is small at the conservative 10% platform-scale probability: research/own it only if one has a differentiated view that G1--G3 can be crossed faster or at more scale than the public record shows. The next high-information events are company half-year disclosures, named customer/fleet announcements, actual 2026 robot-domain-controller deliveries and revenue/working-capital commentary. Until then, a headline about AI Cube, a collaboration or a planned shipment can change the research probability only when it supplies a verifiable commercial field.
