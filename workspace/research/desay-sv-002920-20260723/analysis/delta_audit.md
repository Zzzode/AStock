# Delta Audit — Physical AI Future-Opportunity Update

## Trigger and scope

On 2026-07-23, the investment committee challenge was that the original report answered the near-term fair-value question too narrowly: its CNY86.4 baseline target had only 3.5% upside and treated Physical AI as unmodelled optionality.  The corrected research question is whether Desay SV's existing automotive intelligence capabilities can create an investable 2026--2028 Physical AI profit pool, not whether the current automotive business alone is modestly mispriced.

## Original-control gap

| Control | Original treatment | Gap | Corrective deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Future-opportunity framing | Mentioned central computing, robot controller and AI Cube as zero-EPS optionality | The report did not map the paths, gates or timing that would turn optionality into earnings | Physical AI opportunity tree and value-chain economics |
| Evidence hierarchy | Annual-report excerpts and two broker statements were retained | Product, customer, volume, ASP, margin and recognition fields were not separated in one evidence packet | Source-governed Physical AI evidence register and claim audit additions |
| Growth model | Base 2026E correctly gave no credit to unverified products | No 2026--2028 probability/threshold sensitivity showed what evidence would justify future credit | Explicit option model, with assumptions labelled as sensitivities rather than forecasts |
| Reader-facing decision | `market-supported watch` was driven by 2026 fundamental valuation | It did not distinguish a trading/fair-value view from an event-driven future-opportunity view | Two-layer valuation and Physical AI chapter in the report |

## Cause and prevention

The prior research emphasis selected the mature automotive earnings bridge as the primary valuation method, then relegated the new-domain paths to a disclosure disclaimer. That is methodologically safe but incomplete for a future-opportunity mandate. Future-theme single-stock work must therefore include a separate opportunity tree at R1: technology maturity, customer/contract proof, launch timing, unit economics, revenue-recognition bridge, probability and disconfirming evidence. A qualitative theme label alone cannot receive valuation credit; conversely, absence of near-term earnings must not end the inquiry.

## Re-opened acceptance conditions

1. Evidence tiers must distinguish the company's disclosed strategic direction from broker-reported order information and all unknown unit-economics fields.
2. Each Physical AI path must have a named mechanism, observable milestone, revenue/EPS bridge or an explicit evidence gap, and an invalidation condition.
3. The CNY86.4 automotive base target must remain separate from Physical AI. A numerical Physical AI option may enter the combined reference only as a clearly labelled, probability-weighted and discounted layer; it must not be represented as disclosed earnings or embedded in the base P/E.
4. The final PDF, source registry, claim audit, valuation and sign-off must be refreshed and all R0--R4 gates rerun.

## Second challenge: formal valuation integration

The future-opportunity chapter and sensitivity were initially added, but the CNY86.4 display target still excluded the option. The additional correction is a two-layer valuation: CNY86.44 base-business multi-anchor value plus CNY2.49 per share of FY2028 Physical AI real-option value, giving a displayed CNY88.9 combined reference. The option uses mutually exclusive 55\% no-conversion, 35\% first-commercial-scale and 10\% replicable-platform outcomes, discounted two years at 12\%. These are AStock underwriting assumptions, not issuer guidance, and never change 2026E base EPS.
