# Desay SV Physical-AI Evidence Pack

**Issuer:** Desay SV (002920.SZ)  
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-07-23  
**Scope:** Automotive intelligent-driving capability, robot-domain-control / AI Cube, Chuanxing Zhiyuan low-speed autonomous vehicles, and the adjacent L4 Robotaxi platform. `Physical AI` is an analyst research label; the issuer's own disclosure uses the terms `智能驾驶`, `具身智能`, `机器人`, `无人车` and `Robotaxi`.

## Decision use

The evidence supports a **three-layer opportunity tree**, not an immediate revenue or EPS add-on:

1. **Monetised base — automotive intelligent driving.** FY2025 intelligent-driving revenue was CNY9.70bn (+32.63% YoY); the company discloses scaled delivery to named OEMs. This is the cash-generating engineering and delivery base from which the new applications can be assessed.
2. **Commercialisation option — robot controller / AI Cube and Chuanxing Zhiyuan.** Products, strategic cooperation, a robot-controller nomination and unnamed low-speed-vehicle orders are issuer-disclosed. These items are stronger than a concept-only story, but remain pre-financial disclosure.
3. **Technology / ecosystem option — NVIDIA Thor and DeepRoute Robotaxi.** The company reports POC completion for a production-oriented Thor solution and a named DeepRoute cooperation agreement. Neither source discloses delivery, customer volume, revenue, ASP or margin.

The resulting investment question is therefore not whether Desay SV has entered Physical AI — it has — but whether the 2026-2027 disclosures convert the option into a measurable `customer → SOP/delivery → units → revenue → gross profit/cash` chain. No claim below supports assigning a separate base-case EPS contribution before that chain is disclosed.

## Evidence boundary and valuation rule

The source review covered the FY2025 annual report, issuer IR records available in the case archive through 2026-07-23, and issuer corporate-news pages. No issuer disclosure was located that names a robot customer, identifies the robot programme, states robot-controller or S6 volume/ASP, confirms robot-controller mass-production completion, or reports a separate revenue, gross-profit or cash-flow line for AI Cube, robot control or low-speed autonomous vehicles.

Therefore:

- **Do not use:** a robot-domain-controller nomination, unspecified low-speed-vehicle order, product release, POC, corporate-news phrase or strategic cooperation as backlog, revenue or EPS.
- **Do use:** the disclosures to set a milestone-based 2026-2028 opportunity tree. The next gating evidence is: named customer/programme; SOP or accepted delivery; units and revenue; gross margin and incremental R&D / working-capital burden; repeat order or cash collection.
- **Do not double count:** the FY2025 CNY9.70bn automotive intelligent-driving revenue is the existing vehicle business. It validates capability but is not the robot / low-speed-vehicle revenue opportunity.
