## One-sentence synthesis

The copyable part of global private equity is **underwriting discipline**—not the transaction. A personal public-market investor can demand evidence for cash generation, debt capacity, operating KPIs, valuation and invalidation; they cannot purchase control, force a turnaround, originate a bespoke loan or use institutional liabilities as cheap capital.
