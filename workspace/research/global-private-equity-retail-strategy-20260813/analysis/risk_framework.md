# China public-market risk framework

## Design rule: size for the exit you can plausibly take

The process begins at zero leverage. The investor has no control over corporate actions, trading halts, liquidity, market-wide risk appetite or price limits. Therefore sizing must be solved backwards from a conservative bear-case loss and an executable normal-market exit capacity; it cannot rely on an intraday stop-loss promise.

## Market-structure adaptations

1. **Settlement / reverse trading:** ordinary A-share exposure should be designed around a non-intraday disposal stress scenario: purchased securities may not be sold before settlement except where a specified reverse-trading exception applies. The precise settlement timetable and exception must be checked in current clearing, exchange and product rules. An ETF creation/redemption mechanism is not the same as an assured secondary-market sale.
2. **Price limits and volatility:** price-limit settings vary by board, fund category, special status and listing stage; no single percentage is a whole-market risk assumption. Do not create a full position in newly listed, special-treatment, suspension-prone, continuous-limit or otherwise discontinuous situations. A limit move is a liquidity and thesis-review event, not proof a mechanical stop will fill.
3. **Margin:** no financing add-on, no naked short and no strategy whose expected return needs borrow availability. Two-financing eligibility, collateral and broker terms are not a stable retail LBO capital structure.
4. **Concentration:** look through ETF holdings and group positions by real economic driver—demand cycle, rate sensitivity, property/rental, commodity, financing or a common customer—rather than ticker count.
5. **Public REIT:** model distributable cash flow after asset-level operations, maintenance capex, debt/refinancing and incentives. Historical distribution is an observation, not a promised yield. Treat public REITs as potentially illiquid operating-asset exposure, not cash.

## Sizing logic, intentionally not activated

No numeric weight, loss budget or concentration limit is suitable for publication as a generic recommendation. The user has not supplied income needs, emergency reserves, existing holdings, investment horizon, risk capacity, account permissions, tax treatment, liquidity needs or a tested bear-case tolerance. Therefore the following is only the logical order for a later personalised risk process:

- remove essential spending reserves and known near-term commitments before defining any risk capital;
- start any future named direct exposure smaller than the research conviction would suggest, and enlarge it only after independent evidence, liquidity capacity and an approved loss budget reconcile;
- calculate a tentative size as the minimum of the individually approved single-name limit, permitted planned loss divided by documented bear-case loss, and conservative liquidity capacity;
- group direct-equity and ETF look-through exposure by common economic driver instead of ticker count; a factor limit requires actual holdings and historical/stress evidence;
- retain direct equity, public REIT and formal-announcement event research as optional satellites. The correct exposure can be zero.

No number becomes active until the user-specific inputs, point-in-time stress test and separate risk approval exist. This memo deliberately does not supply that approval.

## Escalation

| Trigger | Required response |
|---|---|
| Audit, fraud, fund occupation, key licence or control-person event | Stop additions; reassess facts and feasible exit under current liquidity |
| Debt/refinancing breaks bear case | Red-team the cash-flow bridge; reduce or exit only through a feasible liquidity plan |
| Two reporting periods miss the bear KPI | Re-underwrite; do not average down automatically |
| Thesis catalyst window expires | Compare expected return with alternatives and remove unsupported upside |
| A broad portfolio drawdown breaches a pre-approved threshold | Pause new satellite risk and conduct a factor/concentration review; threshold cannot be invented after the event |

Relevant exchange and regulator sources: S08–S14 in the local registry.
