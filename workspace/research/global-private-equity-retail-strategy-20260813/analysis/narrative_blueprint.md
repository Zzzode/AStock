# Narrative blueprint

1. Start with the answer: a personal investor can replicate the *investment committee*, not the buyout.
2. Explain why leading managers are multi-asset platforms and why their edge is a bundle of rights and infrastructure.
3. Map the bundle into portable, partial and forbidden components.
4. Turn the portable portion into a five-vote public-information underwriting card.
5. Adjust sizing and selling logic for mainland market structure instead of assuming U.S.-style intraday execution.
6. Describe public REIT as a partial real-asset analogue with a separate project-level gate.
7. End with the strongest counterargument: the framework improves decision hygiene, not the odds by decree.
