# Artifact contract

The artifact contract is intentionally small because this is a research-framework memo rather than a named-issuer valuation report. It still requires traceable sources, a seven-manager translation matrix, a formal future-activation model, public-information underwriting gates, China-specific risk controls, a rendered PDF and independent final review.

The field-level owner, blocker and verifier definitions are in `artifact_contract.json`. No artifact may convert a descriptive manager process into a guaranteed or personalised investment result.
