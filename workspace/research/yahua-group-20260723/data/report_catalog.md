## Source-use policy

- Suggested total external sell-side **forecast-reconciliation cap: 20%** (东吴 10%, 国信 5%, 开源 5%) after the house EPS bridge is complete. The remaining 80% must be the house model, company disclosures, market data and normalized peer evidence.
- Suggested total external **target-price cap: 15%**, all from 东吴 because it is the only archived report with an explicit target and method. The target is one analyst’s July-7 view, not consensus.
- An external target cannot be mechanically rebased from its report-date price to the 2026-07-23 close. The report-date CNY24.65 implied +70.39%; CNY42 divided by the cutoff CNY17.75 would arithmetically be +136.62%, but this is stale-target arithmetic, **not** a current expected return.

## Excluded material

Search results, catalogue metadata, abstracts, media reposts and third-party previews remain discovery leads only. They carry zero forecast and valuation weight and do not populate this catalogue.
