# Broker-forecast reconciliation — 雅化集团（002497.SZ）

## Decision-use conclusion

The three complete broker PDFs establish a credible **external earnings range**, not a target-price consensus. Their 2026E revenue range is narrow (CNY16.16–17.05bn), while attributable-NP range is wide (CNY2.32–3.03bn), so the investment debate is conversion of price/volume gains into margin and cash—not merely revenue growth.

## Target-price evidence

东吴的 CNY42 target is explicitly 16x 2026E P/E and only one attributable target in the three-report set. It may be used as a **maximum 15% external sanity check** after the house valuation is complete. 国信和开源均无可归档目标价或方法；therefore no mean, median or “Street target” is calculated.

### Recommended architecture for the valuation workbook

| Input family | Evidence | Maximum contribution to a final target-price conclusion |
|---|---|---:|
| House segment earnings, cash and balance-sheet bridge | L1 issuer facts plus explicit house assumptions | 60%+ |
| House normalized multiple / SOTP logic | Own comparable normalization and risk adjustment | 20%+ |
| External broker forecast reconciliation | 东吴 / 国信 / 开源 PDFs | 20% combined |
| External broker target price | 东吴 only | 15% and contained within the external 20%; never additive with a peer-multiple check |

This architecture prohibits double-counting: do not add a broker target, a broker EPS and an L2 aggregated peer multiple as independent upside sources.
