### Important classification boundaries

- The cash and trading-financial-asset columns are not automatically unrestricted cash. A net-cash cross-check must state its simple definition and must not net derivative liabilities or lease liabilities without a specific model rule.
- Gross debt in this packet is strictly `short-term borrowings + current portion of non-current liabilities + long-term borrowings`; it excludes trade payables, derivative financial liabilities and lease liabilities. Those omitted items remain balance-sheet risks, not zero.
- 2026Q1 accounts receivable increased CNY654.487m from the 2025 year-end and inventory increased CNY176.960m. The company attributes the receivable increase to higher revenue; that explanation does not prove H1 cash conversion.
