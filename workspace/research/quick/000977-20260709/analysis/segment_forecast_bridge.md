# Segment Forecast Bridge

Segment disclosure is limited. The company reports server and component revenue as the dominant business, but does not disclose AI-server revenue, liquid-cooling revenue, or customer-by-customer AI orders. Therefore this bridge uses disclosed consolidated financials and broker forecast revisions as proxies.

| item | 2025A | 2026E bear | 2026E base | 2026E bull |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| consolidated revenue | CNY164.782bn | CNY230bn | CNY244bn | CNY260bn |
| parent net profit | CNY2.413bn | CNY4.45bn | CNY5.55bn | CNY6.85bn |
| EPS | 1.64 | 3.03 | 3.78 | 4.66 |
| implied net margin | 1.46% | 1.93% | 2.27% | 2.63% |
| broker reference | actual | below Kaiyuan/Capital | between Kaiyuan and Capital | above Capital, below 2027 Kaiyuan step-up |

Interpretation:
- The value is not in revenue growth alone. 2025 already had CNY164.8bn revenue but only CNY2.4bn profit.
- The investable bridge is net margin rising from 1.46% in 2025 to 2.2%-2.6% in 2026 through high-end AI server, liquid-cooling system mix, supply-chain management, and expense leverage.
- Segment purity is insufficient for a separate SOTP. Consolidated PE with working-capital risk discount is more appropriate than applying a high-growth multiple to all revenue.
