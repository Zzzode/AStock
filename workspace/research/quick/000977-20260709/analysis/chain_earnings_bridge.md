# Chain Earnings Bridge

## Theme-Level Profit Pool

AI server and AIDC demand converts into 000977 earnings through three linked variables:

1. Delivery volume: server and full-rack system shipments convert customer capex into revenue.
2. Product mix: AI server, liquid-cooling and super-node mix lifts gross margin versus traditional server delivery.
3. Working capital: inventory, receivables and contract liabilities determine whether accounting profit becomes cash.

## Next-Quarter Validation Thresholds

- H2 gross margin stays above 5.8%.
- H2 parent net profit is at least CNY2.5-3.0bn.
- Operating cash flow improves materially from Q1 CNY-7.772bn.
- Contract liabilities stop falling or management provides enough order visibility.
- Inventory and receivables remain explainable by order delivery, not demand weakness.

## Downgrade Evidence

- H2 parent net profit below CNY2.0bn.
- Gross margin below 5.5%.
- Operating cash flow remains negative while receivables and inventory rise.
- AI accelerator supply constraint or customer price concessions invalidate the margin reset.
