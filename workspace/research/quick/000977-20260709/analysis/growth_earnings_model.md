## Why Precision Modeling Is Required

浪潮信息的投资分歧来自 AI 服务器、液冷和超节点能否把“收入规模”转成“利润率修复”。由于公司没有披露 AI 服务器单独收入、单位出货和ASP，本模型不使用虚假的 unit * ASP 精度，而采用 H1业绩预告、Q1实际数、毛利率、合同负债、存货和券商预测作为可验证代理。

## Forecast Bridge

The H1 midpoint is CNY2.85bn parent net profit. A conservative full-year bridge assumes:
- H2 bear: CNY1.6bn, because Q2 spike partially normalizes.
- H2 base: CNY2.7bn, roughly matching H1 without assuming another Q2 step-up.
- H2 bull: CNY4.0bn, closer to Kaiyuan's 2027 ramp if high-end AI server mix continues.

## Downgrade Triggers

- 2026H2 parent net profit below CNY2.0bn.
- Gross margin falls back below 5.5% after Q2.
- Operating cash flow remains materially negative while receivables and inventory expand.
- Contract liabilities continue falling without new order evidence.
- Export control or accelerator supply constraints reduce delivery visibility.
