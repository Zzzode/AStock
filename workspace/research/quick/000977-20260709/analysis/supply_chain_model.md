# Supply-Chain Model: 000977 浪潮信息

Gate status: CONDITIONAL PASS for single-stock AIDC coverage.

## Chain Role

浪潮信息处在 AIDC 产业链的服务器/整柜/异构算力系统集成层。它不是上游 GPU、CPU、HBM、PCB、连接器或液冷零部件厂商，而是把上游 BOM 集成为 AI 服务器、超节点、液冷整机柜、存储和网络系统，并向互联网云、运营商、政企智算中心交付。

## Value-Chain Economics

浪潮信息的商业模式是大收入、薄毛利、强交付。2025A收入 CNY164.782bn、归母净利 CNY2.413bn，净利率只有1.46%；2026Q1毛利率修复到6.64%，归母净利 CNY0.605bn。H1预告把关键变量从“收入增长”切换到“高端AI服务器/液冷/供应链管理能否稳定提高利润率”。

Key economics:
- 2025前五客户销售占比71.29%，客户议价权强。
- 2025合同负债CNY19.506bn，2026Q1降至CNY12.252bn，说明预收款已部分结转发货，H2需要新增订单/预收款验证。
- 2026Q1存货CNY44.207bn，仍处高位；AI服务器交付需要备货，库存也会放大价格和技术迭代风险。
- 2026Q1经营现金流CNY-7.772bn，主要因预收客户货款节奏变化。利润兑现必须继续观察现金流和应收。

## Classification

Core valuation eligibility: eligible, but with working-capital cap.

Reason:
- Direct beneficiary of AIDC server demand.
- H1 earnings preannouncement confirms profit inflection.
- Broker forecasts were materially revised upward after the preannouncement.

Evidence gaps:
- AI server revenue split is not officially disclosed.
- Named CSP customer revenue is not officially disclosed.
- Unit shipment / ASP / accelerator allocation are not disclosed.

Valuation consequence:
- Use earnings and margin proxy rather than unsupported unit * ASP math.
- Bear case is triggered by weak H2 cash conversion, not simply by revenue slowdown.
