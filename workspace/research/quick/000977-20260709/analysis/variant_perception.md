# Variant Perception

## Market Consensus

The market is repricing 000977 as the clearest A-share AI-server earnings inflection after the H1 preannouncement. Consensus focuses on H1 NP +226%-288%, Q2 profit explosion, AI capex demand, liquid-cooling leadership, and broker forecast upgrades.

## AStock Differentiated View

The H1 preannouncement is not just a one-off beat; it changes the denominator. However, the stock should not be valued as a high-margin AI chip or optical module name. It remains a systems integrator with high customer concentration, high inventory, and working-capital pressure. The right framework is PE on upgraded earnings with a cash-flow discount, not pure theme valuation.

## Strongest Bull Argument

If H2 confirms that gross margin stays above 6% and Kaiyuan's 2027E EPS 5.17 becomes credible, the stock can move from CNY96 base target toward CNY120+ because 2027E PE would compress below 17x at the current price.

## Strongest Bear Argument

Q2 may be a delivery and mix peak. If contract liabilities continue to fall, operating cash flow stays negative, and CSP customers push price concessions, 2026E EPS could settle near CNY3.0 and fair value would fall toward CNY67-75.

## What Would Prove Us Wrong

- Bull upgrade: H2 NP above CNY3.5bn, positive operating cash flow, stable or rising contract liabilities, and no margin compression.
- Bear downgrade: H2 NP below CNY2.0bn, gross margin below 5.5%, receivable/inventory expansion without cash conversion, or accelerator supply disruption.
