April 2026 Market Forecast

April 2026
 

Report Abstract

This report provides a detailed forecast for optical transceivers and a few related products used in Ethernet, CWDM/DWDM, FTTx, wireless infrastructure, Cloud data center and AI Cluster applications. The forecast database includes historical data (2022-2025) and forecast (2026-2031) for shipments, pricing and sales of more than 200 different products.

Key inputs include an analysis of the business and infrastructure spending of the top 15 telecom service providers and of the leading cloud companies, and sales data for more than 30 transceiver vendors, including more than 20 vendors that shared their sales information with LightCounting. The forecast is based on LightCounting’s forecast model, which correlates transceiver sales with network traffic growth and increased investments in AI infrastructure.

LightCounting is also grateful to many industry experts for their critical review of the forecast projections.
