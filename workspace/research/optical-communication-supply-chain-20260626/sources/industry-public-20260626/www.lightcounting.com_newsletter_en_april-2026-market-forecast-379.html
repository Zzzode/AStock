Demand for optical connectivity continues to surprise

April 2026

LightCounting releases April 2026 Market Forecast report

The Ethernet transceiver market was up 93% in 2024 and our latest estimates for 2025 suggest another 82% growth. We now forecast 65% growth for 2026, but maintain more conservative projections for 2027-2031, as illustrated in the figure below.

The growth is once again limited by the production capacity of InP EML and laser chips. The current demand is exceeding supply by 30%, but the shortages should be going away by the end of 2026. Production of GPUs and other types of accelerators (XPUs) is a more critical bottleneck for the broader AI market and it will impact demand for optical transceivers as well. For example, even a minor glitch in the supply of GPUs will reduce demand for other products used in AI clusters, including transceivers. If TSMC could ship more GPU than expected, the shortages for optical transceivers will stretch into 2027 and the market growth will exceed our forecast.

Data on year-over-year growth in quarterly capex of cloud companies and sales of Ethernet transceivers for 2012-2025, presented in the report, shows that the cycles in capex and transceiver sales appear well correlated. It also shows that sales of transceivers spike early in the cycle and drop when capex is still growing. This can be explained by the supply chain dynamics. Shortages for transceivers early in the cycle lead to excessive ordering and more stable pricing. Once the suppliers add capacity and shortages ease, double ordering goes away and price declines accelerate. This will happen again in the current cycle, but we just don’t know when.

Sales of Active Optical Cables (AOCs) and DWDM transceivers were also lifted by the AI boom in 2025 and more growth is expected. An early surge in sales of AOCs (up 25% in 2024) was damped by Nvidia’s move away from AOCs to transceivers, but many other customers continue to use AOCs. We previously expected 53% growth for sales of AOCs in 2025, but sales data suggests a more modest 45%.

Sales of DWDM transceivers were up 12% in 2025, reversing two years of declines. Demand for connectivity in AI clusters distributed across multiple locations, also known as scale-across networks, will boost sales of 800G ZR/ZR+ in 2026 and 1.6T ZR/ZR+ in 2028-2029. This will keep the DWDM market growing at 13% CAGR in 2026-2031 and this forecast may be conservative.

There is no direct impact from AI on sales of FTTx and wireless fronthaul transceivers, but both market segments are expected to recover by 2028-2029. Deployments of 50G PON are starting in China now. First 6G wireless networks are expected as early as 2028 and wireless fronthaul networks will need upgrades ahead of wider deployments of 6G around 2030.

This report provides a detailed forecast for optical transceivers and a few related products used in Ethernet, CWDM/DWDM, FTTx, wireless infrastructure, Cloud data center and AI Cluster applications. The forecast database includes historical data (2022-2025) and forecast (2026-2031) for shipments, pricing and sales of more than 200 different products.
