## Evidence and Valuation Standard

- Market prices, price position, trading value, and quote timestamps must be archived as raw endpoint outputs.
- Financial facts must come from company/exchange announcements or official structured announcement feeds; media summaries cannot substitute for an official filing.
- Broker data must retain a source-quality label. Only original broker PDFs, broker official pages, or auditable consensus snapshots may receive positive Street valuation weight. All other sources receive zero weight.
- Each primary candidate receives a current-price valuation-eligibility disposition: a new range is published only if current price, normalized denominator, cash/driver evidence and a current auditable anchor pass together. Otherwise the case receives an explicit event-validation or watchlist-only boundary. Verification names may receive only a monitoring boundary if evidence does not support a defensible target.
- The report will use business-model-matched methods: cycle-normalized earnings / cash conversion for chemicals, aluminium and lithium; order/backlog and delivery quality for shipbuilding; and a cycle/working-capital discount for memory.

## Downgrade Path

- `core review` → `event-driven validation` if H1 earnings or cash conversion does not validate the driver.
- `event-driven validation` → `watchlist only` if the current valuation anchor, cash flow, or source-quality threshold fails.
- A historical high-upside model remains a `watchlist only` item unless a current price, a normalized denominator, and current high-quality evidence jointly support a new range.
