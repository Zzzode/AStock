# Template Brief

- **Archetype:** cross-sector valuation-refresh note with an evidence-led decision chapter.
- **Reader question:** did earlier low-position / high-upside conclusions survive the current price and latest issuer-disclosure refresh?
- **Narrative order:** answer first; market reset; financial-evidence split; broker-anchor boundary; historical-high-upside recheck; triggers and invalidation; sources.
- **Visual requirements:** one decision matrix, one price-position table, one financial-quality table and one external-anchor-versus-house-status table. Do not turn broker targets into charted house targets.
- **Mandatory caveat:** market measures use 2026-08-10 close because 2026-08-11 collection was pre-open.
- **Publication boundary:** educational investment research, not an order instruction or licensed public securities report.
