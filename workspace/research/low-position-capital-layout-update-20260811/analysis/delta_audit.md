## User Request

Refresh the historical low-position / high-upside research using the 2026-08-11 market snapshot and the latest available annual, quarterly, and interim-report information.

## Original Limitation

The predecessor reports are valid historical snapshots but their prices, scenario spaces, and evidence cutoffs end on 2026-07-15 and 2026-07-22. They cannot be reused as an August conclusion without a market, financial, and source refresh.

## Required Repair

1. Archive a new market snapshot and official financial disclosures.
2. Recheck broker-source quality rather than carrying forward stale targets.
3. Recalculate current-price valuation and explicitly downgrade names whose refreshed evidence is inadequate.
4. Retain the historic reports unchanged and state the conclusion delta in a new LaTeX/PDF update.

## Prevention Rule

Every time-sensitive valuation statement must show its market-data cutoff and must be regenerated, not merely repriced, when material new earnings disclosures become available.
