# Secondary-Market Analysis

## Price, volume and turnover

The relevant **price** is the 2026-08-10 close, because the 8/11 data capture was pre-open. Tencent adjusted series show 000703 at 99.96% of its one-year range, versus 30.94% for 002379, 22.45% for 300014 and 32.67% for 002497. 8/10 **turnover** was CNY2.07bn, CNY1.25bn, CNY2.19bn and CNY0.95bn respectively. Those values are direct Sohu observations, not price-times-volume estimates.

## Drawdown, relative performance and valuation crowding

000703 has virtually no **drawdown** from the adjusted one-year high and a +37.52% 20-day return. Its short-run **relative performance** versus the rest of this refreshed set is strong, but a benchmark-index comparison was not collected and is not inferred. That pattern raises **valuation crowding** risk: earnings can be correct while entry value is gone. 002379, 300014 and 002497 remain 38.79%, 39.16% and 38.46% below their respective one-year highs, but a large drawdown is a context variable rather than proof of value.

## Support, resistance and unavailable flow claims

The one-year low/range is a descriptive **support** reference, and the one-year high/range is a descriptive **resistance** reference; neither is used as a price target. We did not collect exchange **seat** data, named **institutional** holder-flow data, **northbound** flow, margin **financing** balances, or verified **hot-money** tags. Consequently the update makes no claim about **fund attitude**, dealer ownership or flow causality.

## Trading style and trend swing boundary

The report is not a tactical **trading style** signal. The observed **trend swing** is used only to prevent an old low-position label from being applied to a current high-range price. Any event-driven follow-up must be tied to actual earnings/cash disclosures rather than a chart-only trigger.
