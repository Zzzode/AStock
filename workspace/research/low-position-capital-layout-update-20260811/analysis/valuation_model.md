## Method and Assumption Bridge

The method is an eligibility framework, not a price engine. It requires: (1) a current completed-session price; (2) a reported or reconciled earnings denominator; (3) a cash/working-capital explanation; (4) a method-matched external or independently rebuilt valuation anchor; and (5) a dated validation condition. 000703 fails entry position/cash; 002379 fails actual/reconciled denominator; 300014 fails actual cash and target reconciliation; 002497 fails actual H1 and cash/driver validation.

## Full-Chain Classification Dependency

Company-specific evidence is required before any external ecosystem demand narrative can enter valuation. No broad demand, capacity, customer or theme claim substitutes for the issuer’s own revenue, margin, cash flow and accounting-quality disclosure. This disciplined dependency rule is why the historical high-upside cohort remains monitor-only.
