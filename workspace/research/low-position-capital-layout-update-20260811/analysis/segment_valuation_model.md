# Segment Valuation Eligibility Model

This refresh uses a business **segment** bridge to decide whether a valuation can be published. It is not a full **SOTP** report: the public evidence does not support a consistent asset-by-asset split across all four names. A reliable range would need visible **revenue**, normalized **net profit**, a business-model matched **multiple**, explicit **sensitivity** and a dated **validation trigger**. One or more requirements are incomplete in every current case.

| Ticker | Operating bridge available now | Missing valuation element | Validation trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| 000703 | Actual H1 profit; refinery/chemical earnings recovery | Cash conversion is negative and current price no longer offers a low-position entry point. | H2 margins, working-capital release and new risk/reward reset. |
| 002379 | Aluminium-cycle H1 profit preview and positive Q1 OCF | H1 actual revenue/OCF and statutory-to-adjusted profit reconciliation. | Actual H1 plus a reconciled, repeatable profit denominator. |
| 300014 | H1 profit preview and two original broker reports | Actual H1 revenue/OCF and one reconciled EPS/target construction. | Actual H1/Q3 operational and cash data. |
| 002497 | H1 profit preview plus a broker cycle framework | H2 profit, cash conversion and inventory/hedge normalization. | Actual H2 earnings/cash and lithium operating bridge. |

Because these gates are not all met, the update records external references rather than issuing a new scenario price, probability or multiple-derived house target.
