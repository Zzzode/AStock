## Invalidation Rules

- **000703:** it cannot be restored to a low-position list while price remains near the annual high or cash conversion weakens.
- **002379:** no target work begins until actual H1, OCF and adjusted/statutory profit reconciliation are available.
- **300014:** no target work begins until actual H1/Q3 revenue, OCF, operating profitability and the two broker denominators can be reconciled.
- **002497:** no target work begins until H2 supports the broker FY profit bridge and cash, inventory/hedge and lithium operating evidence normalize.
- **Historical high-upside names:** any missing current target, non-recurring driver or cash bridge keeps the status at watchlist-only.

## Year-End Timing Risk

An external broker target is generally assessed over its own institution’s stated rating horizon, not automatically by a calendar date. The 8/11 update does not convert a 6–12 month broker rating convention or a scenario bull case into a 2026 year-end commitment.
