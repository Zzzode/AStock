# Official Actual-Contract Daily-File Sample Manifest

Twelve raw responses are retained: SHFE and CZCE daily files for 2016-01-04,
2018-01-02, 2020-01-02, 2022-01-04, 2024-01-02 and 2026-08-13. Every sample
was retrieved retrospectively on 2026-08-14 and is hash-bound in
`manifest.json`.

This demonstrates that the public paths currently return dated actual-contract
rows; it does **not** prove point-in-time historical availability, a complete
archive, contract lifecycle, costs, margin, price-limit locks or executable
fills. It cannot support a futures return or action conclusion.
