## Use boundary

This is a dated market-context observation of an onshore government-bond yield
curve. It is not a tradable investor yield, ETF return, total-return input,
fee-inclusive return, execution record, forecast, allocation, holding or
action. It supports only the narrow research observation that a current
low-risk government-curve carry assumption cannot by itself substantiate a
stable 5% annual total-return claim. A bond ETF can have price, duration,
liquidity, premium/discount and execution outcomes that differ from the curve.
