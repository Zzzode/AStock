# DPSFS-R0-v1 Draft Rulebook

- Status: **draft, not frozen, not testable, `q=0`**.
- Objective boundary: any future assessment uses an ex-post, cost-aware,
  rolling-five-year TWRR criterion. It cannot be described as a guarantee,
  forecast, annual floor or current action.

## Listed-fund test hypothesis

Use total-return data only after a source-consistent event ledger exists. At the
first observed session of each month, use the preceding observed close; a
marked target cannot become effective before the following observed session.
Risk ETFs must have positive 252-observation total-return momentum and be above
their 200-observation total-return moving average. Rank eligible
510300/510880/518880, select at most two, and subject any mapping to a frozen
overlap-risk cluster rule. The old 50% risk-sleeve mapping appears only in an
exploratory public daily-bar diagnostic with unresolved adjustment basis; it is
not a usable allocation or a pre-registered test.

## Commodity-futures test hypothesis

Only actual AU/CU/TA contracts could be examined. The draft signal uses
21/63/126 observed settlement returns with 0.45/0.35/0.20 weights and a
60-session volatility estimate. A contract must independently pass lifecycle,
delivery-buffer, limit/status, liquidity, margin/fee, position-limit and broker
route checks. A signal at close cannot be recorded as a same-session fill.

If an eligible contract cannot be rolled, reduced or closed due to a price
limit, suspension, delivery restriction or missing liquidity, the ledger must
record a no-fill state rather than assume a transaction. Futures P&L is marked
at daily settlement with explicit fee, impact, roll and collateral conventions.

Neither hypothesis is frozen or approved. The JSON twin lists every required
freeze gate and preserves the absence of an approved numeric risk budget.
