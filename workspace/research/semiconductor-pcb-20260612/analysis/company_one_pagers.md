# Company One-Pager Inputs

## Purpose

Provide company-level research cards for the main report. These are not full models; they are evidence-weighted one-pagers based on the available corpus.

## 沪电股份

- Role: high-speed / high-layer AI PCB, backplane, data center PCB.
- Evidence: public Goldman article states AI PCB demand, backplane and CoWoP-related demand; 2026 capex may reach CNY 6.0bn versus CNY 3.0bn in 2025; target price CNY 142 based on 23x 2027E EPS.
- Upside driver: AI platform upgrade and product mix improvement.
- Verification needed: AI PCB revenue share, 2027E EPS detail, margin and utilization after capex ramp.
- Risk: customer volatility, capex digestion, valuation sensitivity.

## 胜宏科技

- Role: AI PCB, high-layer / HDI, high-beta platform exposure.
- Evidence: public JPM article frames Shenghong as AI PCB beneficiary, cites NVIDIA upgrade cycle, Google ASIC / TPU opportunity, H-share target HKD 600 and 2025-2028 net-profit CAGR 81%.
- Upside driver: strongest earnings elasticity if AI PCB / ASIC forecast is confirmed.
- Verification needed: original report, A/H comparability, customer share, capacity ramp and customer concentration.
- Risk: high expectation, forecast verification, security-class comparability.

## 深南电路

- Role: PCB + IC substrate dual driver.
- Evidence: 2025 revenue CNY 23.647bn, net profit CNY 3.276bn; 2026Q1 revenue CNY 6.596bn, net profit CNY 850mn; 2025 PCB revenue CNY 14.359bn and substrate revenue CNY 4.148bn; 2026-2028 net-profit forecasts CNY 5.678bn / 7.814bn / 10.625bn.
- Upside driver: AI compute PCB and packaging substrate both grow.
- Verification needed: customer platform split, FC-BGA/BT yield, substrate depreciation and margin.
- Risk: new capacity ramp, substrate certification, material cost pressure.

## 生益科技

- Role: high-speed CCL leader.
- Evidence: public Goldman article supports AI CCL price and M9 upgrade thesis; archived snapshot references target prices CNY 127.4 / 146.3.
- Upside driver: material price/mix upgrade and high-speed laminate scarcity.
- Verification needed: M8/M9 revenue share, customer certification, price increase durability.
- Risk: pricing reversal, raw material cost, customer share.

## 华正新材

- Role: high-speed CCL, CBF/BT substrate materials, domestic compute chain.
- Evidence: public Shenwan/CFI text cites CCL margin recovery and 2026-2028 revenue/net-profit forecasts CNY 6.39bn / 7.84bn / 9.07bn and CNY 470mn / 770mn / 970mn.

- Verification needed: CBF/BT customer validation, product yield, actual order conversion.
- Risk: valuation repricing ahead of evidence, R&D/yield, domestic compute demand.
