# Industry Landscape

## House Industry Map

The semiconductor PCB theme is best understood as an interconnect value-pool migration:

1. AI compute platforms raise signal speed, power density, and board density.
2. The old PCB stack cannot carry the new signal-integrity and thermal burden without more layers, lower-loss dielectric, tighter drilling/electroplating, and improved lamination.
3. Advanced packaging pushes part of the value pool toward substrate-like PCB, ABF/BT, glass substrate, and CoWoP-adjacent structures.
4. Material and process bottlenecks slow supply response, making the cycle less like commodity PCB and more like a qualification/capacity scarcity cycle.

## Segment Roles

| Segment | Function in AI hardware | Bottleneck | Representative names |
|---|---|---|---|
| High-end PCB | Connect GPU/ASIC, memory, switch, optical and rack-level systems | Layer count, line width/spacing, signal integrity, yield, customer qualification | 沪电股份, 胜宏科技, 深南电路, 鹏鼎控股, 景旺电子, 生益电子 |
| High-speed CCL | Dielectric and copper-clad base material that determines loss and reliability | M7/M8/M9/M10 material formulation, supply tightness, price pass-through | 生益科技, 华正新材, 南亚新材 |
| IC substrate | Package-level interconnect between chip and board | ABF/BT substrate, FC-BGA layers, high-density routing, qualification | 深南电路, 兴森科技, 华正新材 |
| Equipment | Enables fine process and capex expansion | Drilling, LDI, electroplating, lamination, inspection | 大族数控, 芯碁微装, 东威科技（非本报告覆盖ticker）, 凯格精机 |
| Consumables | High-frequency recurring demand tied to layer count and precision | High aspect-ratio drills, coated drills, carbide tools | 鼎泰高科, 中钨高新 |

## Technology Direction

| Old path | New path | Investment implication |
|---|---|---|
| General multilayer board | High-layer, high-speed AI board | Leaders with certified capacity gain order and ASP leverage. |
| Mid-grade CCL | M7/M8/M9/M10 high-speed laminate | CCL leaders can capture material scarcity and mix upgrade. |
| Board-level interconnect | Board + substrate + package-like interconnect | IC substrate and CoWoP-adjacent suppliers gain optionality. |
| Generic equipment capex | Precision drilling, LDI, electroplating, lamination | Equipment and consumables gain second-order exposure to technical inflation. |

## Key Uncertainty

The main uncertainty is not whether AI hardware uses more advanced interconnect. It is which physical layer captures the economics: PCB manufacturers, laminate/material suppliers, substrate makers, optical interconnect vendors, or packaging platforms.
