# House View

## Core Thesis

Our view is that the semiconductor PCB opportunity is real, but the investable edge is no longer the generic claim that "AI increases PCB demand." The edge is distinguishing which layers have confirmed delivery, pricing power, and valuation room after the market has already repriced the theme.

The cycle has three drivers:

1. **Platform-chain migration:** Blackwell-to-Rubin, ASIC servers, high-speed networking, and rack-level integration increase board complexity and value content.
2. **Material bottleneck:** M7/M8/M9/M10 high-speed CCL becomes a signal-integrity bottleneck rather than a commodity laminate input.
3. **Qualification scarcity:** High-layer PCB, FC-BGA/ABF/BT substrate, CBF and CoWoP-adjacent structures need customer certification and process yield, slowing supply response.

## Stance by Segment

| Segment | Stance | Rationale | Main invalidation |
|---|---|---|---|
| High-end AI PCB leaders | Core, but price-sensitive | Direct order link to AI servers/switches and best evidence density. | AI capex slowdown or backlog/margin miss. |
| High-speed CCL leaders | Core / defensive growth | Material bottleneck and price/mix upgrade can capture margin. | CCL tightness reverses or customers switch share. |
| IC substrate / packaging substrate | Watchlist / option value | Advanced packaging convergence is structurally attractive, but certification and yield are gating. | ABF/BT/glass route delays or depreciation pressure. |
| Equipment and consumables | Aggressive / cyclical beta | Benefits from capex and technical inflation, but order timing is volatile. | PCB capex slows after initial expansion. |
| Broad low-end PCB | Avoid / insufficient edge | Less direct link to AI value uplift and more exposed to commodity competition. | Not applicable. |

## Investment Guidance

The preferred approach is trigger-based:

1. Own or track core names only when backlog, margin, and customer-platform progress confirm the thesis.
2. Treat aggressive names as high-beta expressions; use valuation and evidence-quality gates.
3. Use watchlist names for follow-up research rather than immediate high-conviction conclusions.
4. Re-underwrite the theme if AI capex slows, M9 CCL tightness reverses, or platform architecture shifts value away from PCB.
