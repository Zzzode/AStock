# Publicly Available Research Sentiment

## Scope and Label

The available corpus is not a complete institutional consensus database. It now includes downloaded broker PDFs, public broker abstracts, public previews, official IR records and article reproductions. Therefore the report should call this section **Publicly Available Research Sentiment**, not full "Street Consensus".

## Common Bullish Views

1. AI server architecture upgrades drive both PCB volume and value-content growth.
2. Rubin / Rubin Ultra, ASIC servers, 800G / 1.6T networking, CoWoP-like integration, and orthogonal backplanes raise process barriers.
3. M7 / M8 / M9 / M10 laminate migration turns CCL from a generic input into a signal-integrity bottleneck.
4. IC substrate, ABF / BT, glass substrate, and substrate-like PCB blur the line between PCB and advanced packaging.
5. Equipment and consumables benefit from downstream capex plus technical inflation in drilling, LDI, electroplating, lamination, and drill bits.

## Divergence and Blind Spots

- Margin capture may sit with CCL/material suppliers in tight supply periods, but PCB manufacturers own the most direct AI platform order link.
- Rubin/CoWoP/orthogonal backplane adoption timing is not independently verified in the local corpus.
- Aggressive capex may create 2027-2028 oversupply risk if AI capex pauses.
- Glass substrate and optical interconnect could shift part of the value pool away from traditional high-layer PCB.
- Customer-level supply relationships are often broker-stated or inferred, not company-confirmed.
