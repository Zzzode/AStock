# Template Brief

## Archetype

This report uses a hybrid of:

- JPM equity research first-page dashboard for investable ticker framing.
- BlackRock outlook-in-charts style for thematic forces and scenario uncertainty.
- JPM Guide to the Markets discipline: each major conclusion must map to an exhibit or table.
- Vanguard-style separation of observed data, broker forecasts, our scenarios, and valuation risk.

## First-Page Dashboard Requirements

The first spread must show:

1. House view: AI PCB is a structural interconnect bottleneck, but current valuations require delivery discipline.
2. Segment stance: core, aggressive, watchlist, and insufficient-evidence categories.
3. Evidence-quality warning: source corpus now includes downloaded broker PDFs and official IR, but is still not a complete paid-terminal consensus/customer-chain dataset.
4. Data-date warning: Tencent quote snapshot refreshed 2026-06-16 for price/mcap/PE/PB; still not Wind/Choice standardized valuation.
5. Three investable conclusions: high-end PCB leaders, high-speed CCL/material leaders, substrate/equipment optionality.

## Chapter Sequence

1. Dashboard and house view.
2. Evidence governance and source quality.
3. Technology principles: AI server interconnect, CCL grade migration, CoWoP/orthogonal backplane, substrate-like PCB.
4. Industry chain and supply-chain relationship matrix.
5. Company mapping and financial delivery.
6. Publicly available research sentiment.
7. Valuation and broker target audit.
8. Scenario framework and investment guidance.
9. Risks, monitoring triggers, and invalidation conditions.
10. Appendix: source registry and claim audit summary.

## Required Exhibits

| Exhibit | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Segment stance dashboard | Make the investment conclusion visible before detail. |
| Technology migration map | Explain why PCB value content rises. |
| Supply-chain relationship matrix | Label confidence and avoid rumor leakage. |
| Financial delivery table | Compare reported delivery and broker forecasts. |
| Broker target/rating table | Separate target prices from house scenarios. |
| Valuation sensitivity table | Avoid fake precision from stale prices. |
| Risk trigger table | Convert risk list into observable monitoring points. |

## Avoid

- Do not write "券商认为" as the report's own thesis.
- Do not apply H-share targets directly to A-share prices.
- Do not invent target prices where broker targets are unavailable.
- Do not hide weak source quality in an appendix only.
- Do not use long undifferentiated issuer lists without investability categories.
