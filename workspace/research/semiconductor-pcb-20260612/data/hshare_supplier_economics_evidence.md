# H-Share Supplier Economics Evidence

**Source:** HKEX H-share application/global-offering documents in `workspace/research/semiconductor-pcb-20260612/sources/official-hshare-prospectus-20260616/`.

**Purpose:** Extract supplier concentration, raw-material cost share, supplier type and credit-term evidence to support margin and working-capital assumptions.

## Hudian supplier and raw-material evidence

| Metric | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026Q1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Raw-material cost / cost of sales | 55.8% | 56.5% | 60.7% | 62.3% |
| Top-five supplier purchase | 21.79亿元 | 39.36亿元 | 59.73亿元 | 21.79亿元 |
| Top-five supplier purchase share | 41.3% | 40.5% | 42.2% | 41.6% |
| Largest supplier share | 21.4% | 21.1% | 22.3% | 21.2% |

**Hudian top suppliers:** mainly CCL and prepreg suppliers; credit terms include 60 days, 90 days, 90-120 days, 120 days and 120-150 days. Supplier F provides production equipment with 30-day credit term.

## Shenghong supplier and raw-material evidence

| Metric | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Raw-material cost / cost of sales | 58.6% | 62.7% | 65.9% |
| Top-five supplier purchase | 19.75亿元 | 24.28亿元 | 56.58亿元 |
| Top-five supplier purchase share | 31.4% | 29.3% | 45.2% |
| Largest supplier share | 11.6% | 9.4% | 15.0% |

**Shenghong supplier profile:** Supplier A is a Korea-headquartered global electronic materials supplier; Supplier B is a Guangdong high-end CCL developer listed in Shanghai; Supplier C is a Taiwan electronic circuit materials company; Supplier H is a Guangdong laser intelligent equipment company.

## Interpretation

- Raw-material cost share is rising for both Hudian and Shenghong, making material-price pass-through and supplier terms central to gross-margin sustainability.
- Hudian supplier concentration is structurally high with top-five suppliers around 40%+ of purchases and largest supplier around 21%.
- Shenghong supplier concentration rose sharply to 45.2% in 2025, consistent with high-end material/equipment needs during AI/HPC ramp.
- Supplier credit terms support DPO assumptions and working-capital modeling, but supplier identities are mostly anonymized.
