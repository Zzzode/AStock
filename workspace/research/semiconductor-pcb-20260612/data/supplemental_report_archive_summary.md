## Official IR Impact

- Hudian official IR discloses 2026Q1 revenue 62.14亿元 and NPP 12.42亿元; 2025 high-speed switch and AI-server-related products were about 59% of revenue, general server 13.4%, wireless communication about 5%, intelligent automotive about 16.1%.
- Hudian Thailand data-communication business had over 70% of overseas customers certified and 2026Q1 utilization above 90%.
- Shenghong official IR confirms abundant orders, smooth large-customer and ASIC-customer progress, deeper GPU accelerator card and TPU board supply scale, but does not disclose customer names or revenue amounts.
- Shennan official IR confirms 2026Q1 revenue 65.96亿元 and NPP 8.50亿元; 400G+ switch and optical-module mix increased; FC-BGA 22-layer and below mass production, 24-layer and above in R&D/sample stage.

## Boundary

The supplemental archive materially improves original-report depth, official-company evidence and EPS/model coverage. It still does not close the named customer/platform revenue split or bottom-up customer-level EPS model because the new sources do not disclose customer-level revenue, ASP, shipment, margin, depreciation or working-capital assumptions.
