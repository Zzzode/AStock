# Customer-Side Public Source Probe

**Run date:** 2026-06-16

**Purpose:** Test whether the remaining named platform/customer revenue-split gap can be closed from customer-side public sources rather than issuer or broker sources. Targets included NVIDIA, Google TPU, Microsoft Azure AI servers, AWS Trainium / cloud AI infrastructure and Apple supply-chain public pages.

## Examples of Rejected Source Types

- SEO / marketing pages listing "top PCB manufacturers".
- Industry blogs and non-official English summaries that mention NVIDIA or AI PCB suppliers without source documents.
- Social reposts or wealth-account style summaries.
- Company profile pages describing broad customer groups or "global AI clients" without named-platform revenue.

## Conclusion

Customer-side public sources did not provide official, attributable supplier lists or revenue splits for NVIDIA / Google TPU / Microsoft Azure / AWS Trainium / Apple PCB or CCL supply chains. This reinforces the existing disclosure-boundary conclusion:

1. Public issuer filings and IR records disclose segment/application exposure, customer categories, certifications and anonymized customer concentration.
2. Brokers and reposts state platform-chain exposure, but original complete global-broker PDFs remain missing for some named claims.
3. Customer-side public pages do not disclose named platform/customer revenue by A-share supplier.

The named platform/customer revenue split remains blocked by non-public customer/supplier confirmation, paid terminal datasets, or direct industry-chain research.
