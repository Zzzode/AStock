# CMBI Sector Global Evidence

**Source:** CMBI / 招银国际, *Semiconductors PCB/CCL sector - AI-driven upcycle: structural growth with diverging dynamics*, 2025-11-10.

**Local archive:** `workspace/reports/semiconductor-pcb-sector-global-20260616/01-cmbi-semiconductor-pcb-ccl-sector-ai-driven-upcycle.pdf`

## Evidence extracted for report

| Topic | CMBI / Prismark evidence | Report use |
|---|---|---|
| Global PCB cycle | Global PCB sales reached about US$74bn in 2024 after +5.8% YoY growth; Prismark revised 2025E growth to 12.8% from 7.6%. | Cross-checks the report's AI-driven upcycle framing. |
| Product mix | Multi-layer 18L+, HDI and substrates are expected to be standout performers, driven by AI servers and high-speed networking. | Supports the high-end PCB / HDI / substrate bottleneck thesis. |
| China manufacturing share | China accounted for about 56% of global PCB production value in 2024. | Supports A-share relevance to global PCB supply. |
| CCL cycle | Global CCL market was estimated at US$15bn in 2024, +18% YoY; high-speed specialty CCL grew about 50% YoY. | Supports high-speed CCL structural pricing power. |
| Material generation | M7/M8 grades are becoming baseline for 112G applications, with M9 supporting 224G on the horizon; advanced PPO, HVLP4 and Q glass raise barriers. | Supports technology-generation narrative without implying issuer-specific revenue. |
| Competitive concentration | Global CCL top 10 suppliers held about 77% of market sales in 2024; top four included Kingboard, Shengyi Tech, EMC and Nanya Plastics with about 50% share. | Supports CCL pricing-power contrast versus fragmented PCB. |
| PCB fragmentation | Top 40 PCB manufacturers collectively account for just over half of total industry sales. | Supports differentiation among high-end PCB suppliers. |
| Cost pass-through | LME copper rose about 24% YTD in the report period; copper constitutes 60%-70% of PCB raw-material cost; high-end AI HDI products showed better margins than standard PCB. | Cross-checks supplier/material-cost risk and margin sensitivity. |

## Boundary

This is a sector update, not a company-specific original JPM/Goldman replacement. It does not provide named NVIDIA / Google ASIC / domestic compute / optical-module revenue splits or customer/platform EPS assumptions.
