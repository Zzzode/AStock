## Treatment

- Use as official parent-company read-through for application mix and product momentum.
- Do not use as named Nvidia / Google / platform revenue split.
- Customer X is anonymized in both English translation and Chinese original annual report text and cannot be mapped to Apple, NVIDIA, Google or any named platform from public text.
- Pair with Pengding A-share monthly revenue, official filings and IR evidence for watchlist delivery tracking.
