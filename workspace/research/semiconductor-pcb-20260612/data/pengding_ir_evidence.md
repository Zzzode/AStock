# Pengding Investor Relations Evidence

**Source archive:** `workspace/reports/semiconductor-pcb-pengding-ir-20260616/`

**Source type:** Official investor-relations records / issuer Q&A.

## Key Evidence

| Topic | Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Compute-customer certification | 2026-01 IR says compute customer certification is progressing smoothly, and 2026 is expected to be the first year of direct compute-customer order imports. | Does not disclose customer names or order value. |
| Single customer question | 2026-04 results briefing includes a question asking when Nvidia high-end board orders can be mass produced. The issuer answered that compute customer certification is progressing smoothly and that 2026 is expected to be the first year of direct compute-customer order imports, but it is not convenient to comment on a single customer. | Do not treat as confirmed Nvidia order or revenue. |
| AI server product progress | 2026-04 results briefing says high-end HDI and HLC products have successfully entered the AI-server market and are gradually achieving mass production. | Product progress, not platform revenue split. |
| Server / automotive revenue | 2026-04 results briefing says automotive and server boards revenue was 10.25亿元 in 2024 (+90.34%) and 21.19亿元 in 2025 (+106.67%). | Combined segment, not pure AI-server revenue. |
| Customer cooperation | 2026-04 results briefing says the company has deep cooperation with server terminal customers, high-end HDI entered AI servers, and products passed cloud-service vendor certification. | Customer names remain undisclosed. |
| Capacity ramp | 2026-04 results briefing says Huai'an and Thailand are core AI capacity bases; Thailand Phase I is in yield-ramp stage and is expected to add more than 10亿元 output value after reaching full production. | Output-value estimate, not net profit or platform split. |
| Optical modules | 2026-04 results briefing says SLP products entered 800G/1.6T optical-module high-end market and will realize mass shipments; 3.2T products are in R&D. Q1 exchange says optical-module business growth is significant and expected to grow several-fold this year. | No named optical customer or order amount. |
| Depreciation and margin | 2026 Q1 exchange says fixed-asset depreciation will be recognized in phases; optical-module boards and high-end HDI/HLC for compute have strong demand and profitability, and added capacity should absorb depreciation pressure. Q1 margin improved mainly due to product mix optimization and higher high-value-added product share. | Qualitative margin/depreciation evidence, not full operating-line model. |
| Technology moat | Q1 exchange says becoming a major AI PCB supplier requires rapid response to AI customer iteration; company cites high-precision PCB R&D and mass-production experience, global delivery and customer-service system, and AI-enabled R&D/manufacturing/management. | Qualitative moat evidence. |

## Treatment

- Use this as official issuer evidence for Pengding's watchlist status, AI-server/optical-module validation, capacity ramp and margin/depreciation risk.
- Do not use it to confirm Nvidia, Google, Meta, Microsoft or any named platform revenue.
- It improves customer-chain validation but does not close named customer/platform revenue split or bottom-up EPS assumptions.
