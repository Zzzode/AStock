## Interpretation

- Huatai and Zheshang both provide positive forecast-line evidence for Pengding but differ on revenue, net profit and implied PE.
- The evidence upgrades Pengding from no model coverage to watchlist forecast-line coverage.
- It still cannot support customer/platform EPS attribution because named platform revenue, ASP, shipments, gross margin and depreciation by project are not disclosed.
