## Interpretation

- Hudian’s customer F rose to 19.5% of 2026Q1 revenue, while customer E became the largest in 2025; this is a concrete customer concentration time-series even though names remain anonymized.
- Shenghong’s customer A rose from 5.7% of 2023 revenue to 29.7% of 2025 revenue, explaining the jump in customer concentration and validating high dependence on a major AI/HPC chain customer.
- Customer E in Shenghong’s table is the strongest official anonymous AI infrastructure customer descriptor found, but it still does not provide the customer’s name or platform revenue.
- These tables materially improve customer revenue bridging, but they do not satisfy the unresolved requirement for named NVIDIA / Google ASIC / domestic compute / optical module revenue split.
