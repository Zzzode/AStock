## Customer / market evidence

- The project is positioned for high-speed, high-density, high-layer PCB used in AI servers and switches.
- The issuer states that AI compute demand and high-speed transmission requirements are driving PCB products toward high-density interconnect, multilayer design and signal-integrity requirements.
- The issuer states its AI compute PCB products are already widely used in AI servers and switches, with related capacity utilization high.
- The issuer says it has deep strategic cooperation with multiple leading AI server manufacturers, switch manufacturers, cloud-service providers and chip companies.
- Existing AI compute PCB orders are described as full, and core customers are described as having clear capacity-demand intentions.
- Feasibility analysis cites Prismark: 2024 global server/data-storage PCB market size was US$10.916bn, +33.1% YoY; 2029E expected to reach US$25.729bn, implying 18.7% CAGR.
- The issuer states it has mastered high-speed signal-integrity design, high-precision multilayer lamination and laser micro-blind/buried-via processes, and has achieved batch delivery of high-density, high-speed, high-layer PCB.
- As of 2025 year-end, the issuer had 21,211 employees, including 5,239 employees with bachelor's degree or above and 5,416 technical staff.
