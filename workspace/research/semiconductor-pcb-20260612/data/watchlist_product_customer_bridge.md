## Interpretation

- Nanya provides the strongest watchlist materials evidence because official inquiry materials disclose product-line revenue, gross margin and named PCB customers by CCL / prepreg category.
- Circuit Fabology provides the strongest watchlist equipment customer-chain evidence because the official annual report names major PCB customers that overlap with the report's core AI PCB beneficiaries.
- Xingsen adds an important warning: IC substrate growth is strong, but FCBGA ramp cost and negative IC-substrate gross margin matter more for EPS sensitivity than revenue growth alone.
- Han's CNC and Dingtai add the clearest official equipment/consumables read-through for AI-server PCB capacity expansion, while Jintuo remains a looser PCBA assembly-equipment proxy.
- These data improve the public customer-chain bridge but still do not solve the strict top-tier gap: named terminal-platform revenue and customer-by-platform EPS attribution remain non-public.
