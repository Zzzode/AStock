- Hudian provides the strongest official utilization evidence: Thailand data-communication utilization above 90% in 2026Q1.
- Shennan provides the clearest official depreciation/ramp warning: Nantong IV and Thailand ramp can pressure profit before output scales.
- Huazheng provides the clearest official fixed-asset / impairment evidence and an overseas capex plan, while the Zheshang report supplies a capacity-to-sales bridge for Zhuhai high-grade CCL.
- These data materially improve the EPS sensitivity foundation, but still do not replace customer/platform revenue, ASP, shipment, segment margin or project-level depreciation schedules.
