## Key Evidence

| Topic | Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| AI/HPC PCB market position | Frost & Sullivan cited the company as No. 1 globally by sales revenue in AI and high-performance computing PCB in 1H2025; it also ranked No. 1 in high-build-up HDI PCB and high-layer-count MLPCB with 14+ layers in the relevant categories. | Filing excerpt; not a broker target-price report. |
| Revenue and net profit | Revenue was RMB7.885bn / RMB7.931bn / RMB10.732bn in 2022/2023/2024 and RMB14.117bn in 9M2025. Net profit was RMB0.791bn / RMB0.671bn / RMB1.154bn and RMB3.245bn, respectively. | Does not provide platform-specific revenue. |
| Customer concentration | Top-five customers accounted for 28.0% / 27.1% / 25.1% / 50.9% of revenue in 2022/2023/2024/9M2025. Largest customer accounted for 7.6% / 6.3% / 8.3% / 29.6%. | Customer names are anonymized. |
| Customer E | Customer E is described as a global leading technology company focusing on accelerated computing and AI infrastructure, headquartered in the United States, listed on NASDAQ. 9M2025 sales to Customer E were RMB592.3mn, or 4.2% of revenue. | Do not map to a named company without issuer confirmation. |
| Customer A | 9M2025 sales to Customer A were RMB4.172bn, or 29.6% of revenue; Customer A is described as one of the world's largest original equipment manufacturers for electronics headquartered in Taiwan. | Anonymous customer code, not a named platform split. |
| Technology capability | Products cover AI accelerator cards, servers, AI servers, data-center switches, UBB and optical transceivers. The company can manufacture 70+ layer MLPCBs and has technical capability for 100+ layer MLPCBs; it has mass production capability for 24-layer HDIs with 6+12+6 build-up and 28-layer HDIs with 8+12+8 build-up. | Supports technology ranking, not customer revenue. |
| Product mix | For 9M2025, MLPCBs with more than 8 layers accounted for about 52.1% of total MLPCB revenue, and 14+ layer MLPCBs accounted for about 19.6%. HDIs with 6+N+6 or above accounted for about 77.3% of total HDI revenue. | Product-class mix, not application revenue. |
| Capacity and utilization | Main product capacity/utilization: 2024 single/double-layer PCB and MLPCB capacity 8.652mn sqm at 90.4% utilization; HDI capacity 0.930mn sqm at 69.6% utilization. 9M2025 HDI capacity was 0.436mn sqm at 91.1% utilization. New Vietnam and Thailand facilities are under construction / ramp. | Capacity is aggregated by product category. |
