- All five core names had higher shareholder counts in 2026Q1 than in 2025Q4.

- This complements the fund-heavy-holding bridge: fund-heavy-holding MV fell in 2026Q1 while shareholder count rose, suggesting visible public-fund exposure declined even as the shareholder base broadened.
- This remains a public concentration proxy, not a beneficial-owner or institution-level position dataset.
