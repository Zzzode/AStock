## Interpretation

- Hudian’s utilization is effectively full across major bases in 2026Q1, which supports the capacity-bottleneck and capex-conversion thesis.
- Shenghong’s HDI utilization jumped to 97.7% in 2025, matching the ASP surge and high-end HDI thesis.
- Shenghong has disclosed substantial planned capacity additions, but these are not customer-specific orders or guaranteed revenue.
- Capacity utilization and expansion data improve bottom-up EPS assumptions but still do not disclose named customer/platform revenue or margins.
