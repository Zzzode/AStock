- Pengding, Shenghong and Xinqi show OCF above NPP in 2026Q1, supporting near-term earnings quality, though Pengding's outsized OCF/NPP mainly reflects receivable collection.

- Shennan has positive but low OCF/NPP in 2026Q1, consistent with material procurement and ramp/capex pressure.
- These checks improve EPS quality assessment but still do not replace customer/platform bottom-up assumptions.
