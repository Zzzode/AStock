## Missing Data

For a model-grade report, the following still need official filings or Wind/Choice/Bloomberg style data:

- Full 2023-2026 quarterly financials for every ticker.
- Segment revenue and margin history for all core names.
- Shares outstanding, diluted EPS, net cash/debt, capex, backlog and utilization.
- Broker forecast ranges rather than single public excerpts.
