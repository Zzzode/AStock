## HKEX Northbound Disclosure Rule

HKEX Stock Connect Northbound Shareholding Search pages state that starting from 2024-08-19, Northbound shareholding information is only available on a quarterly basis, with the preceding quarter published on the 5th Northbound trading day following quarter end. This explains why direct Eastmoney/HKEX-style APIs return data up to 2024-08-16 for daily windows but do not provide current daily northbound holdings.

Implication: the report should not require daily northbound holdings as a public-data completion criterion after this rule change. Quarterly official/Exchange-derived holder proxies are the appropriate public-source boundary.
