## Interpretation

- Hudian disclosed improving receivable days through 2026Q1, while inventory and payable days lengthened, consistent with high-end mix and material procurement needs.
- Shenghong disclosed improving receivable days and sharply longer payable days in 2025, consistent with strong OCF and supplier-credit support during AI/HPC ramp.
- These official turnover days are stronger than our simple quarter-end approximations, but only cover H-share document issuers and remain non-customer-specific.
