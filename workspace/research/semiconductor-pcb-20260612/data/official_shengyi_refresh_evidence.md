**Official read-through:**

- 2025 1-9M, Shengyi Electronics' main customers shifted toward server/computer PCB, and server/computer board became the largest application field with 68.01% revenue share.

## Application mix and margin bridge

| Application | 2022 revenue share | 2023 revenue share | 2024 revenue share | 2025 1-9M revenue share | Official read-through |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Server / computer board | 17.92% | 24.39% | 48.96% | 68.01% | Became the largest application field in 2024 and increased further in 2025 1-9M. |
| Communication network equipment board | 60.92% | 48.60% | 28.81% | 19.86% | Former core application; share declined as server/computer board rose. |
| Automotive electronics board | 11.18% | 17.22% | 13.25% | 6.82% | Helped offset weak communications demand in 2023 but became less important by 2025 1-9M. |
| Other board | 9.98% | 9.79% | 8.98% | 5.31% | Smaller contribution. |
| Main-business gross margin | 21.24% | 11.13% | 19.42% | 29.60% | Gross margin recovered sharply as AI server/high-value PCB mix and pricing improved. |

**Official margin explanation:**

- 2023 server/computer board gross margin declined because AI server cards began to ramp, material consumption per unit was high, and Dongcheng Phase IV depreciation lifted manufacturing cost.
- 2024 and 2025 1-9M server/computer board gross margin rose sharply because selling price increased materially and AI data-center demand drove high-value PCB mix.
- Communication network equipment board margin declined in 2023 due to weak demand and price pressure, then recovered from 2024 as AI data-center investment and high-speed networking demand improved pricing.
- The issuer states that server/computer board surpassed communication network equipment board as the largest revenue source and became the main driver of performance improvement.

**Official calculation notes:**

- The issuer assumes production equals sales for both projects, supported by high historical production/sales ratios.

- The issuer states project revenue/profit can cover incremental depreciation, amortization and project-building costs, but early ramp can pressure profitability.
