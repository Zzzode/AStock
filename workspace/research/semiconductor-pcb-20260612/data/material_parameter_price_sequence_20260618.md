## What Improved

- Shengyi now has company-website Dk/Df points for Synamic9GN and Synamic8GN.
- Shengyi now has official TDS and Line Up PDFs for Synamic9GN / Synamic8GN, including multi-frequency Dk/Df tables.
- Shengyi Synamic10GQ / M10 official product page was not found in site:syst.com.cn search or archived Shengyi product lists; Synamic9GN remains the highest directly archived Synamic high-speed CCL route.
- Nanya now has company-website Dk/Df points for NYHP-7300 / NYHP-7300 L and official M6-M10 certification/ramp evidence.
- Nanya now has additional official PDF specification sheets for NYHP-7003C and NY6600; NYHP-7350D and PTFE search-result links returned HTML 404 pages and are retained only as failed-route evidence.
- Huazheng now has official product-list and Line-up Dk/Df evidence for HSD8 / HSD8(K), with broker-extracted HSD8 data retained as cross-check, plus official Ultra / Extreme low-loss certification and batch-order evidence.
- Sector-level input cost evidence is separated from company CCL ASP evidence.

## Remaining Hard Gaps

1. M8/M9/M10 revenue share by company and period.
2. Continuous ASP and delivery-time sequence.
3. Customer certification list and dates by material generation.
4. Product gross margin by material generation.
5. Company-official Dk/Df datasheets for every M8/M9/M10 product.
