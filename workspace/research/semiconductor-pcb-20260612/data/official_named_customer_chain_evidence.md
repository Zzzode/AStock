## Interpretation

- Nanya New Materials has the strongest official named/semi-named customer-chain evidence among the watchlist names.
- This evidence upgrades Nanya's role in the report: it is not merely a generic CCL watchlist name; it has official high-speed material certification and named terminal-customer evidence.
- The evidence still does not close the core unresolved requirement: named platform/customer revenue split by NVIDIA, Google ASIC, domestic compute or optical-module chain.
- For the main report, use this as official customer-chain evidence for the watchlist/CCL segment, not as confirmed revenue exposure.
