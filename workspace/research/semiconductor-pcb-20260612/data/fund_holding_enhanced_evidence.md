## Interpretation

- CNInfo provides a market-wide ranking and fund coverage count for each report period.
- Sina provides fund-name-level holdings for each stock and can reveal concentration in specific active/passive products, but the dataset itself does not tag active vs passive.
- The combination improves institutional-holder evidence, but still does not replace Wind/Choice fund ownership history or terminal-grade active/passive classification.
