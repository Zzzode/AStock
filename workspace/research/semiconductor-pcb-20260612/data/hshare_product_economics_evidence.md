## Interpretation

- Hudian H-share application provides a layer-by-layer revenue and volume bridge. The 32-layer+ revenue share rose to 32.1% in 2026Q1, supporting the high-end layer-mix thesis.
- Shenghong H-share global offering provides direct product ASP evidence. HDI ASP rose from 2,351元/sqm in 2024 to 13,475元/sqm in 2025 as product mix shifted to higher-order HDI for AI/high-performance applications.
- These are the strongest public bottom-up economics found so far, but they still do not identify revenue by named end customer or platform.
