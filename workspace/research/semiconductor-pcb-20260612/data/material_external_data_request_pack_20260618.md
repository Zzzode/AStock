## Completion Test

1. At least one attributable source provides product-level ASP or directly confirms it cannot be disclosed.
2. At least one attributable source provides revenue share by material generation or directly confirms it cannot be disclosed.
3. Certification list or explicit disclosure boundary is available by product generation.
4. Delivery lead-time / backlog / allocation is available or explicitly confirmed unavailable.

## Sendable Questions

1. Can the company disclose quarterly ASP by high-speed material generation or product code?
2. Can the company disclose revenue share for M8/M9/M10 or equivalent products?
3. Can the company disclose product gross margin or margin premium versus commodity FR-4?
4. Can the company disclose customer certification names, dates, stages and batch-supply start dates?
5. Can the company disclose lead time, backlog, allocation or shipment trend by material generation?
6. If any field cannot be disclosed, what is the highest company-approved granularity?
