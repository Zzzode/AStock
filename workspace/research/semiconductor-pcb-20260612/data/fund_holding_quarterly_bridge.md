## Interpretation

- Public fund heavy-holding exposure expanded materially into 2025Q4 across almost all covered names, then contracted sharply in 2026Q1.
- The 2026Q1 contraction is especially large for Shenghong, Shengyi, Hudian and Shennan by visible market value and fund count.
- Watchlist beta is more selective: Dtech and Circuit Fabology retained more visible 2026Q1 market value than Fastprint, while Han's CNC market value stayed close to 2025Q4 despite fund count falling.
- This bridge improves historical public fund-positioning evidence, but it does not identify active/passive ownership comprehensively, beneficial owners, intraday flows or paid-terminal institution categories.
