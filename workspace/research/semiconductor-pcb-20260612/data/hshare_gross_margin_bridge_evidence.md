## Interpretation

- Hudian data-communication PCB gross margin reached 39.6% in 2026Q1, materially above smart automotive PCB at 19.6%, confirming that application mix is central to EPS.
- Shenghong HDI gross margin rose from 22.5% in 2024 to 43.5% in 2025, matching the HDI ASP jump and explaining the large 2025 earnings acceleration.
- This is a major public-source improvement for bottom-up EPS, but it still does not disclose named customer/platform gross margin.
