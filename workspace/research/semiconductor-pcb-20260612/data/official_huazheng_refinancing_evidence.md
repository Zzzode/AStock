## Investment rationale and product evidence

- The project responds to AI server, high-speed switch and optical-module demand for high-frequency/high-speed CCL with low dielectric loss, high heat resistance and high stability.
- The issuer says domestic market share of conventional FR-4 CCL has improved, but high-end rigid CCL categories such as high-speed, high-frequency and IC packaging materials remain dominated by Taiwan, Japan and Korea suppliers.
- The document cites Prismark: 2024 global top-five share in three high-end rigid CCL categories was 62.60%.
- The issuer states that its high-speed CCL product matrix is becoming more complete, technical indicators are approaching international advanced levels and some terminal customers have batch applications.
- Halogenated Ultra Low-loss materials have achieved batch sales.
- Halogen-free Ultra Low-loss materials have passed validation at one important end customer and are actively being certified by several important end customers.
- High-frequency CCL products have existing market share; the project aims to strengthen high-end capacity and market competitiveness.

## Boundary

The feasibility report improves official high-grade CCL capacity and product-generation evidence for Huazheng. The dilution announcement adds official EPS dilution scenarios. However, the official documents still do not provide a quantitative project operating model. P5W/SSE interaction evidence separately supports that CBF has small-batch orders for some items and class-BT/BT materials have market application, but the filings do not disclose CBF/BT project economics, named AI/server/switch/optical-module customer revenue, M8/M9 revenue share, product ASP, project gross margin, project net-profit forecast, IRR, payback period or customer-specific order amount.
