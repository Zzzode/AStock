# Working Capital Days Analysis

**Source:** Archived official 2026Q1 reports and `official_financials.json`.

**Method:** Quarter-end balance sheet divided by Q1 revenue/cost and multiplied by 90 days. DSO = AR / revenue * 90; DIO = inventory / COGS * 90; DPO = AP / COGS * 90; CCC = DSO + DIO - DPO.

**Boundary:** This is a quarterly working-capital approximation. It is not a customer/platform bottom-up EPS model and does not replace full D&A/working-capital schedules.

## Interpretation

- Shennan, Shengyi, Xingsen, Nanya and Xinqi show long cash-conversion cycles by Q1 approximation, so revenue growth needs cash-flow validation.
- Shenghong has negative/very low CCC because payables exceed AR plus inventory on this approximation, while Pengding's low CCC is driven by fast receivable collection; both are consistent with strong Q1 OCF but require monitoring of sustainability.
- Contract liabilities are meaningful for Shennan, Dazu, Xinqi and Jintuo, providing a partial order/advance-payment proxy but not a backlog equivalent.
- These metrics improve working-capital assumptions for EPS quality, but still do not provide customer/platform revenue, ASP or shipment assumptions.
