## AI / Technology / Capacity Evidence

- 2025 annual report says automotive/server boards and other boards revenue reached 21.19亿元, +106.67% YoY, driven by AI server market demand.
- It states the company is advancing certification and sample builds for well-known customers' next-generation AI-server products, and expanding cooperation with cloud-server vendors on AI ASIC-related products.
- It states AI server products achieved revenue growth of more than 1x in 2025, and are expected to keep strong growth in 2026.
- Optical communication: SLP products entered 800G/1.6T optical modules from 2024; 3.2T products are in R&D/design.
- Thailand Phase I began trial production in May 2025, mainly serving AI server, automotive and optical-communication products; server and optical-module products passed customer certification, with several global leading customers still under certification.
- 2026 capex plan is 168亿元, mainly for Huai'an industrial park and Thailand production base.

## Boundary

This official evidence materially upgrades Pengding's watchlist status and official delivery bridge. It still does not disclose named NVIDIA / Google / ASIC / optical-module platform revenue split, per-platform ASP, shipment, gross margin or customer-by-platform EPS assumptions.
