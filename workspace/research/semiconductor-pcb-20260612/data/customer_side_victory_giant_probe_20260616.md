## Key Extracted Lines

From Victory Giant official English page:

- "Victory Giant Technology researches, develops, produces, and sells high-precision multilayer, HDI PCB, FPC, and rigid-flex PCBs."
- "Their products find widespread applications in various fields, including artificial intelligence, big data centers, industrial internet, automotive electronics (new energy), next-generation communication technology..."
- "Globally, Victory Giant Technology ranks 6th among PCB companies (according to Prismark)... establishing close cooperative relationships with many global top AI clients, and has quickly grown into a global leader in AI computing power PCBs."

From Communications Today repost / Reuters-cited article:

- "Chinese manufacturers also dominate the printed circuit board market, with companies like Victory Giant Technology counting Nvidia and Google as clients."

## Treatment

- Use as customer-side / company-side source-exhaustion and qualitative relationship evidence only.
- Do not treat the repost as a customer official source.
- Do not treat the official Victory Giant homepage as named NVIDIA / Google evidence because it does not name customers.
- Do not infer customer/platform revenue, order value, ASP, shipment, margin or EPS.

## Boundary

This probe slightly improves qualitative confidence that Victory Giant participates in global AI PCB supply chains, but it does not close the remaining requirement:

- no named platform/customer revenue split;
- no product or platform allocation;
- no customer-side official supplier list from NVIDIA / Google / Microsoft / AWS;
- no bottom-up EPS input.
