**Source:** Official Pengding 2025Q3 report, 2025 annual report and 2026Q1 report.

**Boundary:** Public shareholder-count and HKCC nominee-holder proxy. This is not beneficial-owner data, not institutional ownership and not a paid terminal positioning dataset. Average shares are approximated from HKCC disclosed shares / disclosed percentage implied total share capital.

## Interpretation

- Pengding shareholder count increased from 75,458 in 2025Q3 to 93,953 in 2026Q1.
- HKCC nominee holding declined from 8239.70万股 in 2025Q3 to 5707.02万股 in 2026Q1.
- This supports the public positioning read-through: visible fund/HKEX proxies declined while the shareholder base broadened.
