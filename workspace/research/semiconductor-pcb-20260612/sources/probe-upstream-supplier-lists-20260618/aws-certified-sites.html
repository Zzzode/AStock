Certified sites

AWS certification demonstrates that a site has successfully completed each of the five steps outlined in the AWS Standard. Certified sites tell us that achieving AWS certification brings demonstrable benefits, including improved customer relationships, increased investor confidence, a strengthened social license to operate, enhanced brand perception and more effective dialogue with regulators and policymakers.

View site directory

A global overview of AWS Standard implementation

Certified sites use their AWS Standard certification to demonstrate the efficacy of their water stewardship practices to a range of audiences, including retail customers, wholesalers, consumers, government agencies, regulators, NGO’s, local civil society organisations, local communities, investors and development financiers.

Benefits of the AWS Standard V2.0

The AWS Standard supports businesses to:

  • Understand how they depend on water and the impacts of water risks.
  • Mitigate operational and supply chain water risks.
  • Ensure responsible water procedures are in place.
  • Build relationships with local water-related stakeholders.
  • Address challenges shared with others in the catchment.

The AWS Standard can also be used by companies, investors and public sector agencies who want to mitigate water risks by evaluating and planning water stewardship practices.
