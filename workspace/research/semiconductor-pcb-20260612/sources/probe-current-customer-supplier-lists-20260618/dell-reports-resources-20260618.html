Reporting standards & frameworks

Reporting standards and frameworks are crucial to providing consistent and comparable reporting.

We welcome the standardization, simplification and harmonization of international reporting standards and we are committed to driving meaningful impact and transparently disclosing our progress. Our approach to disclosure is informed by the following standards and frameworks:

  • Global Reporting Initiative (GRI): GRI identifies the most relevant issues for sustainability reporting in a consistent and comparable manner, and Dell has long supported this approach to enhance the significance of our reporting.
  • Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) Index: We report to the industry-specific standards for technology and communications hardware and software established by the SASB Index.
  • World Economic Forum (WEF) Stakeholder Capitalism Metrics (SCM): Dell, alongside 25 other companies, was one of the initial signatories of the recent initiative in 2020 by the World Economic Forum to align and focus corporate environment, social and governance disclosures.
  • CDP Water Security and Climate Change: Our CDP Water Security and Climate Change responses allow us to measure, manage and report our environmental impact.
