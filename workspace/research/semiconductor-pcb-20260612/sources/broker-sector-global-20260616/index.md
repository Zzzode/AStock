## Quick View

- Global PCB market: Prismark 2025E growth revised to 12.8% from 7.6%; 2024 market sales about US$74bn.
- China PCB share: about 56% of global PCB production value in 2024.
- Global CCL market: 2024 estimated at US$15bn, +18% YoY; high-speed specialty CCL delivered about 50% YoY growth.
- Competitive structure: CCL top 10 suppliers hold about 77% share; PCB market remains fragmented with top 40 just over half of industry sales.
- Cost bridge: copper inflation is more pass-through friendly for CCL leaders, while high-end AI PCB/HDI margins are better insulated than commodity PCB.
