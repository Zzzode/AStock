    Victory Giant Surges on AI PCB Demand After IPO

      • By: Asia Business Outlook Team | Wednesday, 29 April 2026

Chinese printed circuit board (PCB) supplier Victory Giant Technology Huizhou Company reported strong financial results for the March quarter, driven by rising demand for AI server components.

Revenue increased 28% year on year to 5.5 billion yuan (US$805 million), while net income rose 40% to 1.3 billion yuan (US$190 million).

This marks the company’s first earnings report since its Hong Kong listing on April 21, with its stock gaining 43% since debut as investor interest in AI-related listings continues to grow.

A key driver of this growth has been Victory Giant’s role as a supplier to Nvidia. The company joined Nvidia’s H-series AI accelerator supply chain in 2023 and was upgraded to a tier-one supplier in 2024.

Key Highlights

  • Revenue up 28% and profit up 40% driven by AI server demand
  • Nvidia partnership boosts global PCB market share to 13.8%
  • IPO oversubscribed 431x with expansion focused on China

This partnership significantly boosted its market position, with Victory Giant capturing a 13.8% global share in AI and high-performance computing PCBs in the first half of 2025, up sharply from 1.7% in 2024.

The shift toward higher-value products has also improved profitability. Revenue from High Density Interconnect (HDI) boards surged 388.2% year on year in 2025, while gross margins increased to 35.2% from 22.7% in 2024.

The company’s Hong Kong IPO saw strong demand, with the retail portion oversubscribed about 431 times. Victory Giant plans to use the proceeds primarily to expand AI-related PCB production, with 74% allocated to capacity growth in mainland China. The offering includes an option that could increase total proceeds to US$2.6 billion.

This expansion aligns with China’s broader push to strengthen domestic technology manufacturing amid tightening U.S. export controls, positioning Victory Giant as a key player in the global AI hardware supply chain.
