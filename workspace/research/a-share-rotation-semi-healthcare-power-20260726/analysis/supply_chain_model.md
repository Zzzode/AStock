## Eligibility algorithm

1. **Exposure:** dated primary source identifies company product/process and chain role.
2. **Relationship:** named customer/platform/fab/licensee/grid-project evidence, certification/qualification or executable order is retained.
3. **Economics:** price/ASP proxy, capacity and utilization/yield where applicable, revenue exposure, margin/cash conversion are explicit.
4. **Model/valuation:** reconciled financials, earnings bridge and cut-off market data support a business-model-matched valuation.

Failure of any of steps 1–3 classifies the name `satellite_watch`; failure of step 4 independently blocks core valuation. Six test names fail steps 2–4, and five also have no retained company-specific step-1 source. See the relationship and customer-chain audit rows for the field-level audit trail.
