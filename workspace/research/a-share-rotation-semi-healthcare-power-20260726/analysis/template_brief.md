# Template Brief

- **Archetype:** JPM-style thematic equity-research report, adapted from `workspace/templates/global-broker-research/JPM-Equity-Research-Report-Hulu.pdf`; the first page is an investment-committee dashboard, not a source digest.
- **Coverage pack:** Custom cross-sector pack. Mandatory semiconductor modules: demand anchors, design/foundry/IDM, equipment, materials, packaging/test, memory, PCB/CCL/connectors, optical/networking and overseas/private references. Mandatory innovative-drug modules: discovery/platform, clinical development, commercial portfolio, domestic access, overseas BD/licensee economics, CRO/CDMO dependencies, regulatory/IP and global peers. Mandatory power-equipment modules: grid/utility demand, power transmission/distribution, electrical equipment, automation, storage interfaces, power electronics, renewable integration, capacity/utilization and tender/order conversion.
- **First-page dashboard:** ranked core valuation pool; current prices and action; base/bull/bear target/range; what is priced; article hypothesis scorecard; catalysts; invalidation; risk budget; satellite/watch names.
- **Required chapter order:** (1) IC summary; (2) framework and evidence test; (3) full-chain universe and taxonomy; (4) semiconductor economics and company evidence; (5) innovative-drug BD economics and company evidence; (6) power equipment cycle and company evidence; (7) growth / earnings bridges; (8) valuation and market-implied expectations; (9) variant view, risks and monitoring; (10) source notes and appendices.
- **Required exhibits:** hypothesis scorecard; cross-sector flow/positioning evidence; three chain maps; core-versus-satellite matrix; semiconductor cost/value migration; innovative-drug BD cash-flow waterfall; power-equipment order-to-revenue bridge; market-expectation bridge; valuation triangulation; catalysts/invalidation calendar.
- **Avoid:** treating one day of flow as a causal proof; treating sector participation as supplier revenue; translating a licensing headline into EPS without terms and recognition timing; one-size-fits-all PE; or treating technical strength as an intrinsic-value target.
