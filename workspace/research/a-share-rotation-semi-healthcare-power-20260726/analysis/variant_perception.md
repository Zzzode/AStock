# Variant Perception — Where the Article Could Be Wrong

## Semiconductor: the contested comparison is equipment versus optics/PCB, not “AI hardware” as one trade

The apparent consensus shortcut is that domestic fab expansion and localisation make semiconductor equipment the cleaner exposure, while PCB and optical links face upstream inflation or crowded expectations. The variant question is more demanding: **which node converts demand into incremental gross profit and cash after qualification, capacity, price and working capital?**

- Equipment can underperform the narrative if qualification takes longer, orders are not accepted on schedule, service/R&D intensity rises, or cash conversion trails reported revenue.
- Optics/PCB can outperform the narrative if customer qualification is tight, capacity utilisation stays high, price pass-through works, and mix/ASP expansion offsets material inflation.
- The required comparison is a matched company-level bridge, not a sector-label comparison: customer exposure, unit/order, utilisation, ASP/BOM, gross margin, receivable/inventory and cash conversion at a common valuation date.

Until those data exist, the house view has no relative overweight conclusion and all related names remain watchlist-only.

## Innovative drugs: headline BD can be economically smaller—or more valuable—than its announced amount

The visible narrative is that overseas BD validates Chinese innovation and creates a new earnings leg. The variant is that contract headline value, recognised revenue, cash received and shareholder value are different quantities.

- Downside variant: the upfront is non-recurring, milestones are probability-weighted far below headline value, timing is deferred, and clinical/commercial costs consume the apparent value.
- Upside variant: repeatable platform output, credible counterparties, cash receipts and milestone recognition recur while post-launch royalty economics prove durable.
- Decision evidence is asset-specific: contract consideration and performance obligations, accounting note, cash-flow line, trial/readout status, probability, cost to complete, launch price/volume and royalty base.

The 600276 and 688506 broker reports do not close that chain. In particular, the 688506 DCF target is dated 2025-02-28 and is not a current valuation signal.

## Power: trend leadership can coexist with weak value capture

The visible narrative is a mature power/power-equipment trend supported by grid investment and policy. The variant is that sector activity may not belong to a given supplier or may be absorbed by receivables, raw-material costs and competition.

- Downside variant: tender activity does not translate into awarded share, orders convert slowly, acceptance/collection slips, and raw-material or price pressure compresses margin.
- Upside variant: supplier qualification and tender share rise, high-quality orders convert into accepted revenue, working capital remains controlled and overseas/grid-adjacent mix improves margin.
- Decision evidence is tender-to-cash: qualification → award → contract → delivery → acceptance → revenue → receivable → operating cash, with margin and price evidence at each relevant step.

The 002028 and 600406 reports provide forecast context only. They do not demonstrate this full chain, so the power trend is a monitoring regime, not a rated investment case.

## Monitoring triggers

1. Semiconductor: issuer disclosure of named qualification/acceptance, backlog or order conversion, plus matched PCB/optical utilisation and pass-through evidence.
2. Innovative drugs: asset-level contract/accounting and cash disclosure, clinical/regulatory milestone, and a fresh broker or current-price valuation packet.
3. Power: tender-award evidence followed by delivery, receivable and operating-cash confirmation; verify whether margin survives cost and competition.

No trigger is a buy/sell instruction. A trigger only determines whether the case can leave its present `watchlist only / insufficient evidence` state.
