# AStock House View — Evidence-Gated Watchlist

## Decision

The article's three-track allocation story is not accepted as an investment conclusion. The current house view is a **watchlist framework with no ratings, no target prices and no core valuation pool**. All six provisional companies remain `watchlist only / insufficient evidence`; broker forecasts are context, never a substitute for a company earnings and valuation package.

## What would change the view

| Article branch | Present house test | Evidence required for an upgrade | Disconfirming outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Semiconductor equipment versus optics/PCB | Equipment may benefit from localisation and fab-capex, but the relative profit pool is unproven. | For equipment: product/process qualification, named fab/customer, backlog, shipment/acceptance, price/margin and cash. ## Interpretation discipline

The sector reports contain positive broker ratings, but those are broker opinions recorded as evidence, not house recommendations. No target is inferred from P/E, no stale target is rolled forward, and no theme statement substitutes for a frozen market-data snapshot. The upgrade sequence is: source governance → company relationship/earnings bridge → current-price valuation → independent challenge. Until then, the correct conclusion is observation, not allocation.
