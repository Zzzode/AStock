# Broker Evidence Analysis — 2026-07-26

## Bottom line

The case now has six dated original broker reports, but it does **not** have an auditable all-Street consensus or a valuation-ready broker anchor. One report per company is a selected forecast reading, not a consensus population. Five of six reports do not state an explicit target price; their current-price P/E tables are not a substitute for one. The only explicit target, for 688506, is dated 2025-02-28 and is stale at the case cutoff. Every row is therefore `valuation_weight = 0.00`.

The reports are useful only for falsifiable operating questions. They neither establish the article's sector preferences nor offset the documented company-level gaps in `analysis/coverage_gap_matrix.md`.

The evidence does **not** prove that semiconductor equipment is economically superior to optics or PCB. It supports a narrower watch question: whether reported equipment growth survives in disclosed order conversion, acceptance, margins and cash while matched PCB/optical issuer data tests cost pass-through, utilisation and customer exposure. Until both branches are matched, “equipment over optics/PCB” remains a hypothesis (H4/H5), not a portfolio conclusion.
