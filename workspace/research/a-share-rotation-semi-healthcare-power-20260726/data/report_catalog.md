## Quality boundary

Only `original_pdf` records may be read as complete broker documents. `third_party_preview`, `abstract`, and `media_repost` are retained to demonstrate public discovery and coverage gaps, not to prove analyst estimates or ratings. There are no `broker_official_page` or `auditable_consensus_snapshot` records in this phase.

**Do not create a broker-consensus artifact from this catalog.** First select core tickers, then collect at least one dated, auditable company-level source per ticker; keep missing fields as `not disclosed` and do not substitute a sector theme or an unverified media reference.
